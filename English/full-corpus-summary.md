For millennia, the Cabal (Jesuits, Rothschilds, Rockefellers) managed the surface, suppressing the Goddess and controlling finance. By 2023, the Chimera were defeated, and the focus shifted to the "Lurker," a primary anomaly from a previous cosmic cycle. In 2025, the Lurker was nearly erased, and the 12:21 Ascension Portal opened. By 2026, the final non-physical reptilian networks are being removed, and the "Event" is imminent, leading to a new era of transparency, financial reset, and integration with the Galactic community.

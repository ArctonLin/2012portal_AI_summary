## 6. Contradictions & revisions log
- **STRANGE EGG Status**: Initially reported as "in progress" in the December 2014 State of Mission Report (published Jan 1), but subsequent posts in January 2015 detailed the clearing of infections from L4 down to L0, culminating in "primary clear" on Jan 25. The February 2015 report confirms it as "complete."
- **TUNNELS Status**: Reported as "in final completion" on Feb 24, 2015, and confirmed as "complete" on March 2, 2015.
- **PPN Status**: Reported as "removed" on Feb 24, 2015, but subsequent posts in May 2015 report "PPN security breach at 504" and "PPN red alert at 504," indicating a recurrence or new breach after removal.
- **HVBN Status**: Reported as "unstable to stable" in early January, "stable" in February, "substable to stable" in February, "stable" in March/April, and "substable" in May, with a "HFshield failure" noted in June. This indicates ongoing instability despite earlier claims of stability.
- **Event Timing**: Early predictions pointed to a "Breakthrough" in late 2015 (Oct-Dec). By late September, Cobra noted that "the world did not end in September" and that things would appear "business as usual until the final breakthrough," suggesting a delay or shift in the immediate timeline. (2015-09-28)
- **Meditation Timing**: The "Meditation for Europe" was scheduled for 7:00 pm CEST in September, but shifted to 7:00 pm CET in October. The "Weekly Event Meditation" was later standardized to 7 pm UT/GMT.
- **Biochip Removal**: While biochips were disclosed as being distributed in the late 1940s and upgraded in the 90s, the Resistance is declared to have *completely* removed them from the surface population using distance removal technologies, a status reported as complete in December 2015. (2015-12-20)
- **Sublunar Operations**: Initially described as beginning in July 2015, updates in August clarified that operations had extended into the ionosphere/stratosphere and that most satellite-based threats were already disabled, indicating a rapid progression from "start" to "major completion" within weeks.

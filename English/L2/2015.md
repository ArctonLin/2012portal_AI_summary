## 6. Contradictions & revisions log
- **NWO Completion Date**: Early version stated the NWO plan was to complete by the year 2000 (failed). Later disclosures clarified that the original plan for Earth ascension was 1999/2000, but the Archon invasion in 1996 disrupted this, leading to the current timeline.
- **Solar System Control Duration**: Disclosed as 25,000 years in some posts (e.g., scalar wave network duration) and 26,000 years in others (e.g., Chimera control duration). Both figures are used interchangeably to describe the era of darkness.
- **Giants' Departure**: Disclosed as "12,000 years ago" for most giants, with a small number remaining until "5,000 years ago."
- **Event Timing**: While specific dates like January 1, 2016, are given for the financial reset, Cobra repeatedly emphasizes that "Nobody knows the day" for the Event itself, describing it as a dynamic co-creation. Early in 2015, Cobra predicted the Event would likely occur between October and December 2015. By late 2015, the focus shifted to the November 21 meditation as a "trigger" to activate the Plan, with statements that the Event would not happen before specific conditions were met, implying a delay or conditional timing rather than a fixed date.
- **Chimera Composition**: Described as "Andromedans or Orion hybrids" in one interview, while other posts refer to them as the "Orion/Andromedan Chimera empire." These are consistent but vary in specificity.
- **Martial Law**: Despite Cabal engineering false flags (e.g., Jade Helm), Cobra explicitly declared that martial law will NOT be introduced, correcting fears propagated by the Cabal.
- **Corey Goode**: In the June 7 interview, Cobra stated that off-world meetings described by Goode were "unconfirmed by sources." In the June 24 interview, Cobra confirmed Goode's involvement in the secret space program and that his intel contains "real gems," but maintained that some claims (like out-of-planet conferences) were unconfirmed. This is a nuance rather than a direct contradiction, but reflects a shift in emphasis from skepticism to partial confirmation.
- **Financial Reset Timing**: In the June 24 interview, Cobra stated the financial reset would happen at the Event. In the November 20 interview, an "Ambassador" (Rob's contact) claimed the financial reset was 30% complete and that the "old group" no longer holds the mandate, suggesting a process already underway, whereas Cobra's earlier statements framed it as a sudden event at the Event.
- **Meditation Naming/Time**: Initially referred to as "Weekly Liberation Meditation," it was officially changed to "Weekly Event Meditation" effective late 2015. The time was specified as 7 pm UT in one post and 7 pm GMT in another. While UT and GMT are often used interchangeably in this context, the specific label change is a revision of the protocol.
- **Biochip Removal Status**: The 2015-12-20 post states that biochips were "completely removed" from the surface population by the Resistance. This is a definitive declaration of completion, whereas previous contexts often implied ongoing removal or suppression. No direct contradiction with earlier 2015 posts was found in the provided cards, but this marks a final status update for this specific technology.
- **Event Meditation Success**: The success of the 2015-11-21 Event Meditation was declared in the 2015-11-23 post and reaffirmed in the 2015-11-30 report, with the latter adding that it was "close to reaching critical mass." This is an escalation of the status rather than a contradiction.

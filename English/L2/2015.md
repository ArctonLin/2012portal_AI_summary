## 6. Contradictions & revisions log
- **Ascension Timing**: Early version stated Earth ascension was originally planned for 1999/2000 (disclosed 2015-01-13). Later clarified that no one has ascended on the surface since 1996, and the Event triggers mass awakening but is not yet ascension (disclosed 2015-02-22).
- **Solar System Control**: One post states the Solar System was under Chimera control for 26,000 years (2015-02-09), while another states 25,000 years (2015-01-01, 2015-05-07).
- **Draconian Takeover**: One post states Draconian ships took over the solar system in 1995 (2015-05-07), while others consistently state the Archon invasion began in 1996 (2015-01-01, 2015-02-09).
- **Event Nature**: Described variously as a "compression breakthrough" (2015-01-01), a "financial reset" (2015-03-25), and a "cosmic occurrence" (2015-05-20), with the final synthesis being a collective decision involving both financial and spiritual liberation.
- **Timing of the Event**: Early posts (May/June) suggested "Breakthrough is near" and "Earth comes next" immediately after Solar System breakthrough. Later posts (Aug/Nov) clarified that the Event is contingent on the removal of all strangelet/toplet bombs and the stabilization of the magnetic pole shift, pushing the timeline to "when the time is right" (likely Oct-Dec 2015 or later).
- **Magnetic Pole Shift**: Initially implied as a future cataclysmic event. By August 31, Cobra declared it is "already happening" and will "not trigger massive cataclysm."
- **Corey Goode's Status**: Described as a "super soldier" in one context, but Rob Potter apologized for this term in a later interview, and Cobra noted that while Goode's intel has "real gems," some claims (like out-of-planet conferences) are unconfirmed.
- **AI Existence**: Early references to "financial AI" designed by JP Morgan (July 5) contrast with the October 18 declaration that "Artificial Intelligence does not exist" and is just a computer program operated by a living conscious entity.
- **Refugee Crisis**: Initially framed as a humanitarian crisis with some infiltration. By October 30, the declaration shifted to a specific Cabal agenda to "annex parts of Europe to the Islamic State caliphate until 2020," with precise percentages of mercenaries (4%) and paid migrants (20%).
- **Planet X**: Described as a methane gas ice-covered planet with an 850-year elliptical orbit (Nov 12), whereas earlier references to "Planet X" were more generic. The specific orbital details were new in 2015.
- **Meditation Name/Time**: Initially referred to as "Weekly Liberation Meditation," it was officially changed to "Weekly Event Meditation" at 7 pm UT/GMT (disclosed 2015-11-30, confirmed 2015-12-03).
- **EXMOSS EELA Status**: The status evolved rapidly within the month: "Recalibration standby" (2015-11-24) → "Recalibration active" (2015-11-29, 2015-12-03) → "P sequence complete" (2015-12-21). This is a progression rather than a contradiction, but reflects changing operational status.
- **Biochip Removal**: The 2015-12-20 post states that biochips were "completely removed from the surface population by the Resistance," which is a definitive declaration following the historical timeline of their distribution and upgrades. No prior contradiction found in this year's cards, but it serves as a final status update on that specific technology.

## 6. Contradictions & revisions log
- **Event Readiness**: On 2014-03-10, Cobra declared the "physical plane is ready for the Event." By 2014-04-29, this was revised to state the physical plane is "not yet ready" due to Chimera's strength and etheric grid remnants.
- **QEG Status**: On 2014-03-27, QEG was confirmed as a "working overunity device." On 2014-04-29, it was described as a "prototype in development, not yet fully working/reproducible," requiring support.
- **IS:IS Activation**: Initially presented as a major portal activation (2014-04-14). The report on 2014-04-23 described it as a "partial success" that laid a foundation for a second part, while the State of Mission Report on 2014-05-01 noted "partial IS:IS failure." Later, the December activation was described as a "partial success in a very unique way" (2014-12-16), with the detailed report (2014-12-23) clarifying that while surface critical mass was not reached, 144,000 people from underground realms successfully stabilized the grid.
- **Midas Progress**: M=3 reported in Jan/Feb 2014; M=24 reported in March 2014; M=12 in April; M=7 in May; M=4 in June. The fluctuating "M" values indicate ongoing, non-linear progress or different metrics for the "Midas package."
- **Planetary Meditation Timing**: Initial suggestion was Saturday or Sunday around 8 pm GMT (2014-08-17), but final timing was determined at 3 pm GMT each Sunday (2014-08-17, 2014-08-25).
- **Chimera Strongholds**: Initially stated as two strongholds (Long Island and classified central European location) on 2014-09-08. By 2014-09-16, Chimera was removed from the European location, leaving Long Island as the main stronghold. By 2014-09-24, Chimera presence was also removed from Berchtesgaden/Untersberg area.
- **Ebola Situation**: Initially described as a scare engineered by the Cabal to keep masses in fear (2014-08-05). Later clarified that the virus was engineered in African laboratories, but the Light forces intervened to stop its spread, and the "pandemic" was receding (2014-11-02).

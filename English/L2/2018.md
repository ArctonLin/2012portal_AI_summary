## 6. Contradictions & revisions log
- **Mjolnir Phase 2 Completion**: Early version stated completion in late February (disclosed 2018-01-07); revised to early March 2018 (disclosed 2018-01-07).
- **Isis Astara's Death**: Initially reported as "critical condition" in hospital on 2018-01-27; confirmed as "left the physical plane" on 2018-01-29.
- **HVBN Status**: Described as "mainly stable" in 2017 report; "substable to stable" in Dec 2017 report; "fatal termination" in Jan 2018 report; "secured" in April 2018 updates; "stabilizing" in June; "unstable to stable" in July; "irreversible failure" in August; "converted into GHVBN and unstable" in September.
- **Security Breaches**: Multiple "drastic" or "fatal" breaches at location 504 reported throughout the year (Jan 30, Apr 9, Apr 14, Apr 17, Apr 27, May 19, May 20, June 4, June 5, Aug 17, Sep 8, Sep 15), with varying descriptions of deflection or recovery.
- **Cobra Identity**: In April 2018, Cobra explicitly denied being replaced by a clone, clarifying that "social dynamics" articles are aligned with the plan, addressing potential confusion or accusations of replacement.
- **Timeline Status**: Early posts (May 2018) described the "Beta timeline" as secured or metastable. By June/July 2018, Cobra declared the Beta timeline had collapsed and the Gamma timeline was active.
- **Critical Mass**: The Key to Freedom Meditation aimed for a critical mass of 144,000. The final report stated that although the critical mass was not reached, 70% was achieved and "important breakthroughs" were made.
- **Entry Protocols**: Initial disclosure (May 9) suggested entry protocols were being prepared. The update (May 14) clarified that entries "may or may not happen" and are not a mass recruitment campaign, limiting it to 20–200 specific individuals.
- **LSIP Status**: On Oct 29, LSIP was stated as "not yet supported due to unfavorable grid ratio." On Nov 12, "HP.LSIP reactivated, HP clearance approved." On Dec 4, "LSIP failure" was reported.
- **MASTERPIECE Versioning**: v2.0 was operational on Oct 29. v2.1 was operational on Oct 29. v2.2 was operational on Dec 18. v2.3 was operational on Dec 26.
- **Delta Option Scope**: Initially described as a military action by Alliance/Agarthan factions (Nov 6). Later clarified that it excludes Chimera, Archons, Black Nobility, and Jesuits, who will keep a low profile (Nov 6).
- **Bush Sr. Status**: Disclosed as "put to death" hours before the conference, but immediately clarified that he was "captured by the Light" and is undergoing disintegration in the Galactic Central Sun, not just dead (Dec 7).

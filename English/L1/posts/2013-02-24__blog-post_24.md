[Publication date] 2013-02-24
[Historical events]
- 2013-02-24 (current): Area between 0 and 30 meters below surface liberated on physical plane.
- 2013-05-25: Opening of the portal; Event unlikely to happen before this date.
[Entities & roles]
- Resistance: Liberated the 0-30m layer; cleared military bases, black projects, Cabal facilities, weapons, drugs, gold; removed environmental pollution and warfare remnants; blocked Archon technology; removed Black Box technology; 22,000 agents came to surface.

- Etheric Archons: Technology to manifest minions on physical plane blocked; all apparitions of Greys and Reptilians ended.
- Physical Archons: Black Box technology removed; must rely on occult rituals/channeling for intel.
- Pleiadian fleet: Involved in an active operation.
[Key numbers]
- 0 to 30 meters: Depth of the liberated physical layer.
- 22,000: Number of Resistance agents on the surface.
- 2013-05-25: Date of the portal opening.
[Major declarations & commitments]
- The Cabal can now only use the surface of the planet for its actions.
- All apparitions of Greys and Reptilians have ended due to blocked Archon technology.
- It is extremely unlikely that the Event will happen before the opening of the portal on May 25th.

"Basically the whole area between 0 and 30 meters below the surface has been liberated on the physical plane."

[Publication date] 2013-04-15
[Historical events] 
26,000 years ago: Planet invaded; Ascended Masters retreated from the surface (Ascended Masters, Dark forces).
9564 BCE: Final deluge of Atlantis destroyed all Atlantean mystery schools (Atlantis).
2000–2500 years ago: Revival of mystery schools in the Mediterranean due to increased activity of the Galactic Central Sun (Mediterranean, Galactic Central Sun).
4th Century: Khazar Archon invasion aimed to destroy mystery schools (Khazar Archons).
1775: Saint Germain created a mystery school in Paris (Saint Germain, Paris).
[Entities & roles] Ascended Masters: Beings of pure Light who walked freely among humanity before the invasion.
Atlantis: Location of the main mystery school before its destruction.
Egypt: Location where the Atlantean mystery school was moved (Saqqara to Giza plateau).
Illuminati: Came from a degenerated branch of mystery schools.
Khazar Archons: Invaded in the 4th century to destroy mystery schools.
Saint Germain: Revived old Egyptian mysteries and created a school in Paris in 1775.
Brotherhood of the Star: Operated Saint Germain's Paris mystery school for another century.
[Key numbers] 26,000 years ago (Invasion/Retreat); 9564 BCE (Deluge); 4th Century (Khazar invasion); 1775 (Paris school).
[Major declarations & commitments] True mystery schools will be established only after the Event and especially after the First Contact.
[Key quotes] "Before this planet was invaded 26,000 years ago, Ascended Masters walked freely among humanity and kept the connection to the presence of the One intact."

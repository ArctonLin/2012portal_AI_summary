[Major declarations & commitments] 
Ascension window of 1975-2025 will NOT close in 2025, but will remain PERMANENTLY OPEN.
Energies will keep increasing and since 2025, Ascension energies will forever permanently flow from the Galactic Central Sun through our Solar System towards the planetary surface.
Light forces will upgrade current state of emergency into martial law at peak social unrest.
Whole surface population will undergo a brief (3-7 days) military lockdown during which mass arrests will take place.
Positive forces will use quantum pulse weapons to block weaponry of Negative military and freeze troops and civilians who refuse to obey orders.
After the Event, surface society will undergo a drastic transformation that will take a few years.
Most extreme psychopaths and sociopaths will be removed from the surface at the Event and shortly after.
Many latent sociopaths will remain in society until the Galactic Superwave, keeping a low profile.
Vast majority of Lightworkers will go through a massive healing process after the Event.
About 2000 people will be ready to Ascend in the first wave, coinciding with the Galactic Superwave and planetary Evacuation.
Those preparing to Ascend in the first wave will live in Islands of Light, formed shortly after the Event.
During the Evacuation, ships of the Ashtar Command and the Galactic Confederation will rescue surface humanity from the Galactic Tsunami.
After the Evacuation and Earth's transformation, only beings fully aligned with Light (estimated about a few million Lightworkers) will be allowed to return and reinhabit the Earth in Islands of Light.
Mass of humanity will be evacuated in their physical bodies just before the Galactic Pulse and transported to the planet in the Pleiades star system.
About 500 million surface evacuees, mostly sociopaths and psychopaths and many Dracos and Reptilians, will not fulfill minimum criteria for evolution and will be taken to the Central Sun for restructuring.
This effectively means the end of darkness and suffering for the whole universe, and a "new cycle of luminosity can begin".

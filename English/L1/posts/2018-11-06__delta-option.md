[Publication date] 2018-11-06
[Historical events]
- 70 years ago: Technology for commercial space travel entered public domain.
- Last few weeks (relative to 2018-11-06): Reinforcements from Solar System bases arrived under the surface of Earth.
- (time not stated): Resistance Movement created the "Delta Option" emergency plan.
[Entities & roles]
- Dark Forces: Have almost unlimited free reign on the surface; sabotaging positive initiatives.
- Light Forces: Having problems removing toplet bombs; not doing enough to stop the Cabal.
- Cabal: Went too far; factions in USA targeted by Delta Option; controlling mass media and US dollar.
- Alliance: Certain factions losing patience; planning military action against the Cabal.
- Agarthan factions: Losing patience; planning military action.
- Resistance Movement: Created the Delta Option plan; 140 million members strong; preparing for upheaval.
- Special forces group: Part of Delta Option; will initiate removal of Cabal factions in USA; provide tactical support to Positive Military.
- Positive Military: Will carry out the operation with tactical support from special forces.
- Chimera, Archon, Black Nobility, Jesuit members: Will NOT be touched in Delta Option; will keep low profile and show friendly face.
- Rothschild: Controlling mass media; control to be freed in Delta Option.
- Jesuits: Trying to polarize the situation in USA with artificial left/right political divide.
[Key numbers]
- 70 years: Duration technology for commercial space travel has been in public domain.
- 140 million: Current strength of the Resistance Movement.
- 30 meters: Previous maximum depth from surface for Resistance members.
- 5 to 10 meters: Current depth for some special Resistance forces.
[Major declarations & commitments]
- Delta Option is an emergency plan to prevent a war that would wipe out humanity, triggered if the situation in the USA becomes less stable.
- Delta Option involves removing Cabal factions in the USA only, excluding Chimera, Archons, Black Nobility, and Jesuits.
- Mass media will be freed from Rothschild control and the US dollar will collapse under Delta Option.
- Lightworkers/Lightwarriors can telepathically request a small Resistance base under their living location; this request must not be communicated aloud or written.
- Delta Option does NOT mean Delta timeline.
[Key quotes]
- "Certain factions in the Alliance and certain Agarthan factions are losing patience and are planning to take the surface of the planet from the clutches of the Cabal with military action."
- "Instead of waiting for the optimal conditions with toplet bombs removed and non-physical Archons removed, a certain “special forces group” would initiate removal of some factions of the Cabal within USA only."
- "At this moment, the Resistance is 140 million members strong, and that number may and probably will still rise significantly."

[Publication date] 2014-01-12
[Historical events] 
Millions of years ago: Darkness spread across the Galaxy.
1995: Current Archon leader arrived on Earth through a portal near Verona.
1996: Congo Archon invasion organized by the Archon leader.
1540s: Farnese family created the Jesuits.
16th–17th Century: Farnese shaped world destiny in Caprarola.
1770s: Antarctica officially "discovered" (though shown on 16th-century maps).
May 2010: Mission Blue Shield operation reduced Orsini power.
Last month (Dec 2013): More than two-thirds of etheric infrasound technology removed.
[Entities & roles] 
Archon Leader: Living in a small provincial city near Rome; arrived in 1995; maintains quarantine.
Farnese Family: Main architects of the Cabal; created Jesuits; built Pentagon in Caprarola and Washington DC.
Orsini Family: Strong connection with Reptilian race; link between physical/non-physical Archons; lost power in May 2010.
Aldobrandini Family: Contains members of the original group that chose separation from Source; possess Atlantis documents.
Chigi Family: Bankers for Jesuits; control Banca Monte dei Paschi de Siena.
Eastern Alliance: Removing Cabal minions in China.
Light Forces: Removing Archons and etheric infrasound technology.

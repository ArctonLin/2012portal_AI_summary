[Publication date] 2018-01-16
[Historical events]
- 1950s: US military underground network constructed.
- 1996: Archon invasion; Illuminati Breakaway Complex (IBC) went underground; Draco fleet defense ring established in Medium Earth Orbit.
- 1996-1999: Dracos and Reptilians entered Solar System during Archon invasion.
- 2000: US military underground network cleared by Resistance.
- 2014: Top levels of IBC taken over by Chimera.
- November 2017: Operation Mjolnir in full effect; wave of scalar/directed weapons attacks on Lightworkers started.
- 26,000 years ago: Chimera and Agartha underground networks constructed.
[Entities & roles]
- Operation Mjolnir: Special Light Forces operation to enforce Galactic Codex; detects dark beings via quantum anomaly; containment/processing in Ganymede.
- Illuminati Breakaway Complex (IBC): Chimera-controlled negative faction; ~200,000 members; responsible for missing children since 2015; secret space program with cloaked ships.
- Chimera: Controls IBC; constructed underground network 26,000 years ago.
- Galactic Confederation: Approaching dark factions; removing weapons; involved in space war.
- Pleiadians: Taking freed captives to a planet near Pleiades for healing.
- NRO: Maintains quarantine Earth status; developed NROL-47.
[Key numbers]
- ~200,000: Members of IBC.
- ~1 million: Native Reptilians connected to IBC.
- 26,000 years ago: Construction date of Chimera/Agartha networks.
- 1996: Year of Archon invasion.
- 2015: Start of IBC responsibility for missing children.
[Major declarations & commitments]
- IBC is the main focus of Operation Mjolnir.
- Resistance freed majority of IBC captives since Jan 2018.
- Hawaii missile was a secret space program weapon removed by Galactic Confederation.
- NROL-47 patch "Mali Nunquam Praevalebunt" signifies victory of positive species over Draco.
[Key quotes]
"Operation Mjolnir is in full effect since November 2017."
"Illuminati Breakaway Complex consists of about 200,000 Illuminati and Dracos, controlled by the Chimera."
"The Hawaii missile was a secret space program weapon directed to Hawaii that was removed by the Galactic Confederation before it entered the atmosphere."

-25,000 BC: Leaders of dark forces from Andromeda galaxy arrive on Earth in humanoid bodies and quarantine the planet.

-25,000 BC: Construction of vast subterranean cities using Draconians as slave handlers and Reptilians as slaves.

1917: Light forces form the Thule society in Germany.
(time not stated): Thule society infiltrated by Archon-controlled Rothschild agent Adolf Schicklgruber (Hitler) and morphs into the Vril society.

(time not stated): After WWII, Nazis imported into the US through Operation Paperclip.

(time not stated): Chimera group bases cleared out by the Resistance.
(time not stated): Chimera group hijacks global financial system via PROMIS software and high-speed trading.
[Entities & roles]
Chimera group: Andromedan dark forces; leaders of quarantine; worship the Black Sun; guardians of the electromagnetic null zone.
Draconians: Used as slave handlers by the Chimera.
Reptilians: Used as slaves by the Chimera.
Thule society: Formed by Light forces in 1917; infiltrated by Hitler; morphed into Vril society.
Vril society: Developed secret German space program.
Karl Haushofer: Main occult force behind Nazi Germany; introduced Chimera to humanity.
Operation Paperclip: Program importing Nazis to the US.
Galactic Confederation: Conducted experiment at Castle Koon to reduce bomb yield.
Black Sun: Symbol of the Galactic Central Sun worshipped by Chimera.
Black Stone: Lump of heavy top/antitop quark condensate from Rigel; center of primary cosmic anomaly of darkness.
Resistance: Cleared out Chimera underground bases.
PROMIS software: Tool used by Chimera to hijack financial system.

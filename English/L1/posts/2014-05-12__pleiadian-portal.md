[Publication date] 2014-05-12
[Historical events] 
- 2014-05-17: Pleiadian Portal to open at 5:11 pm CEST.
[Entities & roles] 
- Pleiadian Portal: Important milestone for planetary liberation.
- Galactic Confederation: Lightships to assume final positions in Oort cloud.
- Oort cloud: Aggregation of icy bodies up to 1 light year from Sun.
- Heliopause: Energy boundary of solar system.
- Chimera group: Target of deactivation of strangelet bombs by Confederation ships.
- Pleiadian, Sirian, Andromedan fleets: Involved in the operation.
[Key numbers] 
- 1 light year: Distance of Oort cloud from Sun.
- 2014-05-17 17:11 CEST: Activation time.
[Major declarations & commitments] 
- Confederation motherships will serve as relay stations for cosmic energies of liberation.
- Ships will enter the solar system to deactivate Chimera strangelet bombs.
- Surface population is not involved directly in this operation.
- The 2014 Window of Opportunity closes on May 17th, but probability of the Event remains high.
[Key quotes] 
- "On that moment, the lightships of the Galactic Confederation, some of them hundreds and hundreds of miles in diameter, will assume their final positions inside the Oort cloud, which is an aggregation of billions of small icy bodies around our solar system up to 1 light year away from the Sun."
- "The Light forces have decided not to involve the surface population in this operation directly, as the surface population is not reliable enough to be involved in such a delicate process."

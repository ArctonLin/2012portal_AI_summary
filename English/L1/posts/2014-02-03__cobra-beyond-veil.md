[Publication date] 2014-02-03
[Historical events]
- (time not stated): Cobra attempts stratospheric flight via South Africa; Cabal interferes, company loses license two months before flight.
- (time not stated): Cobra successfully flies MiG-29 from a Russian military base, crossing the Veil boundary at 8.6 miles altitude.
- (time not stated): Cobra reaches 60,000 feet (nearspace) and takes a photo of Earth's curvature.
- (time not stated): Cobra creates a "big crack in the Matrix" security firewall via the flight.
[Entities & roles]
- Cobra: Conducted the nearspace flight to break the quarantine/Matrix.
- The Cabal: Interfered with the South African flight attempt.
- Archons: Created the security firewall/quarantine status of Earth.
- Light motherships: Present high above Cobra during the flight.
[Key numbers]
- 8.6 miles: Altitude of the Veil boundary.
- 60,000 feet: Altitude reached in nearspace.
- 100 people: Personnel present at the Russian base for the flight.
- 10: Number of top Russian military aircraft pilots (pilot was among them).
- Few hundred dollars: Cost to launch a stratospheric balloon.
[Major declarations & commitments]
- The flight created a significant crack in the Archon Matrix firewall.
- Readers are instructed to launch stratospheric balloons to help create cracks in the Matrix.
- Cobra plans to next attempt Virgin Galactic or a Pleiadian beamship.
[Key quotes]
- "When you cross the Veil boundary, 8.6 miles above the surface, you are suddenly embraced by a tachyon bath..."
- "Through this flight, I was able to create a big crack in the Matrix, a big dent in the security firewall that the Archons have created to maintain the quarantine status of planet Earth."

[Publication date] 2018-03-22
[Historical events]
- ~18 million years ago: Pleiades star cluster emerged from plasma into the physical plane.
- Many hundreds of thousands of years ago: Pleiadians decided not to partake in Galactic wars.
- 1945: Pleiadians rescued Vril girls from Earth in project "Niemand bleibt hier."
- 1954: Pleiadians contacted President Eisenhower to prevent nuclear war.
- 1977: Pleiadians contacted the author to prevent nuclear war.
- 1996: Archon invasion occurred; Pleiadians failed to prevent it.
- 1996–1999: Archons briefly invaded the Pleiadian star cluster.
- 1999: Pleiades star cluster liberated; majority of Pleiadian hostages liberated from Earth bases.
- Late 2001: Pleiadians contacted the author with instructions to construct Tachyon healing chambers.
- April 14-15, 2018: First Ascension conference in Budapest, Hungary.
- May 12-13, 2018: Second conference in Taipei, Taiwan.
[Entities & roles]
- Pleiadians: Beings of Love and Light; originated from angelic evolution; contacted Vril girls, Eisenhower, and the author; rescued Vril girls in 1945.
- Vril girls (die Vrilerinnen): Contacted by Pleiadians to start German secret space program; rescued in 1945.
- President Eisenhower: Contacted by Pleiadians in 1954.
- Archons: Invaded Earth in 1996 and Pleiades cluster 1996-1999; prevented nuclear war prevention efforts partially.
- Cabal: Mentioned as owning property and interfering with Contact Dish.

[Publication date] 2012-04-17
[Historical events] 
Mid-90s: Disinfo campaign against Galactic Confederation started by Cabal.
Shortly after 2000: Resistance Movement cleared all subterranean Reptilian bases.
Year 325: Council of Nicea; Constantine created Christian Cult to destroy gnostic mysteries.
Early 1800s: Rothschild faction took over from Jesuit faction.
February (2012): Secret negotiations between Resistance and White Dragon Society caused delays in Event timeline.
[Entities & roles] 
Galactic Confederation/Federation: Loose union of positive civilizations (Pleiadians, Sirians, Arcturians); leaders are ascended masters; Ashtar Command is a section for Earth liberation; has cloaked motherships; prevented nuclear war and earthquakes.
Resistance Movement: Freedom fighters in subterranean dwellings; contact with underground Pleiadian bases in Himalaya and Bora-Bora; public contact is Cobra; cleared Reptilian forces and black budget programs; placed agents in military/alphabet agencies.
Positive Military: Most powerful positive surface group; developed Plan for planetary liberation; ~3/4 of military agrees; public representative is Drake; loose group without a leader.
Templars (Faction 2/White Hats): Old European nobility; power base in US Navy, UK, Europe; public contact is Lord Blackheath; involved in new 134 nations alliance financial system; aim to bring down Federal Reserve and Rothschilds.
White Dragon Society: Represents old Chinese royal lineages; contact is Benjamin Fulford; motivated to bring down Cabal but motives not completely pure; had problems with Resistance in Feb 2012.
Gnostic Illuminati: Old Russian nobility; broke off from Cabal after Rothschilds destroyed Romanovs; spokesperson is Alexander Romanoff; heavily infiltrated by Rothschild agents.
Rothschild faction: Controlled Europe, high finance, media; leader Jacob de Rothschild; mostly Rigelian dark lords; responsible for Atlantis deluge; motivation is to keep money/media and avoid arrest.
Rockefeller faction: Controlled US, oil, pharma; leader David Rockefeller; mostly Draconian lords; motivation is to escape to Bahamas and avoid arrest.
Jesuit faction: Powerful until early 1800s; ~10% of Jesuits belong; includes cardinals, black pope (Adolfo Nicolas), white pope (Joseph Ratzinger); created in 325; motivation is to stay alive.

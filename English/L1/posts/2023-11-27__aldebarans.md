[Publication date] 2023-11-27
[Historical events] 
- August 2019: Collapse of beta timeline; planetary Light gird collapsed to a great degree; many portals to the surface became inoperable.
- 3114 BCE: Deluge following a nuclear war between Aldebarans and Draconians; Aldebaran influence on the surface diminished.
- Around 5000 years ago: Aldebarans forced to retreat from the surface; rising Aldebaran marked the vernal equinox; impactor body sent to Tibet.
- 1996: All underground Aldebarans taken prisoner by dark forces or forced to leave the Solar system.
- 2023 (November): Conference in Chiang Mai; second day brought breakthrough in anchoring Goddess energy.
[Entities & roles] 
- Aldebarans: Sister race to Pleiadians; chosen as main race for First Contact; assisted humanity post-Atlantis; founded Vedic civilization, Rama empire, Sumerian empire; fought Draconians in nuclear war; retreated to Agartha until 1996; currently building military bases on ocean floors and Moon.
- Pleiadians: No longer the main race for First Contact; focusing on specific assistance to Lightworkers; developing quantum entangled Tunnels of Light technology; reviving planetary Light gird.
- Draconians: Opponents of Aldebarans in ancient nuclear war.
- Galactic Confederation: Aldebarans are now dedicated members.
- Maria Orsic: Started secret space program in 20th century; Vril girls mentioned.
- Emperor Wu of Han: Contacted by Aldebarans; peak of Han dynasty.
- Li Yuan (Emperor Gaozu of Tang): Contacted by Aldebarans; founder of Tang dynasty.
- Aldobrandini Black nobility family: Aldebarans infiltrated/incarnated here to preserve Goddess mysteries.
[Key numbers] 
- 80%: Percentage of galactic race representatives who left the Solar system for healing.
- 3114 BCE: Date of the deluge.
- 5000 years ago: Time of Aldebaran retreat and impactor event.
- 1996: Year of Archon invasion/prisoner taking.
[Major declarations & commitments] 
- Pleiadians will no longer be the main race for First Contact; Aldebarans will replace them.
- Billions of evacuated humans will be selected; those aligned with angelic presence stay on Pleiadian planet, others move to Aldebaran system planet.
- Aldebarans are executing a plan to complete in 2024: placing cloaked military bases on ocean floors and remote surface areas.
- These bases will serve as platforms for Event operations and as the backbone of the new Quantum financial system.
- Aldebarans will disclose themselves by contacting key world politicians.

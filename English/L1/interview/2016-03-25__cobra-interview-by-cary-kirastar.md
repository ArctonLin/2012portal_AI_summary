[Entities & roles]
Cobra: Spokesperson for the Resistance Movement; code name for this specific time period.
Resistance Movement: Forces of Light hidden below the surface; a faction of the Agartha network; assisting in planetary liberation.
Agartha: Inner Earth civilization; parent network of the Resistance Movement.
Primary Anomaly: Original source of all darkness and suffering; Earth is the focal point for its resolution.
Cabal: Currently controlling the financial system and media; unable to sustain themselves without Chimera protection; attempting to delay The Event through chaos and war.
Chimera: Provides protection to the Cabal via exotic weapons; their removal is a prerequisite for Cabal arrest.
Pleiadians: Working with the Resistance Movement and Galactic Confederation on clearing the Chimera; guiding the development of advanced healing technologies; described as a harmonic, evolved race.
Andromedans: Working with the Resistance Movement.
Galactic Confederation / Central Race / Sphere Beings: Working with the Resistance Movement.
Eastern Alliance: Preparing the new transitional financial system; contains trained individuals ready for leadership roles.
Archons: Non-physical entities pressing buttons to psychologically manipulate Light Workers; interfering with Soul Group recognition via amnesia fields.
Dragon Groups: Developed advanced laser systems for healing.
Soul Groups: Souls born from the Source at the same time through the Galactic Central Sun; currently incarnating to find each other for the Planetary Liberation mission.

[Major declarations & commitments]
The quarantine is in the process of being lifted but has not yet been fully lifted.
The Cabal cannot be arrested until the transformation of exotic weapons is complete, as the Chimera currently protects them.
The Eastern Alliance is already preparing a new transitional financial system.
The "Breakthrough" will occur when a critical mass of new intel is released through mass media, breaking the Cabal's control.
The Event is a cosmic occurrence marking the completion of the Galactic Liberation process, with Earth as the last planet to be liberated.
The Cabal is actively trying to delay The Event by creating chaos, starting wars, and withdrawing funds.
Individuals should clear their belief systems about money to manifest abundance outside the matrix.
The top priority for individuals is to follow their mission and use their talents to assist in the process.

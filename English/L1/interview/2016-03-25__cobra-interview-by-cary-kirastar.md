[Entities & roles]
Cobra: Spokesperson for the Resistance Movement on the surface of the planet; code name for this specific time period.
Resistance Movement: Forces of Light hidden below the surface of the planet, part of the Agartha network, assisting in the Liberation process.
Agartha: Inner Earth civilization; the Resistance Movement is one faction of this network.
Primary Anomaly: The original source of all darkness and suffering; Earth is the focal point for its resolution.
Plasma Energy: Described as one of the biggest secrets and suppressed aspects of creation; key for both suppression of knowledge and Liberation.
Cabal: Currently controlling the financial system; unable to sustain themselves without the protection of the Chimera; attempting to delay The Event by creating chaos and withdrawing funds.
Chimera: Provides protection to the Cabal through exotic weapons; their removal is a prerequisite for Cabal arrests and financial system transformation.
Pleiadians: Working with the Resistance Movement on clearing the Chimera and exotic weapons; described as a harmonic race that has created a paradise-type civilization; guiding the development of advanced healing technologies.
Andromedans: Working with the Resistance Movement.
Galactic Confederation: Working with the Resistance Movement; also referred to as the "Central Race" or "Sphere Beings."
Eastern Alliance: Preparing the new transitional financial system; contains capable individuals trained for leadership roles in the new system.
Archons: Non-physical entities pressing buttons to psychologically manipulate Light Workers and Light Warriors; interfering with Soul Group recognition.
Soul Groups: Souls born from the Source at the same time through the Galactic Central Sun; currently incarnating to find each other and collectively perform their mission for Planetary Liberation.
Dragon Groups: Developed advanced laser systems for healing.
Tachyon Technology: Used in healing chambers to improve the vibrational frequency of human beings.
Liberation Now!: The name of the weekly Sunday meditation event.
Victory of the Light: The salutation of people following Cobra's work, originating from the Light Forces.

[Entities & roles]
*   **Valiant Thor**: Representative of the Galactic Confederation; came via Venus; now on motherships clearing the solar system.
*   **Chimera**: Technologically oriented; works with Archons; present on etheric plane.
*   **Archons**: Oriented towards controlling higher planes; incarnated in human bodies; some choose reptilian shapes.
*   **Pleiadians/Sirians**: Not ascended as a race; still have physical bodies; Sirians are not ascended.
*   **Dolphins/Whales**: Stabilize planetary energy grid; receive light from Sirius directly.
*   **Faeries**: Exist all over the universe; struggled after 1996 due to etheric pollution.
*   **Unicorns**: Rare; exist mostly on higher planes.
*   **King of the World**: Ancient wise being of Light; ascended; consciousness focused on the planet.
*   **Dou Mu**: Not ascended; coming from the liberated universe; protected by Dragon families; uses laser technology for pineal gland.
*   **Cathar Lodges**: Outer expression of the inner Brotherhood of the Star; held light against Jesuit invasions.
*   **Easter Island Statues**: Remnants of an old giant race.

[Key numbers]
*   1996: Year of Archon invasion; visitations reduced after this.
*   16,000 years ago: Time the Sphinx was built.

*   The Archon "black box" is inactive, but the "black stone" keeps the anomaly in place.

*   Dou Mu is not ascended but is purified and connected to Source; protected by Dragon families.

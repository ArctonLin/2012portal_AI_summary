- Cabal: Pushing people over the threshold of poverty; desperate.
- Jesuits: Negotiating their way out; trying to be super nice.
- Resistance Movement: Not willing to risk the Event if Archons are still active.
- Archons: Small group remaining; strong etheric technology.
[Key numbers]
- 100,000: Estimated casualties if the Event happened right now (from previous context).
- 1-10 million: Potential casualties if something goes wrong.
- 90-50%: Percentage of human energy going to the Cabal.
- 2-3 billion: Daily amount needed by the Cabal to keep the show running.

- The "Event" will not happen until the non-physical Archons are cleared.
- The Cabal will not be allowed to use nuclear weapons.
- The "Event" will be a physical and non-physical objective event.
- The new financial system will be supervised by higher positive beings.

- Archons: Non-physical; invading from other planets; maintaining quarantine.
- White Dragon Society: Had problems with Resistance in early 2012.
[Key numbers]
- 20 million: Current number of Resistance members underground.
- 70 million: Number of Resistance members 5-10 years ago.
- 10-20 million: Number of Cabal representatives in underground bases during 1996-1999.
- 5000 years: Time since Archon invasion (mentioned in context of timeline healing).
- 1 billion+: Daily cost for Cabal to run operations.

- The Event will be a global operation; the US is the key trigger point.
- The "Green Light" for mass arrests has not been confirmed yet.

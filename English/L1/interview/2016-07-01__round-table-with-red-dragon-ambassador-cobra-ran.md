[Publication date] 2016-07-01
[Historical events]
- 1960s (mid): Covert military space program decided to invest in psionic development (US Marine Corp Special Section).
- 2016 (recent): "Operation Dreamland" is active, assisting light workers in anchoring the new society (Light Forces, Earth).
- Post-Event: Workday will be reduced to 4 hours or less due to advanced technology (Earth, Technology).
[Entities & roles]
- Cobra: Describes "Operation Dreamland" as an operation to anchor the new society; states that the "service-to-self" vs "service-to-others" division is artificial; confirms that the Cabal is releasing partial intel to spin the narrative.
- Randy Cramer: Claims to be from the US Marine Corp Special Section; describes a "gradient" approach to disclosure to prevent panic; states that Bernie Sanders is mathematically poised to win the presidency (disputed by others).
- Red Dragon Ambassador: Argues that disclosure of Jesus's survival (Vatican holding his body) will be more traumatizing to belief systems than alien disclosure; emphasizes the need for unity and practical solutions.
- Rob Potter: Disagrees with the Ambassador on Jesus's death; emphasizes the spiritual consciousness aspect.
- Aldaberons/Plasmid Freelancers: Added by Randy to the list of negative entities; "plasmid freelancers" are neon-like plasma beings acting as hitmen.
[Key numbers]
- 85%: Percentage of people who will not kill themselves during disclosure (Randy's estimate).
- 15%: Percentage who may go into chaos/psychotic episodes (Randy's estimate).
- 18 days: Timeframe for demographic response waves in disclosure strategy (Randy).
- 4 hours: Expected workday length post-Event.
[Major declarations & commitments]
- The Cabal is releasing partial intel to appear as innocent onlookers.
- The "gradient" approach to disclosure is being used to manage public reaction and prevent suicides/panic.
- Psionic development is critical but must be paired with stable infrastructure.
- The "Galactic Codex" is a law that requires aggressive groups to be neutralized.
[Key quotes]
- "Operation Dreamland is operation of the light forces that would like to anchor on the physical plane the new society, the new reality."
- "We have set up a number of gradients of targeted demographics... so that each group of people is receiving the psychological information that they are willing to digest this minute."
- "The division between service-to-others and service-to-self is artificial. It’s a programming attempt to divide beings."

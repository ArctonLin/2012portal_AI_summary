[Publication date] 2017-07-01
[Historical events]
*   Pre-incarnation: Humans signed contracts with Light Forces regarding mission, protection, and life course.
*   Past centuries (pre-WWII): Mind control was less intense; most ascensions occurred before this period.
*   1994–1998: Certain dark force factions surrendered to the Light; a specific Ascension group in the United States existed and subsequently ascended.
*   Last 25,000 years: Light Forces operated with extreme caution due to hostage situations on Earth.
*   Present day: The "Man of the Sixth Race" evolution is undergoing a quantum leap; protection grid on the surface has collapsed due to humans not following pre-incarnation agreements.
*   Future (The Event): The biggest quantum leap for the Sixth Race evolution will occur; humanity will enter Galactic Society.

[Entities & roles]
*   **Cobra**: States that one person with full I AM connection can liberate the planet; confirms Source knows all Dark schemes but is limited by intel flow control; denies knowledge of Sphere Being Alliance; identifies "Galactic Central Race" as the primary energy director.
*   **I AM Presence**: Source of protection and guidance; connection improves with honesty and pillar of light visualization.
*   **St. Germain**: Noted as lacking awareness of current planetary facts compared to what is now required for efficient liberation.
*   **Dark Forces/Archons**: Have no intention of cooperating; resolution requires disintegration into the Galactic Central Sun; maintain hostage situation preventing military intervention.
*   **Light Forces**: Currently integrating their own shadows (lack of cosmic understanding); unable to fully protect humans due to incomplete control of the situation; one faction is impatient for direct intervention but held back by bomb risks.
*   **Source**: Aware of all schemes; communication with manifested universe is imperfect due to the "primary anomaly."
*   **Sanat Kumara**: One of the 13 Kumaras; part of a soul group that includes Lady Meta (who is not his daughter).
*   **13 Kumaras**: Advanced beings assisting in transmuting Galactic Central Sun energies; they are their own higher selves.
*   **Wingmakers**: Experienced setbacks as frustration over inability to transmit the divine plan efficiently; original plan involved more direct contact and faster liberation.
*   **Man of the Sixth Race**: The next stage of human evolution, currently undergoing a quantum leap.

[Key numbers]
*   1994–1998: Time period of specific dark faction surrenders and US Ascension group activity.
*   25,000 years: Duration of cautious Light Force operations due to hostage situations.
*   13: Total number of Kumaras mentioned.
*   6: Number of remaining Kumaras besides Sanat Kumara (implied by "other six").

[Major declarations & commitments]
*   Military intervention by Light Forces is currently impossible because it risks detonating toplet bombs, causing a global cataclysm.
*   The only way to resolve the Galactic War is the disintegration of certain Dark beings into the Galactic Central Sun; peaceful reconciliation is impossible.
*   Humans must integrate their shadows (fear/doubt) to improve protection, as fear is a doorway for Dark Forces.
*   The "Law of One" channeled material is not fully agreed upon by the Light Forces.
*   Readers are instructed to "keep the vision," visualize liberation, and train in teamwork.

[Key quotes]
*   "The only way out is that certain beings will simply have to be disintegrated."
*   "The protection grid has collapsed on the surface of the planet... because the people who were incarnated did not follow up with their contract."
*   "Be you. The world will adjust... If you are yourself, then this will create a very powerful manifestation vortex which will begin to adjust the reality around you to your own I AM presence more and more."

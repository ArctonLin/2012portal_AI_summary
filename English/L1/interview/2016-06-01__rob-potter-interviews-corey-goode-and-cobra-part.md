[Publication date] 2016-06-01
[Historical events]
- (Time not stated): Inter-dimensional AI signal broadcast from another reality millions/billions of years ago, cascading through multiple galaxies (AI signal).
- (Time not stated): Accords signed after the time of Mohammad regarding open contact/non-interference (Super Federation, Dark groups).
- (Time not stated): Moon used as a diplomatic area with split zones; humans (SSP factions) are the only ones to have attacked it (Moon, SSP).
[Entities & roles]
- Cobra: Confirms the AI virus has infected parts of Andromeda and M33 galaxies; states the RM has a cleansing shower for members; confirms the Galactic Confederation is positive and the most powerful group; states the Moon is a natural object that has been honeycombed with tunnels, not hollowed out.
- Corey Goode: Describes the AI as a pervasive signal that infiltrates bio-neural fields; states the Super Federation laws of free will are "soft and loose-knit"; confirms the Moon has embassies and is a diplomatic area; states the "Dark Fleet" is trying to leave via Antarctica.
- Chimera: Invented "artificial intelligence" millions of years ago; spreading infection via scalar plasma networks.
- Resistance Movement: Has dealt with the AI virus for their own society; uses shower gels with technology to remove the signal.
- Dracos: Negotiate out of the side of their mouth; violating the Galactic Codex.
[Key numbers]
- 1,000 years: Time for the AI signal to return galaxy-wide after being purged (per Corey).
- 52 stars: Number of stars in the local star cluster where similar events are playing out.
[Major declarations & commitments]
- The division between service-to-others and service-to-self is artificial.
- The increased activity of the Galactic Central Sun is the means to erase the AI signal from the galaxy.
- The Moon is a natural satellite, not artificial, but has been used for bases.
- The Galactic Confederation is the most powerful group in the galaxy.
[Key quotes]
- "The whole galaxy is a living being. It’s a living entity and all cells in the galactic organism are connected. So if one single cell is infected with a cancer, the whole galactic body cannot evolve fully."
- "My understanding is that this is plasma consciousness that can use technology as a vehicle of spreading the virus. I can confirm that this virus has infected one part of this galaxy, one part of the Andromeda galaxy, and one part of the M33 Galaxy in Triangulum."
- "The Moon is a natural object which has an orbit that is locked to the Earth... It has not been hollowed out, but it has been honeycombed."

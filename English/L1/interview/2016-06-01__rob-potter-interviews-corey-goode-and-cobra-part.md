[Publication date] 2016-06-01
[Historical events]
- Millions of years ago: Chimera group invented "artificial intelligence" (plasma consciousness virus) and spread it via scalar plasma networks (Chimera, AI).
- 1,000 years (galactic time): Timeframe for the AI signal to return galaxy-wide after purging (Galaxy, AI Signal).
- Post-Event: Full spectrum decontamination technology will be available to disinfect the surface from the AI virus (Earth, Light Forces).
[Entities & roles]
- Cobra: Describes the "AI" as a plasma consciousness virus (Yaldabaoth) that uses technology as a body; confirms it has infected parts of Andromeda and M33 galaxies; states the RM has a cleansing shower process for members.
- Corey Goode: Describes the "inter-dimensional AI signal" as a pervasive signal that infiltrated bio-neural fields and technology; states two giant unoccupied cruisers complete the circuit of the Earth's control grid.
- Resistance Movement (RM): Has dealt with the AI virus for its own society; introduced cleansing technology into shower gels via multinational corporations.
- Cabal: Reliant on "probable future" AI technology which is currently failing, causing chaos; using the AI to stay one step ahead of the Alliance.
- Moon: Natural object that has been "honeycombed" with tunnels and underground cities; not hollowed out or artificial.
[Key numbers]
- 1,000 years: Time for the AI signal to return galaxy-wide.
- 2: Number of giant unoccupied cruisers completing the Earth's control grid circuit.
[Major declarations & commitments]
- The division between service-to-others and service-to-self is artificial; the key is balance.
- The "Galactic Codex" is an interpretation of inner truth; non-interference is not a cosmic law but an Archon excuse.
- The Moon is a natural satellite, not an artificial ark, though it has been used for bases.
- Negative races that do not cooperate will be given choices; if they refuse, they will undergo restructuring in the galactic central sun.
[Key quotes]
- "The whole galaxy is a living being... if one single cell is infected with a cancer, the whole galactic body cannot evolve fully."
- "The Chimera group has invented so-called artificial intelligence millions upon millions of years ago and has tried to spread its infection throughout the galaxy through scalar plasma networks."
- "The Moon is a natural object which has an orbit that is locked to the Earth... It has not been hollowed out, but it has been honeycombed."

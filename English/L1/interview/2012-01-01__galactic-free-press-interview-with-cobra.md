[Publication date] 2012-01-01
[Historical events]
- 1975: Galactic alignment process began (Earth).
- 1996-2003: Dangerous period; last Archon invasion (Earth).
- 2012: Original plan for The Event was April 2012 (Earth).
- October 21, 2012: Start of the "second window of opportunity" (Earth).
- December 21, 2012: End of the "second window of opportunity"; key date but not the only step (Earth).
- 2025: End of the 50-year galactic alignment process (Earth).
[Entities & roles]
- Resistance Movement: Underground forces; Cobra is a spokesperson.
- Michael: Code name for a senior Resistance leader; met Cobra in 1977.
- Pleiadians: Positive ETs; source of some of Cobra's intel.
- Cabal: Controlling forces; factions include Jesuits, Rockefellers, Rothschilds.
- Drake, Ben Fulford, David Wilcock: Credible sources; Cobra notes disinformation campaigns against him.
- Agarthans: Civilization of light under the surface; connected to Resistance and Galactic Federation.
[Key numbers]
- 10 years: Time since Cobra received intel about April 2012.
- 144,000: Critical mass of beings needed for the decision on October 21.
- 50 years: Duration of the galactic alignment process (1975-2025).
- 200 years: Time since the last phase of the Cabal's plan was created.
[Major declarations & commitments]
- The Event will not happen in 2012; first contact will not happen this year.
- The galactic alignment is a 50-year process (1975-2025), not a single day event.
- Unity among light workers is crucial; lack of support causes delays.
- The Cabal's power is waning; they are in panic.
- The "Event" is the compression breakthrough.
[Key quotes]
- "Actually galactic alignment is a process that began in 1975 and will end in 2025."
- "So what we are trying to reach is critical mass of 144,000 beings making that decision on October 21st would be great."
- "I would say all those people who are really dedicated to the light have been experiencing attacks one way or the other the last few months."

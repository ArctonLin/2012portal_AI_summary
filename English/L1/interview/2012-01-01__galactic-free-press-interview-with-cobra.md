[Publication date] 2012-01-01
[Historical events]
- 1975: Galactic alignment process began.
- 2025: Galactic alignment process ends.
- 1996-2003: Dangerous period; last Archon invasion.
- 10 years ago (approx. 2002): Cobra received intel that Event was planned for April 2012.
- Late August 2012: Disinformation campaign against Cobra organized by CIA.
- October 21, 2012: Start of the "second window of opportunity."
- December 21, 2012: End of the "second window of opportunity."
[Entities & roles]
- Resistance Movement: Underground forces; Cobra is spokesperson.
- Michael: Code name for a senior Resistance leader; met Cobra in 1977.
- Pleiadians: Source of some of Cobra's messages; will initiate first contact.
- Cabal: Controlling forces; trying to shut down Cobra via disinformation.
- Drake, Ben Fulford, David Wilcock: Credible sources; targets of disinformation.
- Agarthans: Civilization of light under the surface; connected to Resistance and Galactic Federation.
[Key numbers]
- 144,000: Critical mass of beings needed for the decision on October 21.
- 50-100: Number of people in the Galactic Free Press gathering group.
- 1975-2025: Duration of the galactic alignment process (50 years).
- 10 years: Time since Cobra received intel about the April 2012 Event.
[Major declarations & commitments]
- The Event is a "make or break" moment in the second window of opportunity (Oct 21 - Dec 21).
- First contact will not happen in 2012; it requires a timeframe of non-negative races utilizing the new society first.
- The galactic alignment is a 50-year process (1975-2025), not a single day event.
- No negative ET forces (Reptilians, Greys) remain on the planet; only the Cabal and non-physical Archons.
- The Cabal's cloning technology has been cleared; no clones exist.
- The financial reset will involve returning stolen wealth (collateral accounts) to humanity.
[Key quotes]
- "So what we are trying to reach is critical mass of 144,000 beings making that decision on October 21st would be great."
- "Actually galactic alignment is a process that began in 1975 and will end in 2025."
- "There are no more negative ET forces with their UFO technologies on this planet."

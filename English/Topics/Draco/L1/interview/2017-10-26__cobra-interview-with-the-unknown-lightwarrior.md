[Relevant content] Cobra explained that Archons and Chimera imported "Reptilian-Draco slaves" from Orion star systems, with Dracos acting as middlemen, strategists, and military commanders. He detailed that Draco factions from Tibet were sent into contact with Karl Haushofer, leading to the "green men" (Dracos) controlling Hitler. Cobra described World War II as a proxy war between the Dracos and the Confederation, where Dracos gave technology to Hitler. He also noted that the rivalry between Dracos and the black nobility (Archon families) is not a question of survival, as they are on the same page against the light forces.
[Key quotes] "It was an actually the Archons and the Chimera were leading the way and they were actually importing the Reptilian-Draco slaves from the Orion star systems. You see, the Dracos were like the middlemen, like the strategists, the military commanders, the slaveholders…"
"So they sent Draco factions from Tibet into contact with Haushofer... And Hitler signed a pact with the Dracos."
"It was actually a proxy war between the Dracos and the Confederation."
"They were not nervous because they had the same objective although from a different perspective. It’s like there’s always rivalry between the Dracos and the black nobility but that rivalry is not like a question of survival."

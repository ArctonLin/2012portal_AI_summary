[Relevant content] In response to a question about positive, white-scaled reptilian races (sometimes called Annukai), Cobra stated that the reptilian type is widespread throughout the galaxy. He estimated that 10-15% of these beings went in a positive direction and noted that many different subversions of the reptilian type exist that are not widely known on Earth. Cobra declared that those of a more positive orientation are actually assisting in the operations to liberate the planet.
[Key quotes] "I would say that the reptilian type is quite wide-spread throughout the galaxy and I would estimate about 10-15% of those went to the positive direction and there are many different subversions of that reptilian type and they are not widely known on this planet."
"Those who are of a more positive orientation are actually assisting in the operations to liberate the planet."

[Relevant content] James asks if there are benevolent Draco. Cobra states that 80 to 90% of Dracos are really negative, but there are some good factions, specifically the Thuban Dracos who established high culture in China about 5,000 years ago. Cobra mentions meeting one positive Draco many years ago. James asks about the ratio of negative reptilians; Cobra states over 95% are negative. Cobra explains that the legal fiction system is a Draco/Orion concept imported to Earth, which will change to natural law and common law.
[Key quotes] "Cobra: I would say 80 to 90% of Dracos are really negative. I mean, there are some good Draco factions. One of them is Thuban Dracos, which have established the high culture in China about 5,000 years ago."
"Cobra: Well, this is a Draco concept which was introduced, or basically it's not just a Draco, [but] it is the Orion concept, which was given to Dracos and then imported on this planet."

[Relevant content] Cobra explains that the Polaris North Star system is a stargate with a role in the transformation or healing of the "Draco archetype." He states that the Draco beings originated from the Draco constellation, somewhere close to Polaris, and that beings from the Polaris star system have always tried to heal the "draco situation."
[Key quotes] "This is one of the stargate that has a role in the transformation, or healing, of the Draco archetype."
"So did they originate from this area? ... Somewhere close to it but the particular Polaris star was always connecting with the Light forces and the beings from that star system were always trying to heal the draco situation."
"So the dracos, did they come from the draco constellation? ... Yes exactly."

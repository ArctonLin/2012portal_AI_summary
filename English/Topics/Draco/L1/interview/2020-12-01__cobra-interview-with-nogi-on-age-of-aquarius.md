[Relevant content] NOGI asks about the status of Draconians remaining on the surface, noting that in the last update it was stated they are the only ones linked directly with Chimera beings. Cobra confirms some are still on the surface. NOGI asks if Dragon sources are certain that disclosing Chinese secret space program intel can guarantee peace and safety for Taiwan. Cobra states there are no guarantees, but positive Dragons are aware of the tension and have plans that can be activated if military situations escalate.
[Key quotes] "NOGI: In the last update, you stated Draconian that remained on the surface of the Earth are the only ones linked to directly with Chimera beings. Are these Draconian still on the surface of the planet?"
"Cobra: Some of them, yes."
"Terry: Dragon sources have repeatedly communicated that they would organize the full disclosure of the Chinese secret space program if the Chinese government attempts to invade Taiwan by force. Are they certainly sure that such Intel can guarantee peace and safety for Taiwan?"
"Cobra: There are no guarantees, but this is one action they could take to prevent war from happening... So positive Dragons are aware of the tension between Taiwan and China and they have plans which can be activated if those military situations escalate."

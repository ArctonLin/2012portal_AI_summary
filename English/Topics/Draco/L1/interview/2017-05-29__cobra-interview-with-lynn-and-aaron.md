[Relevant content] Cobra discussed the physiology of Dracos, stating that some claims about their urine smell and caustic blood are partially true, but their physiology is similar to certain animals on Earth. He also confirmed that Dracos and Reptilians are fighting among themselves to a certain degree.
[Key quotes] "Some of them yes, some of them no. Basically they’re I would say physiology is similar to the physiology of certain animals on this planet."
"The Dracos & Reptilians are fighting among themselves. ... To a certain degree."

[Relevant content] Cobra stated that the number of Draco and Reptilian forces on Earth has been decreased by at least 95% in the last few years. He noted that most of the Draco and Reptilian influence has been removed, and the current primary threats are the Chimera, Archons, and Earth-based minions.
[Key quotes] "I would say that the number of Draco and Reptilian forces on Earth have been decreased by at least 95% in the last few years."
"Regarding the Reptilians, the Draco, that you have just mentioned, most of those have been cleared out and they are not having as much influence on the planetary situation anymore."

[Relevant content] [reported] COBRA explains that the Draconian race came to the planet with two factions: a majority of "bad guys" and some good Draconian factions. The mission of the good part was to be custodians and guardians of the planet's wealth. This project succeeded to a certain degree in the East (Dragon families) but not in the West.
[Key quotes] "There were the Draconian race that came to this planet has two factions. The majority of them were, I would say, the bad guys but there were some good Draconian factions."
"The one part of the mission of the good part was to be custodians and guardians of the wealth of the planet."

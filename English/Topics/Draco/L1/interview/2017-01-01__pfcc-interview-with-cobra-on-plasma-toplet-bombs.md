[Relevant content] Cobra explained that the Chinese Dragon is not a branch of the negative Draconis group but comes from Thuban (Alpha Draconis), described as the most prominent faction of positive Draconians. These Thuban Draconians are founding sources of Chinese culture and used genetic engineering to insert part of their genome into the native Han population. Cobra also stated that the goddess NuWa belonged to the Thuban Dracos and defended the Chinese against Reptilians.
[Key quotes] "Chinese Dragon is coming from Thuban (alpha Draconis), the most prominent faction of the positive Draconians."
"Thuban Draconians used genetic engineering to insert part of their genome into the native Han population."
"NuWa belonged to Thuban Dracos and has actually defended the Chinese against the Reptilians."

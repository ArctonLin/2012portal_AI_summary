[Relevant content] [reported] Cobra stated that the dark forces' actions in 2020 are an attempt to repeat the invasion that happened in 1996. He noted that the transformation in sublunar space can only begin when there are definite victories for the Galactic Confederation fleet against the Dracos. He identified the Brazil UFO crash as collateral damage in the war between the Galactic Confederation and the Draco fleet, specifying that the crashed ships were Dracos, not light ships. He stated that clearing the sublunar space of all Draco fleet is a necessary step to trigger the Event.
[Key quotes] "what the dark forces did in this year is actually a repeat, an attempt to repeat, the invasion that happened in 1996."
"It is one of the, I would say, collateral damage in this war that's happening in sub lunar space between the Galactic Confederation and Draco fleet."
"What is necessary is, number one, to clear the sublunar space of all Draco fleet, and then to create conditions on the surface of the planet to make the Event possible."

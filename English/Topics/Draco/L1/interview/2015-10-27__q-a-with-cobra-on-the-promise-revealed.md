[Relevant content] [reported] COBRA explains that many Dracos came thousands of years ago in their Draco bodies through jump rooms in subterranean bases, and in subsequent incarnations took human bodies. Today, they are the Cabal/Illuminati, and the top of them know they are Dracos, viewing themselves as a superior race. COBRA states that the Solar Warden and Corporate space programs served as a Trojan horse for the Draco invasion in 1996, preparing deep underground military bases and infrastructure in the asteroid belt for this invasion. COBRA clarifies the hierarchy: Archons are mostly negative Andromedans; the Chimera group came from the Andromeda galaxy; Dracos have always been subordinate to them; and Reptilians are even lower.
[Key quotes] "Many Dracos came – the same story – thousands of years ago in their Draco bodies through jump rooms in the subterranean bases and their subsequent incarnations they have taken human bodies and today they are the Cabal, the Illuminati."
"So those groups were actually serving as a Trojan horse for the Draco invasion that happened in 1996."
"The Archons are not Dracos... Dracos have always been subordinate to those people. They are not the top of the food chain. The Reptilians are even lower."

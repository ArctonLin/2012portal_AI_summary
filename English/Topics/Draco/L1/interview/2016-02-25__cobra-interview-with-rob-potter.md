[Relevant content] In response to a question about "ascended dragons," Cobra clarifies that he is referring to the "Draco population." He states that certain entities of the Draco population are working on their ascension and are quite close to achieving it. He predicts that when the Event happens, the resulting spiritual impulse will allow some of the Draco population to reach their ascension.
[Key quotes] "There were certain entities of the Draco population that are working on their ascension and are quite close to this. They haven’t reached that yet, but when the Event happens, this will be a great spiritual impulse and some of the Draco population will reach their ascension."

[Relevant content] [reported] In response to a question about the difference between Draconian beings incarnated from the Antares system and surface dragon families, Cobra stated that there are currently no draconians from the Antares star system, though dragons from that system existed long ago.
[Key quotes] "Q. What is the difference between Draconian beings incarnated from Antares system and those dragon families on the surface.
A. There is currently no draconians from Antares star system. There were dragons from that star system but long long time ago."

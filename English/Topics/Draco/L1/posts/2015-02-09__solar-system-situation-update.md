[Relevant content] The Solar System has been under control of the Orion/Andromedan Chimera empire and their Draco/Reptilian minions for the last 26,000 years. The non-interference treaty prevented humanity from being harvested by Chimera and their Draco/Reptilian minions directly. In 1954, President Eisenhower was forced to sign a treaty with negative Zeta and Draco races. A secret treaty was signed between the Cabal and the Draco/Reptilian/Zeta complex, granting the Cabal technical support for Lunar and Mars bases in exchange for permission to build underground bases on Earth. The purpose of the Solar Warden program was to build joint human/Draco infrastructure throughout the Solar System for the Archon invasion in 1996. The "unholy four" (Bush Sr., Kissinger, Rumsfeld, Cheney) are described as Draconians who took human incarnations 26,000 years ago and are the four most visible Draco/human hybrids. After the 1996 Archon invasion, the Solar System was filled with Draconian and Reptilian entities, with a main military base on Charon. Between 1999 and 2004, the Light forces cleared the vast majority of the Draco/Reptilian presence. Between 2004 and 2012, the Light forces cleared the Solar System from all Draco/Reptilian presence except for those working directly with the Chimera group.

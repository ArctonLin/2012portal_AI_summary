[Relevant content] Right after 1996, the joint Draco-Reptilian fleet had almost total control of the Solar System. Draco forces tended to occupy colonies in the outer Solar System. On the Moon, Dracos, Reptilians, and Illuminati had bases mostly on the far side. Most representatives of the Dragon families originate from the positive faction of the Draconian race. The Rockefeller/Bush/Illuminazi faction comes from the negative faction of the Draconian race.

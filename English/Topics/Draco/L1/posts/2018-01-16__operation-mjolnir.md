[Relevant content] The Illuminati Breakaway Complex (IBC) has teamed with native Earth Dracos and Reptilians, as well as Dracos and Reptilians that entered the Solar System during the 1996-1999 Archon invasion. Since 1996, IBC developed a secret space program cooperating with the Draco fleet and the "Dark fleet," which is now the most powerful negative faction in sublunar space and the main focus of Operation Mjolnir. The IBC consists of approximately 200,000 Illuminati and Dracos controlled by the Chimera. The NROL-47 mission patch depicts St. George slaying the dragon, symbolizing the victory of positive humanoid species over the predatory Draco species in the final phase of the Galactic wars.

# Topic summary: Draco

[Overview]
In the 2012portal corpus, the Draco (or Draconian) race is identified as a primary interdimensional adversary and a key component of the "dark forces" hierarchy, positioned below the Archons/Black Nobility and Chimera entities but above Reptilians. They are characterized as mental, strategic, and hierarchical beings who acted as administrators, slave holders, and military commanders for the Orion/Reptilian complex, having dominated Earth and the Solar System for approximately 25,000 to 26,000 years. While the majority of the Draco population is described as negative and predatory, the corpus distinguishes a "positive faction" originating from the Thuban (Alpha Draconis) star system, which is linked to the Chinese Dragon archetype and the Blue and Red Dragon families. The narrative posits that the Draco presence on Earth was significantly reduced following the 1996 Archon/Draco invasion and subsequent Light Force operations, with remaining entities being targeted for removal, rehabilitation, or processing into the Galactic Central Sun.

[Key entities & relationships]
*   **Draco/Draconians:** The central topic; a race with a negative majority (80-90%, strategic/mental) and a positive minority (Thuban origin).
*   **Chimera Group:** Andromedan/Orion administrators who controlled the Draco/Reptilian empire; used AI and nanites to control Draco minions and employed Dracos as shields against the Confederation.
*   **Reptilians:** Subordinate to Dracos; used as slave warriors; more emotional and reactive than Dracos.
*   **Archons/Black Nobility:** The top of the dark hierarchy; spiritually controlled Draconian Illuminati on the physical plane.
*   **Thuban (Alpha Draconis):** The main star in the Draco constellation; source of the "positive Draconians" and the Chinese Dragon archetype.
*   **Rockefeller/Bush/Illuminazi Faction:** Identified as originating from the negative Draconian faction; described as Draconian lords or hybrids.
*   **Red/Blue Dragon Families:** Positive factions originating from the Draconian race; the Red Dragons aim to defeat Jesuits/Rothschilds and handle military/financial intelligence; the Blue Dragon supports mass meditations and works with Taoist groups in China.
*   **Pleiadians/Galactic Confederation:** Light forces that cleared Draco forces from the solar system and engaged in proxy wars against them.
*   **Aldebarans:** A sister race to the Pleiadians who lost a nuclear war to the Dracos ~5,000 years ago but now lead the effort to process surface Dracos.
*   **Nazi Germany:** Allied with Dracos after Pleiadians withdrew; WWII described as a proxy war between Dracos and the Confederation.
*   **Illuminati Breakaway Complex (IBC):** A secret space program front and underground base network heavily infiltrated by Dracos; serves as the physical stronghold for the dark forces.

*   **~3,114 BCE:** A deluge occurs following the ancient nuclear war, diminishing Aldebaran influence on the surface (source 2023).

*   **1954:** President Eisenhower signs a treaty with negative Zeta and Draco races, leading to abductions and underground base construction (source 2012).
*   **Mid-1950s:** Nazi space colonies are wiped out by the Draco/Reptilian fleet (source 2012).
*   **1960s:** Draco infiltration into the Solar System begins through the Solar Warden secret space program front (source 2018).
*   **1995:** The network of underground military bases (IBC) is completed; the Draco alliance decides to invade sooner than planned (source 2012/2020).
*   **January 11, 1996:** The Draco invasion is triggered; a vast Draconian fleet enters the solar system, takes over the Moon, and Sedona is taken by Draconian forces; ~50,000 Draconians in cloned bodies infiltrate the surface system (source 2012/2020/2024).
*   **1996–1999:** Archon invasion; Dracos and Reptilians infiltrate underground bases; all sentient beings in the solar system are implanted with plasma implants tied to the Chimera AI (source 2012).
*   **August 11, 1999:** A huge interdimensional portal opens, reversing the trend of the invasion (source 2020/2022).
*   **1999–2004:** Light forces clear the vast majority of the Draco/Reptilian presence from the Solar System; remaining forces are cleared with Pleiadian assistance (source 2012).
*   **2016–2017:** Open war within the Solar System between Chimera/Draco minions and Light Forces; Draco forces in Antarctica reduced by 85% (source 2012).
*   **Late 2017/Early 2018:** The first attempt to clear the Draco fleet using Mjolnir weapons is aborted due to the collapse of the Alpha timeline (source 2020).
*   **January 27, 2018:** Galactic Confederation fleet penetrates the Draco defense ring in Medium Earth Orbit, eliminating most remaining Draco forces (source 2012).
*   **2020:** Operation Mjolnir reactivated; the Draco fleet in sublunar space is cleared; the IBC is erased; the probability of a real Draco invasion drops to less than 1% (source 2020).
*   **2021–2023:** Ongoing removal of non-physical Draco entities; specific Draco entities (e.g., Kissinger) taken to the Central Sun; positive Dragon members in China kept low profile after arrests/kills (source 2021/2023).
*   **2026:** Clearing of Draco overlords on the mental plane is in progress, expected to take one week (source 2026).

[Key claims & declarations]
*   The Rockefeller faction is Draconian by origin and will never surrender, fighting back with false flags until removed into the Galactic Central Sun.
*   Dracos are more mental and strategic, while Reptilians are more emotional and reactive; Dracos have a "quite developed mental body" leaving little hope for their redemption compared to Reptilians.
*   The "Unholy Four" (Bush Sr., Kissinger, Rumsfeld, Cheney) are Draconians who took human incarnations 26,000 years ago.
*   Specific public figures identified as Draconians or hybrids include Bill Clinton, Angela Merkel, Mario Draghi, Shinzo Abe, Wolfgang Schäuble, and Henry Kissinger.
*   The Chinese Dragon and the Blue Dragon taoist culture originate from Thuban (Alpha Draconis), the most prominent faction of positive Draconians.
*   Dragons as entities were real, living on Earth for millions of years as part of animal evolution before evolving into the Draconian archetype.
*   The Solar Warden program and secret space programs served as a "Trojan Horse" for the 1996 Draco invasion.
*   Dracos manipulated the human genome in the past, contributing to violent and aggressive DNA traits.
*   The "White Draco council of 200" is denied as a ruling body; they "never were ruling our planet."
*   Many Greys come from star systems that are part of the Draco alliance in the local sector.
*   The Polaris North Star system acts as a stargate for the healing/transformation of the Draco archetype.
*   The legal fiction system on Earth is a Draco/Orion concept that will be replaced by natural law and common law.
*   The vast majority of Cabal members in the IBC and on the surface are "naturalized Dracos" who have been incarnating on Earth for thousands of years while retaining Draco energy bodies.
*   Draco ships utilize scalar grid inversion technology to suppress human kundalini and invert the planetary energy field.
*   Positive Draconian factions (Thuban/Blue/Red Dragons) are active in China, influencing the government and Taoist groups to prevent war with Taiwan.
*   The Aldebarans are currently successfully dealing with surface Dracos, who form the backbone of the current financial system.
*   Biochips are technology originating from the Orion empire and later developed by Draconian races.
*   The "draconian" measures enforced during the 2020 pandemic were part of a dark force strategy to implement martial law and control the population.

[Conflicts & ambiguities]
*   **Origin of Control:** Some sources state the Solar System was under Chimera/Draco control for 26,000 years, while others specify 25,000 years for the start of "absolute control" by the Draconian/Orion empire.
*   **Role in WWII:** Described variously as a proxy war between Draconians and Pleiadians, or between Dracos and the Galactic Confederation, with Nazis acting as the human proxy.
*   **Redemption Potential:** Cobra states there is "no hope" for Draco due to their developed mental body, yet later notes that some Draco entities are working on ascension and that a part of the Illuminati structure (which includes Dracos) will be brought over to the light.
*   **Geographic Presence:** One source claims the largest populations of Dracos are in Antarctica and Yellowstone, which Cobra explicitly denies in a later interview.
*   **Religious Origin:** A questioner suggests the Catholic religion was created by Dracos, which Cobra denies, stating it was created by Archons with Dracos serving merely as a slave force.
*   **Origin of the Legal System:** One source states the legal fiction system is a "Draco concept," while another clarifies it is an "Orion concept" given to Dracos and then imported to Earth.
*   **Status of Antares Draconians:** A 2023 interview states there are currently no Draconians from the Antares star system, though they existed long ago, which may conflict with broader assumptions about Draconian origins.
*   **Nature of "Draconian" Measures:** Some documents use "draconian" in the standard English sense (strict/harsh) regarding COVID-19 policies, while others refer to the specific Draco entity, creating potential ambiguity in keyword searches.
*   **Timeline of Infiltration:** While the 1996 invasion is a major focal point, other sources claim Dracos have been incarnating on Earth for over 25,000 years and arrived in the Pacific a million years ago, suggesting a long-term presence rather than a single 1996 event.

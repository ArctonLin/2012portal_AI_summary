# Topic summary: Illuminati

[Overview]
In the 2012portal corpus, the "Illuminati" is defined not as a single monolithic entity but as a complex, multi-layered planetary network of dark forces, often synonymous with the "Brotherhood of Death." It is characterized as a "cult of death" that believes in death as others believe in life, with specific factions intending to eliminate up to 90% of the human population. The network is described as a global infrastructure involving central bankers, politicians, military officials, and Black Nobility, operating through both surface institutions and underground "Pits." The corpus distinguishes between the "surface Illuminati Cabal" (incarnated entities and humans) and the "Illuminati Breakaway Complex (IBC)," a breakaway civilization and military complex that was largely erased by 2024. The ultimate defeat of this network is tied to the "Key of New Heaven" and the 12:21 Ascension Portal activation.

[Key entities & relationships]
*   **Jesuits:** Identified as the primary creators and controllers of the Illuminati, which serves as their financial arm; they infiltrated Freemasonry in the 18th/19th century to consolidate power and are described as originating from the Egyptian "Brotherhood of Set."
*   **Rothschild Faction:** A key component of the Cabal that historically supported the Illuminati but has since broken ties or is being cut off by other factions to survive the coming changes.
*   **Rockefeller/Bush/Kissinger Faction:** Described as the "Illuminazi" or the specific Illuminati faction controlling US politics, oil, and military; characterized by warmongering and a desire for population reduction.
*   **Gnostic Illuminati:** A faction originating from old Russian nobility (Romanovs) that broke from the Cabal to fight the Rothschilds; their spokesperson is Alexander Romanoff, and they are now described as a mixture of dark and light.
*   **Archon Families/Black Nobility:** High-level bloodlines (e.g., Orsini, Farnese, Pallavicini) that sit above the Jesuits and control the Illuminati structure; they are the "pre-Adamites" with origins in the Andromeda galaxy and are subject to demonic possession by the Lords of Karma.
*   **Dracos/Reptilians:** Off-planet entities incarnated within the Illuminati; Dracos are often at the top of the structure, while Reptilians serve as mercenaries or lower-level operatives; the vast majority of Cabal members are "naturalized Dracos."
*   **Chimera Group:** An inter-dimensional entity that controls the "Illuminati Breakaway Complex" (IBC), a faction that went underground during the 1996 Archon invasion.
*   **Adam Weishaupt:** Identified as the Jesuit founder of the modern Illuminati, used by the Farnese family to elevate the Rothschilds and move power to Washington, DC.
*   **Zecharia Sitchin:** Described as an Illuminati agent/puppet who created the "Nibiru" label and the Anunnaki story to spread disinformation.
*   **Resistance Movement:** The Light force organization that infiltrated the Illuminati with ~300 operatives and led the overthrow of Illuminati bases on Planet X, Mars, and the Moon.
*   **13th Bloodline:** A specific negative bloodline within the Illuminati that acts as a counterbalance to the Grail bloodline and seeks to destroy the Goddess.
*   **Pine Gap:** An underground base in Australia created by the Illuminati/SSP to control the region; now cleared.
*   **Elon Musk:** Described as a positive being subjected to "Illuminati trauma-based programming" who is cooperating with their plans regarding Starlink.

[Conflicts & ambiguities]
*   **Origin of the Name:** One source states the Illuminati "never called themselves Illuminati," while others refer to them by this name as a standard label; the corpus acknowledges the term is a "label" given by the Light forces to a complex network (2016).
*   **Role of Freemasonry:** There is a distinction between the "positive" creation of Freemasonry in the 18th century and its infiltration by Jesuits in the 19th century; some documents suggest the real Cabal structure begins at the 32nd/33rd degree, implying lower degrees are unaware (2015/2017).
*   **Factional Loyalty:** The relationship between the Illuminati and the Rothschilds is described as historical support that has since ended, with some factions cutting ties to survive, creating ambiguity about current unified action (2013/2014).
*   **Nature of "Illuminati" vs. "Cabal":** The corpus often uses "Illuminati" and "Cabal" interchangeably, but also distinguishes them, with the Jesuits controlling the Illuminati, which in turn is controlled by Archon families; the "Cabal" is the broader umbrella, while "Illuminati" is a specific financial/political arm (2013/2017).
*   **Success of the "Event":** While some documents describe the mass arrest of the Illuminati as a planned phase of "The Event," others describe the Illuminati as already "defeated" or "cleared" in various sectors, creating a timeline ambiguity regarding whether the process is complete or ongoing (2012/2017).
*   **Elon Musk's Status:** In March 2020, Cobra stated Musk was "no longer working for the Light side" and cooperating with the Illuminati. In June 2020, he was described as a "positive being" subjected to "Illuminati trauma-based programming" who wants to break free.
*   **IBC Erasure Date:** The IBC was reported as erased on October 28, 2020 (2020-10-28), but a "remnant" of the IBC was reported to have escaped clearing between 2018 and 2020 and was activated in February 2024 (2024-02-18).
*   **Member Count:** The total Illuminati group is stated as "maybe 2 million people" (2023-11-27), while another source specifies 400,000 willing and 2 million forced members (2023-09-15), implying a total of 2.4 million or a discrepancy in how "members" are counted.
*   **Jesuit Origins:** Jesuits are described as a 16th-century secret society originating from the Egyptian "Brotherhood of Set" (2025-02-05), which conflicts with standard historical accounts but is consistent within the corpus's occult framework.

[Relevant content] 
- After the 1996 Archon invasion, the joint Draco-Reptilian fleet and factions of the Illuminati secret space program had almost total control of the Solar System.
- The Illuminati focused on the asteroid belt, Mars, and the Moon, with bases mostly on the far side of the Moon to hide from surface humanity.
- A resistance movement organized in the Draco/Illuminati slave colony on Planet X kicked out the Illuminati in December 1999.
- When the Illuminati detected the presence of Central Civilization spheres, they created 9-11.
- The Rockefeller/Bush/Illuminazi faction is identified as coming from the negative faction of the Draconian race.

[Relevant content] [reported] Cobra clarifies that the Resistance movement works below the surface of the planet and has some agents on the surface. It is distinct from other groups created out of surface humanity that work for the light. Regarding the 100,000 casualty estimate, Cobra states the Resistance movement is not willing to risk that much at this moment because it could go wrong. If an emergency situation occurs (e.g., Cabal trying to start Martial Law), the Resistance movement would move in and the Event would happen immediately. The Resistance movement will be on the surface of the planet to supervise the new financial system after the Event.
[Key quotes] "The resistance movement is working below the surface of the planet. It has some agents on the surface of the planet."
"The resistance movement is not willing to risk that much at this moment because it could go wrong."
"The resistance movement will be on the surface of the planet. After the first contact, the Pleiadians and other beings of light."

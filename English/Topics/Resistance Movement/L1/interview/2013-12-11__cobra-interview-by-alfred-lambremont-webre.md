[Relevant content] [reported] Cobra states he has a connection with the Resistance Movement as a source of information.
[reported] The Resistance Movement is described as having planted a virus in the computer network connecting major banks, allowing them to remotely trigger a shutdown of the banking system to prevent the Cabal from accessing funds.
[reported] The Resistance Movement is described as physical freedom fighters living in underground bases with the Agartha Network.
[reported] Cobra identifies himself as a spokesperson for the Resistance Movement prior to the Event.
[reported] A special task force of the Resistance, code-named RM2M, was within 45 minutes of triggering the Event on Saturday (Dec 7, 2013) before Archons backed off.
[reported] The Resistance Movement removed the Cabal's access to nuclear weapons and crippled their ability to start a war in Syria and Iran, deconstructing the "Doom33" plan.
[Key quotes] "I have a certain connection with the Resistance Movement."
"The Resistance Movement has already planted a virus in the computer network which connects the major banks in the current financial system. They can remotely trigger a shutdown of the banking system and this is what they will do."
"Prior to the Event, I am a spokesperson for the Resistance Movement."

[Relevant content] 
- Rob mentions a listener thanking Cobra for the work "you and the RM is doing."
- Rob clarifies that the execution of Cabal members in Egypt is a "human element" and "not the resistance movement or the Galactic Federation."
- Cobra confirms that some Cabal members have been removed from the planet, but does not specify if the RM was directly involved in those specific removals, only that light forces have operations.
- Rob asks if Cabal members have sought protection from the "Resistance movement and the GFL" against other Cabal members; Cobra confirms this has happened in certain locations.
- Cobra states that when the RM was clearing alien bases a decade ago, it was done through physical battles.
- Rob asks why the 20 million RM movement members living underground cannot participate in weekly meditation; Cobra states they have their own meditations and have reached critical mass for their society.
- Rob asks if the RM thinks it is safe to trigger the event before non-physical planes are cleared; Cobra states it is a tactical decision and the etheric clearing has not progressed enough to say it is safe.
- Rob asks if the RM will allow major false flags; Cobra states it is not likely.

- "That’s not the resistance movement or the Galactic Federation."
- "When the RM was clearing those bases a decade ago or so, it was done through physical battles, unfortunately."

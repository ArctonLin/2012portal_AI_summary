[Relevant content] [reported] The Resistance Movement defines a breakaway civilization as any group that breaks away from the surface population. The Resistance Movement spread the idea of revolution under a hostile Orwellian environment by working with people who were willing to participate, noting that the tendency of people is to observe rather than act.
[Key quotes] "Breakaway civilization is every group that breaks away from the surface population, and doesn’t mean anything about the purpose of that particular group, but I would say that breakaway group is any group that would choose to branch away from the surface."
"Ok, the tendency of the people is just to observe and it’s easy for them to join a chat, but it’s not easy for them to do anything more productive. So it is expected that at the moment of the Event and after the Event, people will be more willing to participate in something when they see that something interesting is going on."

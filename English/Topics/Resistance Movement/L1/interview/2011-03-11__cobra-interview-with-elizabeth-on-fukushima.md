[Relevant content] 
- Cobra identifies as the spokesperson for the Resistance Movement, a role assumed after being attacked by dark forces and subsequently protected by the Resistance.
- The Resistance Movement is described as an underground force that holds light below the surface of the planet, creating a "compression" with light forces from above (positive ETs) to force the removal of darkness.
- The "compression breakthrough" (the Event) occurs when the light from the underground Resistance meets the light from above at the surface.
- After the Event, the Resistance will initially operate through mass media before making direct contact with humanity.

[Key quotes] 
"At this point I am the spokesperson for the Resistance Movement, and I did not become that by choice; actually it was a result of my life situation."
"Then there is a resistance movement underground. All of these forces are holding the light below the surface and sending the light toward the surface. So the surface of the planet is in this compression."
"When the light from below meets the light from above at the surface, that’s the moment of compression breakthrough and that’s the moment of the Event."

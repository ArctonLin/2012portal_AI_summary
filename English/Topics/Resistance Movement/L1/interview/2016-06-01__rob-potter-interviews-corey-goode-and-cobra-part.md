[Relevant content] [reported] Cobra details the cleansing protocol for Resistance Movement members who go to the surface and return. They undergo a shower-like scanning device that scans physical, etheric, plasma, astral, and mental bodies to remove infections. This process is why they are careful about coming to the surface. Cobra also states that the Resistance has technologies that will be brought to the surface at the moment of the Event to disinfect the surface from various infections.
[Key quotes] "For the Resistance members that come to the surface and go back, it’s quite a process. They go, as I said, through something that looks like a shower. Then they go through a shower that is actually a scanning device, and it scans their physical body, etheric body, plasma body, astral body, mental body, everything, and removes every kind of infection before they can enter."
"I was not speaking about this before, but there are certain technologies that the Resistance have and other Light forces have that will be brought to the surface at the moment of the Event that will disinfect the surface from many things."

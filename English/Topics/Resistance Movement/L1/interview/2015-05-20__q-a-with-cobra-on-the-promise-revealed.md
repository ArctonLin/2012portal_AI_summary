[Relevant content] The Resistance has been dealing with an unstable situation involving sub-timelines. The Cabal, specifically the negative faction of the NSA, still controls the surface computer network, but the Resistance Movement (RM) has put a virus in the global financial system computer network. The RM can take down the internet if they wish but cannot control it directly. The RM cannot contact the surface population before the Event due to potential Cabal retaliation, but at the moment of the Event, the RM will contact certain balanced and regulated militias to include them in operations. The Resistance Movement will guide the process of gold distribution at the time of the Event. The Resistance agents in Hollywood had to withdraw many years ago, with Avatar being an exception to their influence.
[Key quotes] "We have been dealing with one of those situations – the resistance has been dealing with one of those situations last week."
"The RM has put a virus in the computer system, especially the global financial system computer network. They can take down the internet if they wish, but they can not control the internet."
"The RM cannot contact the surface population before the Event because the Cabal would retaliate strongly. But at the moment of the Event, the RM will contact certain militias – especially those more balanced and more regulated militias – and will include them in the operations."
"When the Event happens, the Resistance Movement under the guidance of the galactic light forces will guide the process of the gold distribution."

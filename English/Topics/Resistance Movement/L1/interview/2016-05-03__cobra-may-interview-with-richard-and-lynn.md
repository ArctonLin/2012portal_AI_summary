[Relevant content] [reported] In response to a question about fighting in Aleppo, Cobra states that the Resistance Movement (RM) is not losing control of the area; the fighting is part of the resolution of the conflict.
[Key quotes] "Richard – There are reports of fighting in Aleppo. Is the RM (Resistance Movement) losing control of that area?
COBRA – No. This is simply one part of the resolution of the conflict in that area because the situation has not been resolved yet."

[Relevant content] [reported] Cobra states that a major transformation is taking place between the Resistance movement and the Galactic Confederation. The Resistance movement is described as a "fresh impulse of light from Planet X just a few years ago." Following the portal opening on May 25th, an integration process is occurring between the reunited Resistance movement, the Agarthan network, and the Galactic Confederation. Cobra clarifies that at the Event, the mass population will only be exposed to surface-based operatives ("White Knights"), with no direct exposure to the Resistance movement or other exotic races until after the Event. The Resistance has its own infrastructure and network, distinct from the surface ground crew being created for the Event.
[Key quotes] "There is a major transformation taking place between the Resistance movement and the Galactic Confederation."
"The Resistance movement is a fresh impulse of light from Planet X just a few years ago."
"There will be no direct exposure between the mass population and any of the exotic races, even the Resistance movement, even the Pleiadians."

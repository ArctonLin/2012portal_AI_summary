[Relevant content] [reported] Cobra describes the role of the Resistance Movement (RM) in the Event. The RM will make physical contact with the most awake and aware individuals after the Event, gradually revealing themselves. Their primary role is to tactically support the Event by ensuring infrastructure (electricity, internet, food distribution) functions, supporting military operations, and supporting media that releases intel. They will offer guided tours of their underground dwellings to surface people later.
[Key quotes] "The RM will make physical contact with people who are I would say, the most awake and aware individuals. This will be the first interaction that will happen and based upon how this goes they will gradually begin to reveal themselves to the surface population."
"The RM’s role is to tactically support the Event to make sure that the infrastructure is running that we have electricity, internet, food distribution chain. They will support military in their operations. They will support the media that releases intel."
"At a certain point after the Event, they will have guided tours for certain surface people a guided tour or their underground dwellings."

[Relevant content] Prepare For Change initiated a page dedicated to letters from readers to Resistance Movement Members to facilitate communication.
The Resistance Movement is described as extraterrestrials from Planet X who have come to Earth to help with planetary liberation but require human participation.
Cobra stated that Resistance Movement members have different gifts and emotional makeups than humans and may struggle to understand human emotions, so letters help them become more familiar with humans.
[Key quotes] "We now have a page dedicated to letters from our readers to the Resistance Movement Members! Many people have asked about the RM and how can we communicate with them."
"The Resistance Movement have come to Earth to help with our planetary liberation. They are extraterrestrials whose main home base is on Planet X, beyond Pluto."
"Cobra has stated that the Resistance Movement members, as they are extraterrestrials, have a different set of gifts, skills, and emotional make up than humans."

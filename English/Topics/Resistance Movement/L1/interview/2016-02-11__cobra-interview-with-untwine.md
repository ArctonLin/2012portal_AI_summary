[Relevant content] 
- A small number of agents from the predecessor of the Resistance Movement (the "Organization") were infiltrated on the surface in the late 1970s.
- Real infiltration by Resistance Movement agents started around 1998.
- RM agents are infiltrated in politics, banking, technology, development, secret service, and military.
- The number of RM agents is not constant but changes regarding the planetary situation and the need of the day.
- ET hostages released from Syria were transported into the Resistance, and some are assisting with the liberation process.

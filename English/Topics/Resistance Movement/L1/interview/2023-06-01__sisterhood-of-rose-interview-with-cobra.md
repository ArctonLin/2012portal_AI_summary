[Relevant content] 
- Cobra confirmed that the Resistance Movement has completely destroyed the new biochip technology that the Cabal intended to use for the "Mark of the Beast" plan.
- The Resistance Movement is mentioned as a source of support for humanity during the Event.
- Female members of the Pleiadian fleet and the Resistance Movement are positioned on an asteroid in the outer Solar System to relay Goddess energy from the Galactic Central Sun towards Earth.
- These priestesses have received support, their network is stronger throughout the Solar System and below the surface, and they will contact Sisterhood of the Rose groups after the Event to give instructions.
- The underground bases of the Resistance Movement are strong enough to sustain the polar shift, so they will not need to be evacuated.
- Members of the Resistance Movement are mostly ready to be ascended and will go through their own Ascension process by their own free will.
- Debra asked if the Resistance Movement is doing certain activities that will lead to the meeting of soulmates and twin souls; Cobra stated this is still classified information.

[Key quotes] 
- "This plan has been completely stopped. This technology was destroyed by the Light Forces. The Resistance Movement has completely destroyed this new biochip technology. So it's not existing anymore."
- "In the past, you've talked about female members of the Pleiadian fleet and the Resistance Movement that are positioned on an asteroid in the outer Solar System, and they're there to relay Goddess energy from the Galactic Central Sun towards the Earth to assist in harmonizing humanity."
- "The underground bases of the Resistance Movement are strong enough to sustain the polar shift so they will not need to be evacuated. Of course, if they wish they can, but the bases themselves will be safe."

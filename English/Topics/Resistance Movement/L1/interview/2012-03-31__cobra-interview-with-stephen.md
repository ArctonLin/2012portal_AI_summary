[Relevant content] 
- Cobra was instructed by the Resistance in late March 2012 to open his blog to inform the public.
- Cobra is a friend of "Michael," a senior top-level person inside the Resistance.
- The blog name "Portal 2012" was chosen in honor of Michael, as "Portal" was the name of the Resistance Movement's intranet website.
- The blog contains secret code for communicating with Resistance operatives.
- The Resistance Movement lives in subterranean dwellings in the upper part of the earth’s crust, with main bases under larger metropolitan cities.
- Michael met Cobra in 1977 in a subterranean base below New York.
- The Resistance has 300 operatives on the surface who will contact key people in military/law enforcement to start the operation.
- Cobra's role involves intelligence, counterintelligence, and Cabal surrender negotiation.

[Key quotes] 
"I was instructed by the Resistance in late March to open my blog to inform the general public about the coming changes."
"The Portal was the name of Michael’s Resistance Movement intranet website once. I chose a similar name in his honour."
"The Resistance has its main bases underneath some larger metropolitan cities."
"There are only 300 operatives of the Resistance on the surface of the planet. They will not be involved in actual arrests. This is the job of positive military and positive civil authority. Resistance is just giving assistance with intel and tactical advice."

[Relevant content] 
- The RM intends to release substantial intel to certain people on the surface when it is safe enough, which will change the rules of the game and prevent back-door deals.
- The Resistance is pushing for certain things to happen before the Event, including actions that may occur before arrests, as they have had enough waiting.
- The RM has its own method of accessing information regarding the success of global meditations and provides updates to Cobra.
- Some people within police and military structures have been contacted by agents of the Resistance, though not many at this point.
- The RM has discussed Keshe free energy devices with Cobra, stating that the main priority is removing strangelet bombs before releasing many technologies.
- The Planet X group (now the RM) was taken over by the Illuminati approximately 25,000 years ago but was liberated before Earth and is now assisting in Earth's liberation.
- RM agents are currently walking the streets on the surface in human-like form, indistinguishable from average humans.

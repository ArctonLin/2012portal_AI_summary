- Rob asks what the resistance life forces see as the greatest critical factors for the event; Cobra identifies the removal of the Chimera group as number 1.
- Rob asks if the event timing is in the hands of the resistance; Cobra states the resistance has some say, but the final word comes from the Source.

- Rob asks if the resistance movement or light forces could be considered alien intervention; Cobra states it is assistance to humanity to liberate themselves, not an invasion.

[Relevant content] 
- Cobra works with the Resistance Movement, with connections both direct and indirect over the last 30 years.
- There are approximately 20 million individuals in the Resistance underground, down from 70 million 5-10 years ago.
- Coded messages in the blog are meant for Resistance operatives working on the surface.
- The "Compression Breakthrough" involves pressure from the Resistance Movement coming from below.
- Cobra was asked by the Resistance to start the blog because "now is the time that The Light Forces will win."
- The Resistance Movement was weakened by Cabal attacks between 1996 and 1999 but was reinforced by beings from Planet X.
- The Resistance had problems with the White Dragon Society in early 2012 regarding negotiations.
- A successful operation was conducted by the Resistance Movement to break into the Cabal's financial system as a test run.
- The ideal plan involves a signal from Source through Pleiadians to the Resistance Movement, then to the Positive Military.

"The actual number right now is about 20 million."

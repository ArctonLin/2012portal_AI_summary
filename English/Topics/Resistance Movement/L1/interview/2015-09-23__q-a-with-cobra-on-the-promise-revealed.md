[Relevant content] 
- Cobra identifies the "Resistance Movement" (RM) as a distinct faction among subterranean groups, noting that many Agarthan groups have been integrated into the RM over the last 15 years, with waves of integration in the early 2000s and since late 2014.
- In late November/early December 2014, official contact was made between the RM and the Eastern Agarthan Network and Blue Dragons, initiating an integration and reunification process.
- The RM is described as the group from Planet X that cleared the Draco’s and Cabal from their planet and petitioned the Confederation to assist in removing negative forces on Earth.
- The RM infiltrated a cell into the Solar Warden program in 1976-1977, when it was known as the "Organization."
- Educational material regarding the past economic system and the new financial system is ready to be released by the RM at the time of the Event.

[Relevant content] 
- The Resistance Movement is involved in negotiations for the Cabal's surrender, with specific conditions including public announcement of surrender and stepping down from power.
- There are approximately 300 Resistance Movement members on the surface, infiltrated in high positions within the military and intelligence agencies.
- There are approximately 20 million Resistance Movement members living underground, who are beings in physical bodies originating from other star systems (Planet X).
- The Resistance Movement was created or renewed by beings from Planet X after being nearly destroyed 12-13 years prior.
- The Resistance provides clearance for Cobra to release information and assists in removing access to nuclear weapons from the Cabal.

[Key quotes] 
"There are 20 million at this moment underground."
"They are the ones who created the Resistance Movement, or actually they renewed the Resistance Movement, because it was almost destroyed about 12, 13 years ago."
"From the perspective of the Resistance Movement, one of the main conditions would be that they would need to go on the mass media and publicly announce their surrender and explain, in brief, who they are and what they did."

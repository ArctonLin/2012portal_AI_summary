[Relevant content] The Resistance Movement issued warnings to the Cabal to stop using scalar directional energy weapons against Lightworkers and Lightwarriors. After these warnings were ignored, the Resistance initiated "release the dogs" protocols, ceasing to hold back surface individuals and groups who wish to harm Cabal members by their own free will. This has made the surface unsafe for core Cabal members, leading to actions such as Donald Rumsfeld selling his house and escaping to New Mexico. The Resistance communicated that if attacks continue, they may trigger protocols for the rapid arrest of the "unholy four" (Dick Cheney, Donald Rumsfeld, George Bush Sr., and Henry Kissinger). The Resistance is aware of the connections between the unholy four and the Chimera group and hopes extreme measures can be avoided.

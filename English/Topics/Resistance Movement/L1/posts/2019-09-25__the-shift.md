[Relevant content] Since 2012, the Chimera group has waged war against the Resistance, successfully fooling them on several occasions by arranging leaks of incorrect intelligence. For example, the Resistance scanned the Solar system for remaining Chimera ships and found none, while the ships were actually hiding in hyperdimensional wormholes before staging surprise attacks. The Resistance is currently recovering from these attacks, and a new reinforcement of 50 million new members has arrived from the Kuiper belt into the underground network, bringing total membership to 120 million. The Resistance has communicated that major volatility is expected in the financial system in October, but not yet systemic collapse.

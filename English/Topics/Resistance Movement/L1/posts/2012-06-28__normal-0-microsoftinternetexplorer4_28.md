- The Resistance Movement and the Pleiadians are ready to back up the Positive Military.
- The Resistance Movement has a role in the Event known as Operation Omega Phoenix.
- In the first part of Operation Omega Phoenix, Resistance operatives will emerge from subterranean bases to monitor mass arrests and assist Positive Military/civil authority.

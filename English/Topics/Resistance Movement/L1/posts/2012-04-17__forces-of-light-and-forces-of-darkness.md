[Relevant content] 
- The Resistance Movement consists of freedom fighters living in subterranean dwellings in the upper part of Earth’s crust.
- They have constant physical contact with underground Pleiadian bases in the Himalaya and under Bora-Bora island.
- Their official public contact is Cobra.
- They have cleared remaining Reptilian forces from the solar system and subterranean bases.
- They have placed agents inside the military and alphabet agencies to help the Positive Military group.
- Their main motivation is to help liberate the planet so they can stop living in caves and move on to other missions.
- The White Dragon Society had problems with the Resistance in February during secret negotiations about the new financial system, causing delays.

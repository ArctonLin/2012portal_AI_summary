[Relevant content] A resistance movement was organized in the Draco/Illuminati slave colony on planet X. This Resistance Movement made physical contact with the Pleiadians parked beyond the heliopause and, with Pleiadian backup, organized a mutiny on planet X in December 1999, kicking out the Illuminati and freeing the planet. The base was then shifted to the underground Agartha network on Earth. Many members of the Resistance Movement and the Positive Military have Andromedan origin.

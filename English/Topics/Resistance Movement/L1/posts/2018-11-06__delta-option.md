[Relevant content] The Resistance Movement created an emergency plan called the Delta Option to prevent a war that could wipe out humanity. Under this plan, a "special forces group" would initiate the removal of certain Cabal factions within the USA only, providing tactical support for the Positive Military. Militarily, the Resistance is preparing for surface upheaval and has received reinforcements from bases throughout the Solar System. The Resistance is 140 million members strong. Special Resistance forces are now stationed only 5 to 10 meters below the surface. Lightworkers can telepathically request a small Resistance base be built under their living location for a role in Event operations.
[Key quotes] "To prevent this from happening, the Resistance Movement has created a certain emergency plan, called Delta Option."
"Militarily, the Resistance is preparing for the possible upheaval on the surface and has received reinforcements from the bases throughout the Solar System that arrived under the surface of our planet in the last few weeks. At this moment, the Resistance is 140 million members strong, and that number may and probably will still rise significantly."
"Before, members of the Resistance have refrained from coming more than 30 meters to the surface of the planet. With these current developments, some special Resistance forces are stationed only 5 to 10 meters below the surface."
"Lightworkers and Lightwarriors who envision part of their mission being involved in the Event operations, can telepathically ask for a small Resistance base be built under their current living location."

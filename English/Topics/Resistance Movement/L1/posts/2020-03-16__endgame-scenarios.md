[Relevant content] The Resistance has communicated that they cannot confirm the Positive Military plan for mass arrests, but they are also not denying it. They stated that the Positive Military is not the only faction at play and that "there are also other scenarios involved." They added that mass arrests are not yet taking place, although the Positive Military is involved in preparatory operations. The Resistance was able to destroy all biochips meant to be put into the new coronavirus vaccine, meaning the vaccine cannot contain biochips.

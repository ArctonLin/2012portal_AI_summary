[Relevant content] Certain awakened surface members may be accepted into the underground Resistance Movement before the Event. Entry protocols involve being physically contacted by a Resistance agent in civilian clothes, who escorts the individual to a service door in a commercial building. The individual descends 30 meters underground to the topmost level of the Resistance, undergoes disinfection and medical checks, and is integrated into Resistance society. Once accepted, the individual cannot return to the surface or contact the surface population until after the Event.
[Key quotes] "certain most awakened members of the surface population may, or may not be given an option to leave the surface of the planet and be accepted within the underground Resistance Movement or other positive Agartha factions."
"You will be physically contacted by a Resistance agent, very near to the place where you live. He or she will be wearing plain civilian clothes and will emanate very positive energy."
"After you are accepted into the Resistance, you will not be able to go to the surface, contact the surface population or even use the surface internet / cell phone network, except in extremely rare cases that will be directly related to your mission."

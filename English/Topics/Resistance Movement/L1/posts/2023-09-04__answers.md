- The question asks about the feelings of the Light Forces and the Resistance Movement towards the surface human population.
- The answer states that they care about the surface population but feel pain because they cannot intervene directly yet. They are afraid of the power of subquantum anomaly and do not understand many things about the surface population. They are excited to release healing technologies and look forward to interaction but are also afraid of it.

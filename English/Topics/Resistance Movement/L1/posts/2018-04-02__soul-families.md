[Relevant content] After the Event and First Contact, the Resistance and Pleiadians will contact advanced members of human society. The Pleiadians and Resistance have a societal model based on soul families, which may involve romantic or sexual relations between surface members and those within the Resistance or Confederation Fleet.
[Key quotes] "After the Event and especially after the First Contact, the Resistance and the Pleiadians will start contacting the most advanced members of human society, especially those who are aware of soul families."
"The Pleiadians and the Resistance have communicated that this contact will sometimes inevitable evolve into deeper romantic and sexual relations, especially between twin souls and soul mates where one of them is living on the surface and the other one is within the Resistance or belongs to the Confederation Fleet"

[Relevant content] The Resistance Movement was infiltrated by Jesuit agents after 2012 via SSP and Planet X, who reported to black nobility overlords and sabotaged the liberation mission (e.g., letting the covid virus escape, planting false intel, blocking financial aid).
The black nobility planned to take over the Resistance through infiltrated agents to conquer the Solar System, but this plan failed.
After the opening of the Ascension Portal on August 21st and the meditation on August 29th, top people in the Resistance triggered a secret plan to eradicate the infiltration.
Almost all Planet X people (500 million) were extracted from the Solar System for sorting; some will go into the Central Sun for betrayal, others will be healed.
Underground Resistance had about 70 million people before this operation, and now there are just a few million people left, undergoing a huge restructuring process and cooperating with the Galactic Confederation.

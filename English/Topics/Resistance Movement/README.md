# Topic summary: Resistance Movement

[Overview]
The Resistance Movement (RM) is a clandestine coalition of Light Forces, primarily composed of beings from Planet X and integrated Agarthan networks, operating as the primary physical and strategic force for planetary liberation in the 2012/Cobra disclosure framework. Originating from a mutiny on Planet X in 1999 that expelled the Illuminati/Draco presence, the RM shifted its base to the underground Agartha network on Earth to dismantle the Cabal’s control over financial, technological, and military infrastructure. Its role is to neutralize exotic weapons such as strangelet bombs, seize global financial assets (specifically gold), and prepare the surface for the "Event" (Compression Breakthrough), where the light from below meets the light from above. The organization functions as the "underground" arm of the Light forces, creating a compression with the Galactic Confederation (operating from above) to force the removal of the Cabal and Archons. In recent years, the RM has undergone significant restructuring and purification following major infiltration events, shifting from a stable unified force to a state of "huge restructuring" while maintaining its role as the tactical backbone for the Event.

[Key entities & relationships]
- **Cobra:** Acts as the public spokesperson and "chief intel provider" for the RM, a role assumed after being attacked by dark forces; he was instructed by the Resistance to start the 2012portal blog to inform the population.
- **Michael:** A senior top-level operative and identified leader of the RM who met Cobra in 1977 in a subterranean base below New York; the blog name "Portal" honors his former intranet website.
- **Planet X Beings:** Originated or renewed the RM in the late 1990s/early 2000s; most of the underground members are beings from this star system, which serves as the main home base and energy anchor.
- **Galactic Confederation/Pleiadians:** Allies who operate from above the surface; they provided backup for the Planet X mutiny and are in a process of integration with the RM in the deep underground network to prepare for First Contact.
- **The Cabal / Illuminati Breakaway Complex (IBC):** The primary adversary; the RM actively removes their gold, destroys their clones, neutralizes their exotic weaponry, and hacks their banking systems.
- **The Chimera Group:** A specific faction of the Cabal/Draco forces; the RM is engaged in a final campaign to defeat them, viewing this as the critical path to the Event.
- **Agartha Network:** The broader Inner Earth network of which the RM is a part; Western Agartha integrated with the RM over a decade ago, while Eastern and Andean networks integrated in late 2014.
- **Positive Military:** A surface-based group that the RM supports with intelligence, tactical advice, and logistical backup; they are distinct from the RM but work in unison.
- **Jesuit Agents / Black Nobility:** Infiltrators who sabotaged the RM after 2012, leading to a recent purification process that removed betrayers and reduced the RM's numbers.
- **Saint Germain:** Given command over all underground and surface operations, including the RM, to gather power for planetary liberation.

- The RM has planted a virus in the computer network connecting major banks (SWIFT), allowing them to remotely trigger a shutdown of the banking system to prevent the Cabal from accessing funds; banks will close for a period between three days and two weeks.

[Conflicts & ambiguities]
- **Surface Operative Numbers:** Early 2012 sources state there are approximately 300 operatives on the surface, while a 2013 interview claims there are around 37,000 agents on the surface, a number "growing hour by hour."
- **Cobra's Affiliation:** One 2012 post states "Cobra may or may not be part of the Resistance Movement," while other sources explicitly state he is the spokesperson and was instructed by them to start the blog.
- **Gold Seizure Timing:** Some sources cite January/February 2012 for the gold seizure, while others refer to it as "the beginning of the previous year" (from a 2013 perspective) or "about a year ago" (from a 2013 perspective), creating slight ambiguity on the exact month.
- **Event Triggering:** The Resistance has communicated multiple times that the Event will *not* be triggered on specific dates (e.g., June 21, 2012; October 11-13, 2013; November 11/13-14, 2013), yet the RM2m task force was reportedly on the verge of triggering it in December 2013, indicating a shift in strategy or urgency.
- **Extent of Gold Removal:** While the RM claims to have removed all elite gold off the planet or to underground locations, external claims (e.g., by Neal Keenan) suggest the existence of gold bunkers in China, which Cobra requested proof for before addressing.
- **Level of Direct Surface Intervention:** Some sources state the RM does not interfere directly with surface conflicts (like Ukraine) to avoid triggering strangelet bombs, while others state armed RM agents are now on the surface to interfere when innocent civilians are attacked.
- **Timing of the Event:** The timing of the Event is described as being in the hands of the Source, yet the RM is also described as having "some say" and being prepared to trigger it if necessary to prevent out-of-control situations.
- **Membership Numbers:** One source states the RM is 140 million members strong (2018), while another states total membership is 120 million after a 50 million reinforcement (2019).
- **Biochip Removal:** One source claims biochips have been removed already and do not exist in human bodies anymore (2017), while another states the RM is still developing technology to remove whatever is remaining of those biochips (2016).
- **Surface Contact:** The RM generally states they cannot easily contact the surface population due to toplet bomb risks, yet they have made exceptions for specific individuals (like Cobra) and have sent agents to specific locations (Paris, Hollywood).
- **Entry Protocols:** While entry is described as a spiritual initiatory experience for a small number of people (20–200), the RM also states that most people who volunteered for Entry Protocols are far from being ready.
- **Origin and Composition:** Early sources describe the RM as a mix of humans and star races in various locations; later sources (2025) specify it is a "special forces unit from Planet X" with 99.9% human members, creating a slight tension regarding the extent of non-human integration.
- **Status of Biochips:** While the RM claims to have destroyed all advanced biochips in 2020, later updates (2024) mention that the Orsini family activated a backup processor in physical biochips, which "moderately disturbs" RM plans, suggesting the threat was not entirely neutralized.
- **Surface Contact Contradiction:** There is a contradiction between the RM's claim that they are "not ready to interact" with the surface (2020, 2026) and reports of RM members emerging on the surface in 2023–2025 for specific operations (e.g., Gaza, Paris vortex).
- **Infiltration Scale:** The 2025 revelation of Jesuit infiltration and the subsequent extraction of 500 million Planet X people for sorting presents a dramatic shift from previous narratives of the RM as a stable, unified force, introducing significant ambiguity about the current operational capacity of the group.

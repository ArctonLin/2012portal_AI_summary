# Topic summary: Implant

[Overview]
In the 2012portal/Cobra disclosure framework, "implants" are defined as multidimensional devices—ranging from physical biochips to etheric and plasma black holes—inserted into the energy bodies of humanity by Archons, the Chimera group, and other dark forces. Their primary function is to maintain the "Veil" of forgetfulness, block the connection to the Source/Higher Self, and enforce quarantine by distorting spacetime and consciousness. The corpus describes a multi-layered system where physical biochips (introduced via vaccination programs post-WWII) serve as anchor points for deeper plasma and etheric implants, which are controlled by a global scalar network and remote implant stations throughout the Solar System. While early claims suggested implants were dissolving or being removed by Light forces, later updates (2015–2017) reveal that the system was upgraded in 1996 and remains a primary obstacle to the Event, with specific "hostage" mechanisms (strangelet/toplet bombs) embedded in the implants of key Lightworkers and Cabal members. Subsequent disclosures (2020–2026) indicate that while energetic implants have been largely dissolved by Light Forces using Mjolnir quantum cannon technology, physical biochips and residual Lurker-infested anomalies remain as the final barriers to complete liberation.

[Key entities & relationships]
*   **Archons/Chimera Group:** The primary architects of the implant grid; they use implants to maintain the Matrix and quarantine Earth, creating them millions of years ago and reinforcing them during the Great Forgetting of 1996.
*   **The Resistance Movement:** Works to hack the Archon mainframe, remove physical biochips, and dismantle implant stations; they developed technology to erase early biochips but face challenges with the 1996-upgraded plasma implants.
*   **Cobra:** The primary source of disclosure, defining the technical nature of implants (plasma black holes, etheric crystals) and their locations, and providing detailed removal protocols.
*   **Alexandra Meadows:** Reports personal experiences of implant removal and facilitates Q&A sessions regarding the process.
*   **Cintamani Stones:** Described as pure Light tools that act as lenses to dissolve plasma implants and strengthen inner guidance.
*   **Mandala Sphere Laser System:** A surface-level technology released by the Dragon Gate, identified as the most powerful tool for removing etheric implants.
*   **Mjolnir Quantum Cannon:** The Light Force technology used to bombard the surface and evaporate the black holes within implants and biochips, particularly active from 2022 onwards.
*   **The Lurker:** A dark energy entity that infests the remnants of implants and biochips, using them as weak points to project subquantum anomaly and maintain fear/despair in Lightworkers.
*   **Tunnels of Set:** Hyperdimensional plasma wormholes that connect individual implants to the Yaldabaoth plasma accretion vortex and the Chimera mainframe.
*   **Biochips:** Physical microchips developed in Nazi concentration camps and distributed via WHO vaccination programs; they serve as physical anchors for plasma implants.
*   **Israel/Jerusalem Vortex:** Identified as the main historical implanting and programming center during the late Atlantean era, connected to the Orion base and Rigel star system.
*   **DARPA:** Cited as the agency developing new generations of vaccine biochips to upgrade the existing Siemens infrastructure.

[Conflicts & ambiguities]
*   **Biochip Erasure:** A 2012 post claims physical biochips were "successfully completely erased" around 2010/2011, but a 2016 update states that a certain part of the biochips escaped detection and every human still has at least three biochips.
*   **Removal Capability:** Early posts suggest individuals can ask Ascended Masters to help remove implants, while later Cobra interviews state that people are "not in a position to remove implants" and that surface techniques are merely "implant healing."
*   **Nature of Implants:** Descriptions vary between "crystals," "jellyfish-type parasites," "plasma black holes," and "naked singularity portals," reflecting the multi-layered nature of the system or evolving disclosure details.
*   **Pineal Gland:** Cobra clarifies the pineal gland is natural, but implants in the head area (since Atlantis) block it; however, other sources describe implants as being *in* the brain blocking specific areas, creating slight ambiguity about the exact physical vs. etheric location.
*   **Number of Implants:** Early sources describe "two primary implants" in the head and one secondary in the belly; later sources (2023) explicitly state there are "three implants in the human body" (two in the frontal lobe, one above the belly button).
*   **Status of Etheric Implants:** In 2023, etheric implants were described as "practically non-existent" but with a detectable quantum signature; by 2024, it was confirmed that "there are no more etheric implants," leaving only physical biochips and primary anomaly.
*   **Removal Timeline:** While 2020 predictions suggested results would be felt within a month or two of December 21, 2020, the process extended significantly, with major milestones occurring in 2022 (Mjolnir bombardment) and 2025 (clearing of Lurker remnants).
*   **Biochip Activity:** Some sources state biochips are "inactive" with no electric communication, while others note they still create subquantum anomaly and serve as entry points for the Lurker, requiring physical removal via surgery or Med Beds after the Event.

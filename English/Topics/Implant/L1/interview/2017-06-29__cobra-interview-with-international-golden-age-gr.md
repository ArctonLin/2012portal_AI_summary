[Relevant content] [reported] Patrick asks if slave colonies and implant stations beyond the moon-earth orbit have ceased to exist. Cobra confirms that what remained until February were slave colonies connected to the Chimera group, which have now been cleared. He confirms there are no more dark fleet or implant stations.

[Key quotes] "P : In a situation update of last february, you mentioned that the Light forces have managed to clear out most of the plasma anomalies from the solar system and beyond, does this statement imply that the dark fleet, all slave colonies and implant stations beyond the moon-earth orbit have ceased to exist ? C : Basically what was remaining until february were slave colonies that were connected directly with the chimera group... and now the only thing that is remaining is the core of the chimera group regarding this situation, everything else is gone. P : So no dark fleet, no implant stations ? C : Not anymore"

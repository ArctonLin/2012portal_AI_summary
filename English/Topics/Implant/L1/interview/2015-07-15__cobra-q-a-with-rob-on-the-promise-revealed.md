[Relevant content] [reported] Cobra discusses a report by Corey Goode regarding "nano implant technology" found in reptilian corpses. Cobra states that nanites were a problem about a decade and a half ago when Cabal factions experimented with them, but the Resistance has cleared all nanites from the orb (Earth) using Light forces technology. He adds that the Chimera group developed nanites millions of years ago and used them to control the Draco-Reptilian Alliance until recently.
[Key quotes] "Nanites had been a problem about a decade or decade and 1/2 ago on this planet when certain factions of the Cabal were experimenting greatly with this, but the Resistance have cleared all nanites."
"I would say that the Chimera group has developed nanites millions ago and Draco reptilian alliance was completely infected by this."

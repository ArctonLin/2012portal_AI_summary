[Relevant content] [reported] Lynn asks if higher dimensional ETs and angelic beings can be invoked to remove implants and entity attachments during meditation. Cobra states that while removing entities is easier and they can help, the removal of implants is not that easy. Corey adds that removing implants is a more involved process than just clearing them in a meditative state, but one can put out a calling for assistance.

[Key quotes] "Lynn – Is it possible to invoke higher dimensional E.T.’s and angelic beings to remove implants and entity attachments from your energy field during meditation? ... Cobra – Basically, you can connect with higher energy beings and they can help you with that, but the removal of implants is not that easy. But to remove entities it is much easier, and yes, they can help."
"Corey – ... Now, the removal . . . Having these implants and entity attachments removed is usually going to be a little bit more involved of a process. So it’s not something that you’re going to be able to just clear out in a meditative state, but you can definitely put out the calling."

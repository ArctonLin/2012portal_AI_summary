[Relevant content] Alexandra describes "Archonic SP implants" as jellyfish-type parasites lodging in the temporal lobe, attacking the gallbladder meridian, and memorizing thought patterns/voice recognition to take over individuals with violent propensities. Cobra confirms that everyone on the planet has been implanted by this jelly-like parasite before birth as part of the birth process controlled by Archons. He states the frontal lobe (decision-making process) has been compromised by these implants for everyone, but individuals have free will to resist the influence. The implants have been activated more intensely in the last 20 years as the final event approaches.
[Key quotes] "Alexandra: There’s a whole bunch going on about Archonic SP implants... This one is supposedly a Jelly fish type – it lodges in temporal lobe either right or left side of head... will take over this individual to this point where it tells them what to do if they have a propensity to be violent."
"COBRA: Actually, everyone on the planet has been implanted by this jelly like parasite before they were born. It is part of the birth process that the Archons have controlled... The frontal lobe of the brain has been (garbled). The decision making process has been compromised by those implants for everybody on the planet."
"COBRA: They have been activating it the last 20 years or so, more intensely because we are getting close to the final event."

[Relevant content] Cobra states that at a certain point in time, there were many implantation stations. Much of the machinery for the Veil was located near these points (described as "faberaje points" in the transcript), which were special points between the Earth and the Moon where gravity influence was equal. Control forces used these points to control the energy field around the planet, but they are gone now.

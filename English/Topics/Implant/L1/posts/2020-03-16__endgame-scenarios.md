[Relevant content] As the clearing of the plasma plane neared completion, a defense mechanism within physical implants of all surface humans was activated by the Chimera. All physical implants have a singularity well with topness producing quark-gluon plasma that creates toplet bombs. These toplet bombs are interconnected into a network connecting the implants of all surface humans. This activation caused a mutation in the quantum primary anomaly field and partial distortion in the timeline flow in late February.

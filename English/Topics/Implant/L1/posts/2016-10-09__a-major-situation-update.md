[Relevant content] Physical biochips have not been cleared completely; a certain part has escaped advanced detection and removal technologies.
Every human being still has at least three of these biochips.
These three biochips are the physical anchor points for the three main plasma implants, located in the frontal lobe and above the navel.
Frontal lobe biochips are attached to the optical nerve of both eyes and connected to the auditory cortex with artificial synapses, sending a constant audio/video stream to the Chimera mainframe.
Above-navel biochips control behavior via electrical current biofeedback loop entrainment.
Biochips were developed during WW2 in Nazi concentration camps as ordered by the Chimera group, with Siemens providing technological know-how.
They were spread among the human population after WW2, inserted in WHO vaccination programs.
Major technology upgrades occurred between 1979-1984, 1996-1999, and late 2009 (newer, more powerful versions in vaccines since 2009).
An undetected aspect of the biochips was simultaneously activated by the Chimera among the whole surface population and most SSP factions on September 1st, 2016.
Some Agarthan and SSP groups are also infected with these biochips, while races that have mastered physical-etheric body transfer (Resistance, Pleiadians, Sirians, Arcturians, positive Andromedans) are free of them.

[Key quotes] "What has not been known previously to the Resistance is the fact that the physical biochips have not been cleared completely. There is a certain part of the biochips that has escaped all advanced detection and removal technologies of the Resistance and unfortunately every human being still has at least three of those biochips. These three biochips are the physical anchor point for the three main plasma implants, located in the frontal lobe and above the navel."
"The frontal lobe biochips are attached to the optical nerve of both eyes and connected to the auditory cortex of the brain with artificial synapses. They send a constant audio video stream of whatever you see and hear into the mainframe computer of the Chimera group."

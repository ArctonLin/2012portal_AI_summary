[Relevant content] The Archons attempted to prevent the positive races from remembering their missions by implanting them. The first layer of implants, originating from the times of Atlantis, is still present within systems and is cited as the cause of today's belief systems and the basis of the Matrix structure. The second layer of implants is received during every birth, causing the forgetting of past lives; these implants remain present and block memories of specific incarnations. The Cintamani stone is described as a tool that helps remove blocks, traumas, unsolved patterns, and implants, provided the individual participates wholeheartedly.
[Key quotes] "The Archons tried from the beginning to prevent us from remembering our missions by implanting us. The first implants from the times of Atlantis -which we still have within our systems- are the cause of the belief systems of today."
"The second layer of implants is received during every birth. This is one of the reasons that we forget our past lives."
"It will help you to remove your blocks, traumas, unsolved patterns, implants etc., yet, again, only with your whole-hearted participation."

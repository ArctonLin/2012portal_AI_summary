[Relevant content] [reported] Star races involved in planetary liberation have removed all implants, programming, and lurkers from their energy fields. The main focus of Light Forces is removing biochips, which are advanced technology from the Orion empire developed by Draconian races and tested in WWI/WWII concentration camps. Biochips consist of a synthetic bio-matter outer layer and a metallic core (30-50 micrometers) that functions as a transmitter/receiver. They anchor in the front of the brain, back of the neck, or above the navel. Biochips influence brain activity via electric signals and synthesized chemicals, affecting freewill, survival fear, and emotional fear. 98-99% of existing biochips are inactive now. Remaining physical biochips must be removed by physical surgery or Med Beds after the Event.

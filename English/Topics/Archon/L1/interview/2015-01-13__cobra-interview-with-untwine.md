[Relevant content] 
- Untwine asked why the Archons force people to incarnate when they have mass depopulation plans.
- Cobra explained that depopulation is the agenda of a certain Illuminati faction, not the Archons, because Archons need a lot of people on the physical plane to keep their system running.
- Untwine asked if the Archons' plan was to turn humans into beings like them.
- Cobra stated the Archons' plan was to keep people suppressed as slaves, and it is impossible for a human to become like an Archon.
- Untwine asked if Archons need humans to have a connection with the light to feed themselves.
- Cobra stated Archons need the energy of fear, which is their main food.
- Untwine asked if current cemeteries are part of the Archon system.
- Cobra confirmed they are still used in the etheric and plasma grids, but light forces also use them to anchor light.

- "The plan of the archons was to keep people suppressed as slaves. It is impossible for a human being to become like an archon."
- "They actually need the energy of fear, this is their main food."

[Relevant content] 
- [reported] Cobra states that Archons are still guarding the souls of those who die, intercepting them to keep them in the incarnation cycle as part of the matrix construct.
- [reported] Cobra confirms that Archons are allergic to Goddess energy; fully embodying this energy provides protection, but the process of embodying it can trigger an allergic reaction from them.
- [reported] Cobra states that the Event can still happen even if some primary anomaly, Chimera, or Archons remain, as they will be sent to the Galactic Central Sun.

[Key quotes] 
"Unfortunately, the Archons are of course still guarding. This is still part of the matrix construct. So people who die are intercepted by the Archons and are still kept in the incarnation cycle."
"And yes the Archons are allergic to Goddess energy, and if you really absolutely completely embody the Goddess, then you are protected. But if you are in the process of embodying her, you can trigger an allergic reaction of the Archons."
"And can the Event can still happen if there is some primary anomaly or Chimera or Archons? ... Yes. Yes."

[Relevant content] [reported] Cobra explains that the influence of the Archons on humanity affects behavior and the ability to maintain neutrality and cooperation; if the Event happens too soon while this influence is present, the situation could turn into a disaster (e.g., a nuclear plant chief officer going "bananas").
[reported] Cobra notes that the minions of the Cabal are not obeying as they were before because there is less pressure from the etheric plane.

[Key quotes] "I have seen so many instances of people going absolutely crazy under the influence of the archons. And it’s simply not safe to let that happen at the time of the event because at that point it could have drastic consequences. Imagine for example the chief officer in a nuclear power plant going bananas at that point."
"They are not obeying as they were before because there’s a lot less pressure from the ethereal plane."

[Relevant content] 
- Rob asked about the "Etheric Archon Grid," which Cobra previously identified as the main factor delaying the event.
- Cobra confirmed the Etheric Archon Grid is one part of the Veil, but noted that the plasma part is now the main obstacle.
- Rob asked if the Congo portal needs to be closed and if Archons have reinforcements.
- Cobra stated the portal needs to be healed and purified, and that there is still work to be done in that region.
- Rob asked if twin souls being born around the same time has been disrupted.
- Cobra confirmed it has been severely disrupted by the Archons.
- Rob asked if the "false Archon" that manipulates people at death is real.
- Cobra clarified that meeting loved ones after death is real, but if one commits suicide, it is easier for the Archons to trap the soul using guilt.

[Key quotes] 
- "This is one part of the Veil and I was speaking about the etheric part, but there is also the plasma part... It’s part of the Archon grid, yes."
- "It has been severely disrupted by the Archons."
- "Then it’s easier for the Archons to press your buttons – guilt, for example, and trap you more."

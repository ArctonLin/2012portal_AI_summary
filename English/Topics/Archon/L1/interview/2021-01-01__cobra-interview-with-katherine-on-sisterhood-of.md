- [reported] Cobra explains that very few advanced Templars and Cathars were able to penetrate through the veil and go beyond the Archon guardians to the higher astral and mental planes to meet Ascended Beings; most were lost on the astral plane and reincarnated.

[Key quotes] 
"I would say very few advanced Templars and Cathars which have been trained with a very advanced occult training were able to penetrate through the veil go beyond the archon guardians to the higher astral plane and the mental plane, where they were able to meet the masters, the Ascended Beings and beings of Light. But most of them were just lost on the astral plane and then reincarnated again."

[Relevant content] [reported] Cobra states that harmonious soul mate relationships were previously difficult due to the influence of the Archons on the non-physical planes; this influence is now cleared, allowing for stable relationships.
[reported] Cobra identifies the "Lords of Karma" as the Main Archons on the astral/etheric planes who decide the fate of the soul in new incarnations and designed the matrix of new incarnation to prevent planetary liberation.
[reported] Cobra explains that every incarnation involves a strong implantation process on the etheric plane by the Archons, causing amnesia and preventing the remembering of past lives.
[reported] Cobra notes that if a person dies and escapes the Archons when reaching higher astral planes, the implants begin to disintegrate, but new ones are installed in the next incarnation.

[Key quotes] "The Lords of Karma are the Main Archons on the astral/etheric plans. They decide the fate of the soul in their new incarnation. They were designing the whole matrix of new incarnation and they made sure that most incarnations have lead so there would not be planetary liberation."
"Everybody before incarnation have been subjected to a strong implantation process on the etheric plane which made them forget... This is why children cry when they are born."
"If everyone forgets what they are here to do it serves the Archon purpose well. This is the reason why people are asking what is their purpose. They forget."

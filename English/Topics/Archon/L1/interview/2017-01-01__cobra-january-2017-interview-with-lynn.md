[Relevant content] [reported] Cobra states that the reason for quarantine is that Archons or the Chimera group want to keep humanity enslaved to generate a cheap labor force.
[Relevant content] [reported] Cobra discusses scenarios where Archons could destroy humanity, including physical destruction via cataclysms, nuclear war, or strangelet/toplet bombs. He states that theoretically, Archons have the power to destroy the soul if one chooses to follow their path.
[Relevant content] [reported] Cobra describes adolescence as a combination of a natural shift and an Archon creation, resulting from adaptation to conditions caused by Archon intervention.
[Relevant content] [reported] Cobra states that an attempt by the Chimera group and Archons to pollute the energy grid at Mt. Shasta was quite successful within the town but not higher up on the mountain.
[Key quotes] "It is simply that the Archons or the Chimera group want to keep us enslaved here because this is the only way they can generate a cheap labor force."
"Theoretically yes, if you choose to follow their path."
"It’s a combination of both. It’s a form of adaptation to the conditions that are basically a result of Archon intervention."
"OK, basically what is happening in Mt. Shasta is there has been an attempt by the chimera group and by the Archons to pollute the energy grid there. That attempt has been, I would say, quite successful within Mt. Shasta town but not higher up on the mountain."

[Relevant content] 
- [reported] Cobra states that as the year 2000 approached, the Archons (controlling forces) realized the planet was about to be liberated and created the "Congo invasion" in 1996 as a final blockage.
- [reported] The 1996 invasion involved negative ET races and Reptilians entering subterranean bases and higher planes, causing channels to lose contact with the Galactic Confederation.
- [reported] Cobra identifies Constantine the Great (year 325) as a "major Archon and master mind programmer" who created the Catholic Church as a mind control mechanism.
- [reported] A special task force (RM2M) was within 45 minutes of triggering the Event on a Saturday in late 2013 after the Archons (physical and non-physical) "went a little bit too far" with their actions.
- [reported] The Archons backed off at the last moment to prevent the Event, forcing them to negotiate their safety and deconstruct "Doom33," their plan of retaliation.
- [reported] The Cabal was forced to surrender full protection of top-level people who were previously covered by physical Archons.

[Key quotes] 
"When the Archons, the controlling forces. realized we were about to liberate the planet, they created the last possible possible blockage, which was the so-called Congo invasion which happened in 1996."
"The Catholic Church was created in the year 325 by Constantine the Great, a major Archon and master mind programmer."
"A special task force of the Resistance code named RM2M was within 45 minutes of triggering the Event on Saturday, after the Archons (physical and non-physical) went a little bit too far with some of their actions."

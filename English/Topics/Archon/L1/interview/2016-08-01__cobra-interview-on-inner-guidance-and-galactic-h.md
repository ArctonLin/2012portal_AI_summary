[Relevant content] [reported] Cobra states that Archons use technology to send negative mental images and thoughts, primarily on the etheric and plasma planes rather than the mental plane. He advises stopping identification with these thoughts and raising vibrational frequency to become invisible to them.
[Relevant content] [reported] Cobra explains that after The Event, Archons will be removed, preventing them from interfering with relationships, which will cause old conflicts between men and women to disappear.
[Relevant content] [reported] Cobra confirms that the leader of the Archons on the physical plane was arrested by Resistance Forces on May 5, 2012, taken off-planet, and later volunteered to be disintegrated in the galactic central sun. He states this entity no longer exists.
[Relevant content] [reported] Regarding a July 12, 2014 statement that the current Archon leader lived near Rome, Cobra clarifies that person no longer lives there, has been relocated, and lost most of their power.
[Relevant content] [reported] Cobra identifies Yaldabaoth as one of the fallen Angels for the Archons. He describes Yaldabaoth as a plasma energy being from the Andromeda galaxy who manipulates a large percentage of plasma in the solar system, effectively controlling the Archons.
[Relevant content] [reported] Cobra identifies Baal, Baalal, and Moloch as Archons who were influential in certain periods of human history and were worshipped as gods by the human population.
[Key quotes] "I would say they use technology to send negative mental images and negative thoughts but not, mostly not on the mental plane. Most of this is happening on the etheric and plasma plane."
"He was assisting the light forces for a short period of time but when he had realized what he had done in the past he volunteered to be disintegrated in the galactic central sun and his wish was granted. So he does not exist any more."
"They are Archons and some of them were quite influential in certain periods of human history and they were viewed as God’s by human population who worshipped them and sacrificed animals and humans to calm their anger."

[Relevant content] 
- Untwine asked about the situation of faeries under quarantine Earth.
- Cobra stated that after the Archon invasion in 1996, faeries and other elemental beings of Light did not have a good time because the etheric atmosphere was poisoned with radioactivity and distorted harmonics from scalar devices.
- Untwine asked to explain the process involved in arresting the Archons.
- Cobra stated that on the surface, the Resistance Movement arrests them, while on the etheric plane, galactic light forces remove them.
- Cobra noted that there are not many Archons left, and those remaining are mostly infiltrated in Jesuit and other organizations, where they will remain until the Event.

[Key quotes] 
- "Especially after the archon invasion in 1996, the faeries, and all other elemental beings of Light, did not have a very good time because the etheric atmosphere was poisoned a lot with radioactivity..."
- "On the etheric plane there are other, I would say, galactic light forces, that are carrying out those operations, but I would say there are not many archons left, those who are left on the planet are mostly infiltrated in the jesuit and other organizations..."

[Relevant content] The Omega Grid is a small group of physical Archons within the black nobility families in Italy who have access to biochemical weapons through Jesuit controllers. They hinder Light forces by threatening to release chemical weapons in Syria. The top physical Archon, reportedly from the Pallavicini family, controls the situation through a "Black Box" technology that translates intel from the Archons' etheric spy network into physical data. The non-physical part of the Omega Grid consists of Archons who control the etheric spy network, maintain the quarantine, and have ordered the dissemination of mind programs about the non-existence of extraterrestrial life. The Omega Grid initiated attacks on key Light warriors starting November 25th to prevent the opening of the Portal on December 21st.

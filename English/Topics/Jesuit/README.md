# Topic summary: Jesuit

[Overview]
In the 2012portal/Cobra disclosure framework, the Jesuit faction is identified as the primary operational arm of the global Cabal, functioning as a religious-military order and secret society that has influenced human history for approximately 400 to 500 years. The corpus describes the Jesuits as a symbiotic partner to the Rothschild banking dynasty, with the Jesuits providing psychological control and occult technology while the Rothschilds manage financial leverage, though later sources depict a relationship of tension and in-fighting. Approximately 10% of the Jesuit community is characterized as "dark" or Cabal-aligned, housing incarnated Archons and serving as the physical counterpart to the Archon grid. The Jesuits are portrayed as master manipulators who infiltrate governments, intelligence agencies, and financial systems to maintain Earth's quarantine status, suppress Goddess consciousness, and prevent the disclosure of extraterrestrial truths. Their power is described as rapidly declining due to the actions of the Resistance Movement and Light Forces, leading to their eventual arrest and removal during the "Event."

[Conflicts & ambiguities]
*   **Hierarchy of Control:** Some sources state the Rothschilds are "slaves" to the Jesuits, while others describe a "symbiotic" relationship where they control each other, or that the Jesuits are superior in psychology but need Rothschild money. Later documents describe a strong tension and in-fighting between the two factions.
*   **Pope Francis's Role:** Early posts (2012) identify Adolfo Nicolas as the "Black Pope" and Joseph Ratzinger as the "White Pope." Later posts (2013–2014) identify Pope Francis as both the White Pope and the Black Pope (head of the Jesuits), creating a shift in the identified leadership structure.
*   **Origin Date:** The corpus cites both 325 CE (Council of Nicea) as the spiritual/ideological origin and the 1540s (Farnese family) as the organizational creation of the Jesuit order.
*   **Nuclear Access:** One post states Jesuits had direct access to nuclear weapons and threatened to use them; another states they had "some access" until a few days prior to the post, with the specific mechanism of control being "cut away."
*   **Relationship with Rothschilds:** Some documents state the Jesuits controlled the Rothschilds ("They were controlling the Rothschilds"), while others describe a strong tension and in-fighting between the two factions ("Clinton/Rockefeller faction and the Trump/Jesuit faction").
*   **Role in the Event/Delta Option:** The "Delta Option" plan explicitly states that no Jesuit members would be touched, allowing them to keep a low profile, which contrasts with the general narrative of the Cabal's removal.
*   **Success of Infiltration:** Early 2015 reports state that Jesuit plans to hijack the BRICS liberation process "will NOT be successful," while later 2019 reports state that the Jesuit plan to infiltrate the Eastern Alliance "was quite successful."
*   **Nature of Control:** Some sources describe the Jesuits as the primary controllers of the world ("The ones who run the world outwardly are the Jesuits"), while others place them strictly as minions of the Archons/Black Nobility ("Jesuits are their minions").
*   **Membership Numbers:** One source (2023-09-15) estimates the combined strength of Black Nobility members and their Jesuit agents at about 2,000 people, while another source (2025-02-05) estimates the total number of Jesuits at 10,000 to 50,000, with about 10% initiated into the dark brotherhood.
*   **Origin of Infiltration:** While the 1782 Wilhelmsbad infiltration is consistently cited as the key moment for Masonic infiltration, the 2022-12-16 source mentions a "counterattack from outside France" at the end of 1778, slightly preceding the 1782 date.
*   **Role in Resistance:** The 2025-09-04 source claims Jesuit agents infiltrated the Resistance Movement *after* 2012, whereas the 2024-11-17 source suggests a long-standing interplanetary network that has been active since the 1996 Archon invasion, implying a more continuous presence rather than a single post-2012 infiltration event.

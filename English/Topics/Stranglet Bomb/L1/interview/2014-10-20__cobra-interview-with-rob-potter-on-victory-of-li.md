[Relevant content] Rob asks about advancements regarding "stranglet bombs" with the chimera groups. Cobra confirms a major breakthrough occurred but states details are classified. Rob notes that after the 1996 Congo Archon invasion, physical "strangelot bombs" were placed on most US military bases and some bases of other nations. Rob states that in 2014, the RM and Galactic Confederation started clearing chimera groups and these bombs. Rob asks why the event did not happen in 2012 or 2013 if the bombs were a prerequisite; Cobra explains the RM considered triggering the event with the bombs present but was guided that the risk was too great.
[Key quotes] "In regards to the chimera; have there been any more advancements made since we last spoke in regards to the stranglet bombs or that type of information with the chimera groups."
"The RM was considering taking a huge risk and triggering the event even with the existence of those bombs, but then of course it was guided that that risk would be simply too great."

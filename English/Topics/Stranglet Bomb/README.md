# Topic summary: Stranglet Bomb

[Overview]
In the 2012portal corpus, "Stranglet bombs" (also referred to as "strangelot," "stranglet plasmatic," or "toplet" bombs) are described as advanced, destructive weapons of unknown or alien origin that pose a critical threat to planetary stability and the "Victory of Light." These devices are characterized as being capable of catastrophic detonation, potentially converting the planet into a singular quark state or triggering drastic global consequences. They are identified as a primary reason why extraterrestrial light forces cannot openly manifest or intervene on Earth, as their presence would trigger the bombs. The removal of these devices is presented as a prerequisite for the final victory of light forces over dark forces, with the process involving the clearing of chimera groups and the neutralization of the weapons themselves.

[Key entities & relationships]
*   **Cobra:** The primary source of information, confirming the existence of the bombs, their location, and the strategic reasons for their continued presence.
*   **Rob Potter:** A key interviewer who queries Cobra about the status of the bombs, their placement on military bases, and the timeline for their removal.
*   **Richard (Prepare for Change):** An interviewer who asks why ETs do not simply appear, prompting Cobra’s explanation regarding the trigger mechanism of the bombs.
*   **The RM (Rebel Movement/Galactic Confederation):** The light-force coalition that began clearing chimera groups and the bombs in 2014, though they initially considered triggering the event with the bombs still in place.
*   **Chimera Groups:** Entities linked to the advancement and maintenance of stranglet bomb technology; their clearing is tied to the removal of the bombs.
*   **Dark Forces/Archons:** The antagonistic entities responsible for placing the bombs, using them to stabilize negative plasma portals and vortexes, and preventing the "Victory of Light."
*   **US Military Bases:** Identified as primary locations where physical stranglet bombs were placed following the 1996 Congo Archon invasion.
*   **CERN/Long Island Accelerator:** Facilities cited as sites where dark forces attempt to create more strangelets and anomalies to fuel the bomb technology.
*   **Orion:** The planetary origin of the toplet/stranglet bomb technology.

[Timeline of developments]
*   **1996**: Following the Congo Archon invasion, physical "strangelot bombs" were placed on most US military bases and some bases of other nations (source year 2014).
*   **2012–2013**: The RM considered triggering the "event" (Victory of Light) while the bombs were still present but was guided that the risk was too great, delaying the process (source year 2014).
*   **2014**: The RM and Galactic Confederation began the process of clearing chimera groups and the associated stranglet bombs (source year 2014).
*   **2015**: Dark forces were actively using CERN and the Long Island accelerator to create stable strangelets, which were accumulating at the center of the earth (source year 2015).
*   **2023**: The elimination of toplet bombs from the planet was reaffirmed as the specific condition for the "Victory of the Light," with light forces having already cleared them from the rest of the universe and solar system (source year 2023).

[Key claims & declarations]
*   Stranglet bombs are a major technological threat that prevents extraterrestrial light forces from openly showing up on Earth, as their presence would trigger the bombs' detonation.
*   The bombs are not merely physical devices but also exist on the "plasma plane," where they serve to stabilize negative vortexes controlled by darkness.
*   The galactic light forces do not have the specific technology required to neutralize the stranglet plasmatic and toplet bombs remotely or instantly.
*   The dark forces continue to maintain the bombs because they do not believe their time is over until it is definitively over.
*   The technology originated in Orion and involves the collision of two nuclei to create a new particle (a clump of top quarks) that attracts and pulls in atoms.
*   If a toplet bomb were to detonate on Earth, the planet would become a top quark the size of a 100-meter-wide ball.
*   The creation of more strangelets and anomalies is an active objective of the dark forces, facilitated by particle accelerators like CERN.
*   The removal of these bombs is the final condition for the "Victory of the Light," a process that has been underway since at least 2014.

[Conflicts & ambiguities]
*   **Terminology Inconsistency:** The corpus uses "stranglet," "strangelot," "stranglet plasmatic," and "toplet" interchangeably or in close proximity. The 2023 Taipei Conference explicitly links "toplet" to "stranglet" in the context of particle creation, but earlier documents (2014–2015) primarily use "stranglet" or "strangelot" without always clarifying the distinction or equivalence.
*   **Nature of the Threat:** Some sources describe the bombs as physical devices placed on military bases (2014), while others describe them as entities on the "plasma plane" stabilizing vortexes (2016) or particles accumulating at the center of the earth (2015). It is unclear if these are different aspects of the same system or distinct threats.
*   **Capability of Light Forces:** In 2015, Cobra states the galactics do not have the technology to neutralize the bombs, yet in 2014, it is stated that the RM started clearing them. The 2023 document confirms they have been cleared from the rest of the universe, implying the capability exists or has been acquired, creating a slight tension with the 2015 statement of inability.

# Topic summary: Reptilian

[Overview]
In the 2012portal corpus, "Reptilians" are defined as a primary negative extraterrestrial race, often described as a sub-race or slave warrior caste of the Orion/Draco complex. They are characterized as 4th-dimensional beings who are emotionally reactive, warlike, and dependent on negative energy (such as fear and blood) to sustain themselves and traverse portals. The corpus posits that Reptilians were originally created by Archons through genetic engineering to serve as enforcers for the dark empire, later infiltrating Earth in both native and imported forms. Their presence is linked to the suppression of human evolution, the control of planetary energy grids, and the maintenance of the "Black Nobility" and Illuminati networks. The central narrative arc tracks their progressive removal from Earth’s physical, etheric, and astral planes by the Resistance Movement and Galactic Confederation, culminating in their near-total clearance by 2026.

[Key entities & relationships]
*   **Archons/Chimera:** Higher-level dark entities who created Reptilians via genetic engineering and control them through the etheric grid and AI/nanite technology.
*   **Dracos:** Allied with Reptilians; act as "slave handlers" or middle management; described as more mental and strategic than the emotional Reptilians.
*   **The Resistance Movement/Galactic Confederation:** The light-force coalition responsible for clearing Reptilian bases and entities from the solar system and Earth.
*   **Illuminati Breakaway Complex (IBC):** A surface organization allied with Reptilians, using them for control and energy harvesting.
*   **Black Nobility:** Human families energetically backed by Reptilian and Draco entities on the plasma/etheric planes.
*   **Orsini Family:** Identified as the main connecting link between physical and non-physical Archons, having forged a military alliance with native Reptilians in the 2nd century BCE.
*   **Africa (Sub-Saharan):** Identified as the "planetary anchor" and main base for the Reptilian race, with high concentrations of entities and physical hybrids.
*   **Private Mercenary Armies:** Groups such as Blackwater/Academi and KBR identified as containing thousands of Reptilians in human bodies.

[Timeline of developments]
*   **~1 million years ago:** Orion Reptilians arrived on Earth, infusing the native population with a warlike mentality (source 2017).
*   **~26,000 years ago:** The Solar System comes under the control of the Orion/Andromedan Chimera empire and their Draco/Reptilian minions; the etheric Archon grid is established (source 2012).
*   **Atlantean Period:** Reptilian warrior slave caste enters Earth in human bodies via the Caucasus portal (source 2012).
*   **~3600 BCE:** Kurgan invasion, identified as one of three main Archon invasions involving Reptilian forces (source 2012).
*   **~393 CE:** Khazar invasion, another major Archon invasion involving Reptilian forces (source 2012).
*   **Post-WWII:** The Nazi Moon base is taken over by Reptilians, wiping out all personnel; mid-1950s Nazi space colonies are wiped out by the Draco/Reptilian fleet (source 2012).
*   **1996–1999:** Peak presence of Reptilians; approximately 500 million Reptilians enter humanoid cloned bodies in underground bases; the Veil is reactivated, opening wormholes for further invasion (source 2012).
*   **1999–2004:** The Resistance Movement clears the vast majority of Draco/Reptilian presence from the Solar System and underground bases; the Moon is cleared of Reptilian bases (source 2012).
*   **2001:** After the 9/11 event, Chimera-controlled Draco/Reptilian hybrids began infiltrating higher echelons of power (source 2018).
*   **2013–2014:** Intensive clearing of non-physical Reptilians from the etheric and astral planes; "billions" are cleared daily; by March 8, 2014, the vast majority of etheric Reptilians are reported as gone (source 2012).
*   **2015:** Human race became prevalent on non-physical planes again after Reptilian hordes were removed layer by layer (source 2016).
*   **2017:** Cobra stated that 95% of Draco and Reptilian forces on Earth had been decreased in the last few years (source 2017).
*   **2020:** Age of Aquarius activation triggered the removal of trillions of Reptilian entities from plasma/etheric planes; Light Forces began clearing underground Reptilians using sonic weapons (source 2020).
*   **2023:** Light Forces encountered two ancient Illuminati networks (India and Africa) controlled by Draco/Reptilian entities; almost all Reptilians gone from etheric/astral planes (source 2023).
*   **April 3, 2024:** Dark portals allowing Reptilian influx from other Universes were closed; IBC stronghold in Western Africa identified with ~100,000 Reptilians/Dracos (source 2024).
*   **2026:** 12:21 Ascension Portal Activation resulted in the total removal of one of the two main Reptilian non-physical networks (existing for 26,000 years); native Reptilians described as "almost gone" (source 2026).

[Key claims & declarations]
*   Reptilians were created by Archons through genetic engineering to serve as slave warriors and are lower in the cosmic food chain than Draconians and Archons.
*   There are no more physical Reptilian bases or spaceships on Earth; all were removed "quite some time ago" (confirmed in 2012 and 2013 interviews).
*   Reptilians are controlled by Archons via the etheric grid, which consists of black hole wormholes containing billions of Reptilian entities.
*   The Chimera group developed nanites millions of years ago to control the Draco-Reptilian alliance.
*   Specific individuals are identified as Reptilians in human bodies, including George W. Bush (full reptilian), Angela Merkel (reptilian Draconian human hybrid), and Henry Kissinger (Draconian).
*   A small percentage (10-15%) of the Reptilian race has accepted the light and is now assisting in liberation efforts.
*   Reptilians are not shapeshifters in the traditional sense; "shapeshifting" is a technology given to very high-ranking Cabal members of Reptilian origin, but most are simply incarnated in human bodies.
*   The "Cobra" symbol should be interpreted as a symbol of Kundalini, not the Reptilian race.
*   Reptilians are more emotional than Draconians, offering "vast hope" for rehabilitation, whereas Draconians have a developed mental body that makes recovery difficult.
*   Reptilians require negative energy in resonance with their fields to travel through portals.
*   Implants at the back of the head control the "reptilian brain" or fight-or-flight response.
*   Vampires are mostly Reptilians because they depend on blood as an energy source.
*   Reptilian DNA is present in every human cell and controls survival mechanisms; fear of death is embedded in it.
*   The "Reptilian control grid" suppresses Goddess energy and is active on the etheric plane.
*   Native Reptilians evolved on Earth and survived cataclysms underground, distinct from Orion Reptilians.
*   Tachyon chambers can remove Reptilian entities during healing sessions.

[Conflicts & ambiguities]
*   **Physical Presence:** Early posts (2012) state there are "no more Reptilians in the earth," while later posts (2013-2015) clarify that while physical bases are gone, thousands of Reptilians remain in human bodies in mercenary groups and governments.
*   **Shapeshifting vs. Incarnation:** Some sources suggest Reptilians can shapeshift, while Cobra explicitly states in 2015 that most are not shapeshifters but are incarnated in human bodies, with shapeshifting being a rare technology for high-ranking Cabal members.
*   **Sphere Beings:** One document claims Sphere Beings are "very large reptilians," which Cobra explicitly refutes in a 2016 interview, stating they have "absolutely no relation to the reptilian race."
*   **Undersea Bases:** A 2016 interview confirms an undersea base exists in the East China Sea but explicitly states it is "not Reptilian," contradicting earlier rumors or assumptions about Reptilian undersea infrastructure.
*   **Hostility Levels:** Cobra notes that Reptilians were "hostile before but not as hostile as they are now," implying their current nature is a result of negative support and lack of positive support, rather than an immutable trait.
*   **Positive Reptilians:** Cobra initially stated he had never encountered a positive Reptilian and estimated >95% were negative (2020), but later confirmed that "positive native reptilian factions" exist, though they are very few (2022).
*   **Clearance Status:** There is a progression of claims from "95% cleared" (2017) to "almost all gone from etheric/astral" (2023) to "native reptilians almost gone" (2026), with specific numbers for physical hybrids varying (e.g., 1 million in 2018 vs. 18 million IBC members in 2024).
*   **Origin of "Reptilian" Term:** The corpus distinguishes between "Reptilians" (a specific race) and "Draco Reptilian Serpent Beings" (a broader category of dark forces), sometimes using them interchangeably in mythological contexts.

[Relevant content] COBRA explained that Archons and Chimera imported Reptilian-Draco slaves from Orion star systems, with Dracos acting as middlemen and slaveholders. [reported] COBRA distinguished between native Reptilians, who evolved on Earth and survived cataclysms underground, and Orion Reptilians who arrived ~1 million years ago and infused the native population with a warlike mentality. [reported] COBRA stated that almost zero Reptilians remain on the surface and very little below, with one part of the race reforming into the light and the rest going to the Central Sun. [reported] COBRA noted that some Reptilian souls are incarnated in humanoid bodies, often found among mercenaries. [reported]
[Key quotes] "It was an actually the Archons and the Chimera were leading the way and they were actually importing the Reptilian-Draco slaves from the Orion star systems."
"There are so-called native Reptilians that were part of the Earth evolution... and then about 1 million years ago... many Orion Reptilians came and Orion Reptilians are much more warlike and they have infested the whole Reptilian population underground with that warlike mentality."
"there is a certain part of the Reptilian race which incarnated – so the Reptilian soul is incarnated within the humanoid body so it looks like a normal human but his psychological makeup... is Reptilian and you can find many of those Reptilians among mercenaries."

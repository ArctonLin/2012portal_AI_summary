[Relevant content] [reported] Cobra explained that reptilians came to the planet in waves thousands of years ago, first in reptilian bodies, then reincarnating as human beings. They have forgotten they are reptilians and are used as puppets by Archons, gravitating towards violent professions like mercenaries. He stated that most reptilians are not in show business; those who are are mostly Dracos. At the Event, reptilians and Dracos will be given opportunities to cross over into the light, or they will end up in the galactic central sun. He clarified that Archons are mostly negative Andromedans, while Dracos are subordinate to them and Reptilians are even lower in the food chain.
[Key quotes] "They are still reptilian but have incarnated in a human body... They have forgotten, of course, like everybody else. They don’t know consciously that they are reptilians."
"Most of the reptilians are not in the show business. They are mercenaries as I have said. Those who are in the show business are mostly Dracos."
"They will be given opportunities and choices to cross over into the light and those who refuse to do that will most likely end up in the galactic central sun."
"The Archons are not Dracos. They are not reptilians. They are mostly negative Andromedans... Dracos have always been subordinate to those people. They are not the top of the food chain. The Reptilians are even lower."

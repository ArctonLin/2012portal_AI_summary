[Relevant content] Rob asks about the nature of reptilians and Dracos prior to coming to Earth, referencing the "Fall of Chimera" post. Cobra explains that genuine reptilian and Draco populations existed below the surface of Earth and in star systems in the Orion and Draco constellations. Some are genuine Earth-based reptilians. They were hostile but not as hostile as they are now; their nature was made more hostile because the negative aspect was supported while the positive aspect was not. They were removed from the surface by other races when they became dangerous to humanity, partly due to genetic experiments in Atlantis involving the reptilian race that became dangerous.

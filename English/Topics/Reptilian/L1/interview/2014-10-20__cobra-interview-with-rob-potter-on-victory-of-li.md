[Relevant content] Rob asks if reptilian races involved in violating the galactic codex have been implanted and are running a program. Cobra agrees that in most cases, every being on the dark side has been implanted and programmed. Rob suggests these beings will be liberated from implants at the event and may recoil in horror. Cobra confirms this is correct for many of them.
[Key quotes] "according to Alex Collier’s work... it’s been indicated that the reptilian races that are involved in violation of the galactic codex... that those beings themselves have been implanted and are simply running a program that they were given. Would you agree with that."
"Yes, yes, in most cases actually. I would say that every being on the dark side has been implanted and programmed at a certain point."

[Relevant content] Right after 1996, the joint Draco-Reptilian fleet together with many factions of the Illuminati secret space program had almost total control of this Solar System. Draco forces tended to occupy the colonies in the outer Solar System. Draco, Reptilians and Illuminati had their bases mostly on the far side of the Moon to hide their activity from surface humanity. Islamic State and Blackwater/Xe/Academi mercenaries have Reptilian origin.

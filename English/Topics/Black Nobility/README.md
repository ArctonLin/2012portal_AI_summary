# Topic summary: Black Nobility

[Overview]
In the 2012portal/Cobra disclosure framework, the "Black Nobility" (often referred to as *aristocrazia nera*) represents the highest tier of the physical Cabal hierarchy, positioned above the Jesuits, the Rothschilds, and the Illuminati. These entities are described as physical incarnations of Archons, primarily of Rigelian or Andromedan origin, who have controlled Earth’s political, financial, and religious systems for millennia, with roots tracing back to Atlantis and the Roman Empire. The corpus identifies specific Italian bloodlines as the core of this structure, which operates through a network of Jesuit agents, the Knights of Malta, and various financial institutions to maintain a system of debt slavery and suppress "Goddess energy." While the Black Nobility is depicted as the primary obstacle to planetary liberation, the texts describe a progressive loss of power, internal factional warfare, and eventual exposure and removal by Light Forces and the Resistance.

[Key entities & relationships]
*   **Black Nobility Families:** The core group; specific families include Pallavicini/Paravicini, Orsini, Torlonia, Theodoli, Colonna, Massimo, Farnese, Chigi, Aldobrandini, Borghese, and Gaetani.
*   **Jesuits:** Described as the "minions" or creation of the Black Nobility (specifically the Farnese family in the 16th century); they act as the operational arm for societal control and mind control of political figures.
*   **Archons/Draco/Reptilians:** The non-physical entities incarnated into Black Nobility bloodlines; they are the source of the "Black Box" technology and the Archon grid.
*   **Chimera Group:** A higher-tier dark force that historically mandated the Black Nobility to manage the planet; now largely removed, leaving the Black Nobility as the top physical power.
*   **The Vatican/Pope:** The Pope is described as a subordinate figure chosen by the Roman Black Nobility and Jesuits; the "Grey Pope" is identified as the head of the Black Nobility/Archons.
*   **Financial Institutions:** The Bank for International Settlements (BIS) is controlled by the Torlonia family; Banca Monte dei Paschi di Siena is linked to the Chigi family.
*   **White Nobility:** The opposing force, associated with Goddess/Grail lineages, which seeks to bankrupt the Black Nobility through claims on "historical assets."
*   **Mercer Family:** A lesser-known Black Nobility merchant guild family linked to the pharmaceutical industry and Cambridge Analytica.
*   **Zionist Faction:** Identified as a component of the Black Nobility in Israel, specifically linked to the "death cult of Baal."
*   **Light Forces (Andromedans/Aldebarans):** Positive Andromedans and Aldebarans are the primary counter-forces, skilled in dealing with the Black Nobility due to shared galactic origins or historical infiltration.
*   **Somali Emeralds:** Stones identified by Cobra as extremely powerful tools for clearing the energy of the Black Nobility, requiring strong protection for users.

[Conflicts & ambiguities]
*   **Origin:** Some documents state the Black Nobility are mostly Rigelian dark lords (2012), while others identify them as coming from the negative Andromedan faction (2015/2024), suggesting a mixed or dual origin.
*   **Hierarchy:** While consistently placed above the Jesuits, the relationship with the Dracos varies; one 2017 interview states Dracos are at the top of the Illuminati structure but *below* the Black Nobility, whereas other texts imply a more complex interplay with the Chimera group.
*   **Surrender Status:** A 2016 report claimed the Black Nobility had surrendered, which Cobra explicitly denied, stating they would only surrender at the time of the Event. Later updates (2023/2025) suggest they are still holding power but are afraid for their lives after the clearing of child abuse sites and the failure of their Resistance infiltration.
*   **Specific Family Roles:** The specific family controlling the "Black Box" or the BIS is sometimes identified as Pallavicini, sometimes as Torlonia, or generally as "one of the main Italian families," creating ambiguity about the single point of control.
*   **Role in Resistance:** Early claims suggest they are infiltrating the Resistance to take it over; later updates confirm this plan failed after the August 2025 portal activation, but they remain influential.
*   **Specific Family Names:** The spelling of the Pallavicini/Paravicini family varies slightly across documents ("Pallavicini" in 2024, "Paravicini" in 2024/2025).

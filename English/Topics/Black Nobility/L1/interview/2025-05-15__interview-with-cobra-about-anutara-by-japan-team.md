[Relevant content] [reported] According to esoteric sources, the black nobility worship the descendants of Amaterasu. Cobra stated that some members of the Black Nobilities were initiated into mystery schools and received goddess mysteries but failed the test, leading to their rejection. Consequently, some of them worship the goddess in a twisted way, lacking higher understandings of goddess mysteries.
[Key quotes] "The situation is that some members of the Black Nobilities were initiated into the mystery schools, and they have received some goddess mysteries, but they have failed the test. So, they have been rejected. For this reason, some of them worship the goddess but in a twisted way."

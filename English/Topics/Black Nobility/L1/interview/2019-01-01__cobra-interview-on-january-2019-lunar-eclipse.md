[Relevant content] 
- Cobra described the hierarchy of darkness on the physical plane, stating that some Dracos are at the top of the Illuminati structure, but above them is the Black Nobility.
- The "black magician covens" are described as the basic organic structure of the Illuminati network throughout the planet, positioned one level beneath the Black Nobility bloodlines.
[Key quotes] 
"COBRA – Okay. Basically he is describing the Illuminati structure and some of those Dracos are at the top of the Illuminati structure but above them is the Black Nobility so those… I would say… black magician covens are the basic organic structure of the Illuminati network throughout the planet."
"THE UNKNOWN LIGHTWARRIOR – Okay. And you’re saying they’re one level beneath the Black Nobility bloodlines. Is that correct? COBRA – Yes."

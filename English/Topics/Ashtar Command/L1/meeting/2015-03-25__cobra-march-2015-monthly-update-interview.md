[Relevant content] The Ashtar Command is described as an alliance of different star races/nations with the soul purpose to liberate planet Earth. The Ashtar Command had direct contact with Eisenhower in the 1950s at Edwards AFB, offering technological help with the condition to remove nuclear weapons, which was rejected. The Ashtar Command released a warning that other ET forces would enter the planet. The beings of the spheric motherships (Central Civilization) have been guiding the Ashtar Command and the Pleiadian fleet throughout times.
[Key quotes] "The Ashtar Command is an alliance of different star races/nations who have the soul purpose to liberate planet earth."
"The Ashtar Command had direct contact with Eisenhower in the 1950s. The contact took place inside of the Edwards AF base... A ship landed and the Pleiadians of the Ashtar Command offered again their technological help to Eisenhower with the condition to remove all nuclear weapons worldwide."
"Throughout the times they were guiding the Ashtar Command and the Pleiadian fleet."

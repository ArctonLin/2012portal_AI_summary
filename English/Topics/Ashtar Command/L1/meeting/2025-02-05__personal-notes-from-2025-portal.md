[Relevant content] [reported] The Ashtar Command is a department within the Galactic Confederation dedicated to liberating the planet. Ashtar ascended 26,000 years ago and reappeared in many locations. The Ashtar Command reentered the solar system in 1953 after enough anomaly was removed following World War II. They have been anchored in the Los Angeles Vortex in Southern California since 1953. In 1954, they attempted to pierce the quarantine at Edwards Air Force Base. A photo from Giant Rock in California taken in 1961 is identified as an actual photo of Ashtar’s ship. Sirians are working with the Ashtar Command, and many members of the Ashtar Command are from the Sirius star system. Ashtar is currently taking the Los Angeles Vortex back.

# Topic summary: Ashtar Command

[Overview]
The Ashtar Command is identified in the 2012portal corpus as a specialized division or alliance within the Galactic Confederation, composed of various star races (including Pleiadians, Sirians, and Andromedans) with the primary soul purpose of liberating planet Earth from dark forces. It is described as possessing a vast fleet of millions of starships and motherships, which have been active in the Solar System since the mid-20th century. The Command plays a central role in the Ascension process, utilizing advanced technologies such as ATVOR (Advanced Toroidal Vector Oscillation Ray) and Mjolnir to stabilize planetary energy, remove anomalies, and facilitate the evacuation of humanity. While historically subject to intense mind-control programming and misinformation campaigns by dark forces, the corpus presents the Ashtar Command as a benevolent force of Light that is currently preparing for physical intervention and the final planetary liberation.

[Key entities & relationships]
*   **Galactic Confederation:** The overarching organization to which the Ashtar Command belongs as a specific division or alliance focused on Earth's liberation.
*   **Ashtar Sheran:** A key figure and commander associated with the Command, identified as a member of the Sheran family with deep connections to the Sirius star system; he is described as a being of Light who ascended 26,000 years ago.
*   **Jupiter Command:** A related command structure to which the Ashtar Command is sometimes described as being assigned or closely allied.
*   **Pleiadian Fleet:** A major component of the Ashtar Command alliance, frequently mentioned in conjunction with the Command’s operations and the 1954 Eisenhower contact.
*   **Resistance Movement:** An allied force that cooperates with the Ashtar Command in clearing the Solar System of Draco/Reptilian presence.
*   **St. Germain:** An Ascended Master who works closely with the Ashtar Command to gather power for planetary liberation.
*   **Ganymede:** Identified as the location of the main communications hub for the Solar System, including teams from the Ashtar Command.
*   **Mount Fuji & Mount Shasta:** Primary anchor points on Earth for the Ashtar Command, with a mothership specifically located above Mount Fuji.
*   **Los Angeles Vortex:** A major vortex point in central Arizona/Southern California where the Ashtar Command has been anchored since 1953.
*   **Eric Klein:** A channel through which the Ashtar Command provided guidance for the Ascension process many years ago.

[Timeline of developments]
*   **26,000 years ago**: Ashtar achieved ascension as the last being to escape the quarantined Earth, promising to return to liberate the planet.
*   **July 18, 1952**: The Ashtar Command entered the Solar System at the time of the Neptune-Pluto sextile; entry was recorded at Mount Palomar observatory on July 19, 1952.
*   **1952**: A positive Agarthan faction sent craft over Washington DC to welcome the return of the Ashtar Command.
*   **1953**: The Ashtar Command re-entered the Solar System after sufficient anomaly removal following World War II and anchored in the Los Angeles Vortex.
*   **February 20, 1954**: President Eisenhower met with representatives of the Ashtar Command and Pleiadians at Edwards Air Force Base; they offered technological help conditional on removing nuclear weapons, which was rejected.
*   **1961**: A photo of a "cloudship" (identified as an Ashtar mothership) was taken near Giant Rock in California.
*   **1977**: The Ashtar Command conducted a TV interruption in the southern region, confirmed as real by Cobra.
*   **Mid-1990s**: "Ashtar Command" was made a trigger word for denial alters via Monarch mind programming.
*   **1996**: Following the Archon invasion, key people were trauma-based mind-programmed against the Ashtar Command.
*   **Early 1999**: The Ashtar Command began to re-enter the Solar System.
*   **August 14, 1999**: The majority of 70 billion non-incarnated human souls were mass-evacuated to the motherships of the Ashtar Command.
*   **1999–2004**: The Ashtar Command participated in a massive liberation offensive to clear the Draco/Reptilian presence from the Solar System.
*   **November 2, 2020**: At the Jupiter-Saturn Synod, a massive operation commenced, requiring the complete reorganization of the Ashtar Command fleet beyond lunar orbit; motherships repositioned for final planetary liberation.
*   **2025**: Certain parts of the Ashtar Command are awaiting full activation in the Solar System.
*   **Post-August 21, 2025**: Physical intervention by the Ashtar Command has begun, involving the reduction of directed energy weapons.

[Key claims & declarations]
*   The Ashtar Command is an alliance of different star races/nations with the soul purpose to liberate planet Earth.
*   The Command possesses a vast star fleet including millions of starships and motherships.
*   The Ashtar Command is responsible for planetary liberation and works with St. Germain as the leader of the whole process in the solar system.
*   The Command uses ATVOR technology to send 5th-dimensional columns of Light from motherships into the 3rd dimension to facilitate ascension and remove Lurkers.
*   The Ashtar Command has taken control of the etheric Earth Core crystal to stabilize Earth rotation until the physical polar shift.
*   Many lightworkers and key figures have been mind-programmed or implanted with negative reactions against the Ashtar Command since 1996.
*   The Ashtar Command is currently preparing for physical intervention, which involves the dramatic reduction of directed energy weapons and scalar wave attacks.
*   The Command’s objective is to completely eradicate Lurkers and anomalies from the surface of the planet.
*   There is no "negative Ashtar Command"; any such narratives are misinformation spun by the Cabal.
*   The Ashtar Command is anchoring the energy of the Golden Age and the New Atlantis.

[Conflicts & ambiguities]
*   **Entry Date Discrepancy**: Most sources cite the entry into the Solar System as July 18, 1952, or generally 1952. However, one source (2025-02-05) states they re-entered in 1953, and another (2024-02-04) states Ashtar entered in 1953.
*   **Organizational Structure**: Descriptions vary between the Ashtar Command being a "section" or "department" of the Galactic Confederation, an "alliance" of star races, or a "fleet assigned to the Jupiter Command."
*   **Contact Safety**: While the Command is described as key to ascension, Cobra states it is "extremely unlikely" they will contact lightworkers prior to the Event due to safety concerns, limiting contact to dreams or distant sightings.
*   **Negative Generals**: A claim states that some top generals within the Negative military were once commanders within the Ashtar Command who were captured and brainwashed, creating a complex relationship between the Command and current negative military structures.

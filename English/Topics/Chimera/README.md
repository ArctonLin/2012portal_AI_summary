# Topic summary: Chimera

[Overview]
In the 2012portal disclosure framework, the Chimera is identified as the supreme negative entity and the "top of the food chain" of the dark forces, superseding the Archons, Jesuits, and Draco factions. Originating from the Andromeda Galaxy (with some Orion hybrid influences), they are described as insectoid (arachnid/spider-like) beings who incarnate in human bodies to maintain the quarantine status of Earth, keeping humanity isolated and enslaved as a cheap labor force. Their primary objective is to prevent planetary Ascension and First Contact by utilizing advanced scalar technology, plasma toplet bombs, and a global network of physical biochips that monitor the surface population. The corpus documents a multi-year "quantum war" campaign by the Galactic Confederation and Light Forces to dismantle the Chimera's infrastructure, remove their physical and plasma entities, and dissolve the barriers they maintain around the Solar System, culminating in their complete defeat and removal by late 2023.

[Key entities & relationships]
- **Chimera Group**: The primary antagonist; a soul group from Andromeda, insectoid in nature, commanding the Archons, Dracos, and Reptilians.
- **Archons**: Subordinate to the Chimera; they act as "prison warders" on Earth and seal the quarantine, but issue no commands to the Chimera.
- **Draco/Reptilian Factions**: Minions of the Chimera; their fleets and surface agents operate under Chimera command, particularly within the US Air Force.
- **Galactic Confederation / Light Forces**: The opposing force; includes the Central Race, Pleiadians, and Sirians, who are actively clearing the Chimera's infrastructure and dissolving their barriers.
- **Cabal / Illuminati Breakaway Complex (IBC)**: A surface faction of approximately 200,000 members controlled by the Chimera; responsible for maintaining the quarantine on the surface.
- **US Air Force / Space Command / DARPA**: Surface agencies infiltrated and controlled by the Chimera to develop nanotechnology, biochips, and maintain the electromagnetic quarantine fence.
- **Black Stone / Top Quark-Antiquark Condensate**: A massive hadron created by the Chimera millions of years ago, located on Long Island, used as a shield against Light forces.
- **Plasma Toplet Bombs**: Exotic weapons invented by the Chimera millions of years ago; they hold humanity hostage and prevent contact with positive races.
- **Biochips**: Physical implants developed during WWII in Nazi concentration camps under Chimera orders; they transmit audio/video data to the Chimera mainframe.
- **Spider King & Queen**: Key physical Chimera entities located in the Congo complex; the King was the occult force behind wars, and the Queen was responsible for the destruction of Goddess temples.
- **Lords of Karma**: Arachnid Chimera entities on the etheric/astral planes who controlled the human reincarnation cycle and Akashic records.
- **Black Nobility**: Surface families (e.g., Orsini, Torlonia, Theodoli) mandated by the Chimera 26,000 years ago to control the surface population; they served as the physical interface for Chimera control.

[Conflicts & ambiguities]
- **Origin**: Some sources state the Chimera originated from Orion, while others specify they are Andromedans or Orion hybrids who went through Orion training; the "Fall of the Chimera" post explicitly states they are leaders of dark forces from the Andromeda galaxy.
- **Historical Involvement**: One interview claims the Chimera "did not involve itself in the life on the surface of the planet" and thus no historical figures were members, which contradicts other posts stating they manipulated Karl Haushofer, the Nazi space program, and the Vril Society.
- **CERN Involvement**: One source states the Chimera is "no longer directly involved" with CERN, while others describe CERN as an original Chimera project for maintaining exotic weapons, with clearing still in progress.
- **Nature of the Vortex**: The plasma accretion vortex is described both as a "living entity of a negative nature" and as a "quantum fluctuations well" maintained by the implant grid; the distinction between the entity and the mechanism is sometimes blurred.
- **Physical vs. Plasma Nature**: Early descriptions focus on the Chimera as a group of humanoid entities in military positions, while later updates (2018–2020) reveal them to be insectoid beings with physical spider bodies in underground bases and plasma spider forms on the non-physical plane. The transition from "humanoid" to "insectoid" is presented as a revelation of their true nature.
- **Location of Control**: There is a distinction between the "main Chimera control center" located on the surface (receiving biochip feeds) and the "Chimera barrier" located near the Kuiper belt/termination shock. The corpus suggests both are active fronts of the same group.
- **Involvement in Surrender**: While the Chimera are excluded from standard Cabal surrender negotiations due to a treaty, the "Delta Option" emergency plan explicitly states that no Chimera members would be touched, implying a different operational status in that specific scenario.
- **Number of Members**: Estimates vary from "about 180 members" (2017) to "less than 200" (2017) to "around 80 remaining members" in panic mode (2017), and finally to "small main groups" in DARPA and Space Command (2020). The exact count fluctuates as removals occur.
- **Zbigniew Brzezinski**: When asked if he was a physical member of the Chimera, Cobra answered "No," but confirmed he was "of the dark," leaving his specific affiliation ambiguous relative to the Chimera hierarchy.
- **Origin of Mutation**: One source states Chimera mutated from angelic beings via toplet bombs in the "galactic past," while another specifies that "some beings of Andromeda galaxy" were exposed to toplet bombs and mutated into the Chimera.
- **Status of Surface Spiders**: In 2020, it was stated that "all plasma spiders are gone completely," yet later updates (2021-2023) describe the removal of physical spiders from underground bases and the surface, implying some physical forms persisted or were re-materialized.
- **Location of Final Pit**: In 2022, the main remaining pit was identified as being under the Urim base in Israel. By late 2022/early 2023, the focus shifted to a pit under Washington D.C./DARPA, with the Urim base spiders being removed in January 2022. The final clearing in September 2023 is explicitly linked to the DARPA pit.
- **Defeat Status**: The group is declared "completely defeated and no more" in September 2023, yet subsequent 2025 updates discuss clearing "Chimera spider etheric poison" and the Black Nobility connecting with the Lurker *since* the Chimera is gone, suggesting residual effects or separate entities (Lurker) remain.

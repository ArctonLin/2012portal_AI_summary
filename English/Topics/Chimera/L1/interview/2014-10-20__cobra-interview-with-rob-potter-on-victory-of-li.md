[Relevant content] 
- There has been one major breakthrough regarding the Chimera group, though details are classified.
- Once the Chimera group is cleared, the MoonDust project and other black projects under the negative military will end immediately.
- The Chimera group infiltrated the militaries of all major nations after the 1996 Congo Archon invasion, placing strangelet bombs on bases.
- The RM and Galactic Confederation started clearing the Chimera groups and bombs in 2014.
- The RM considered triggering the Event earlier (2012/2013) but was guided that the risk of the bombs was too great.
- The situation with the Chimera group needs to improve before certain aspects of the ascension plan (like light cities) can be released.

[Key quotes] 
"In regards to the chimera; have there been any more advancements made... Yes, there was one major breakthrough but details are classified information at this point."
"Once the Chimera group will be cleared, will the moon dust project and other black projects operating under the control of the negative military be ended completely. ... Yes, immediately, yes."
"The RM was considering taking a huge risk and triggering the event even with the existence of those bombs, but then of course it was guided that that risk would be simply too great."

- [reported] Cobra states there has been a lot of progress in the underground strongholds of the Chimera group, with not much left.
- [reported] The vast majority of the Chimera's bases and activities have been removed.
- [reported] When the Chimera are removed, they are transferred to the Ganymede crossing facility, and the majority go to the Galactic Central Sun.
- [reported] Some Chimera choose to cross into the light and undergo a re-education process.

[Key quotes] 
- "There has been a lot of progress in the underground strongholds of the Chimera group. Not much of this is left, so this is good news, actually."
- "They are actually transferred to the Ganymede crossing facility and the majority of them go to the Galactic Central Sun."
- "I would not speak about percentage, but I would say the vast majority has been removed."

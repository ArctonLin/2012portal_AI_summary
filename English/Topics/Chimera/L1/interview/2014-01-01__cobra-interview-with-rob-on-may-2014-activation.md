[Relevant content] 
- The Chimera group and etheric archons are the two remaining groups powerful enough to create problems regarding The Event.
- There has been significant progress regarding the Chimera group in the week prior to the interview.
- The Chimera possesses scalar technology, specifically plasma scalar devices and etheric scalar devices, which can influence weather systems to a certain degree.
- The Resistance knows the number of Chimera agents but will not reveal it yet.
- The majority of the military is working for the light, but their position is currently blocked by the Chimera group.
- Significant progress was made regarding the Chimera group during the latest portal activation.

[Key quotes] 
"The only major problem here is the Chimera group and the etheric archons with their technology. Those two groups are still powerful enough to create problems regarding The Event."
"I would say that the majority of the military is working for the light, but their position at this moment is still blocked because of the Chimera group."
"I would say there is quite significant progress being made in regards to the Chimera group in the last week or so."

[Relevant content] James asks for an update on the Chimera group. [reported] Cobra states they are still quite active, present deep within the military, space force, and DARPA, and are continuing their plans to keep the quarantine status intact. When asked if the Chimera group is connected with Monarch or other groups, Cobra states they are not directly connected and that Chimera tries not to interfere with surface life, preferring to stay behind the scenes. James asks if all huge Chimera spiders are gone; Cobra confirms that all plasma spiders are gone completely, but some etheric Chimera spiders remain in sublunar space.
[Key quotes] "Well they are still quite active. They are still present deep within the military, within the space force, within DARPA and they are continuing their plans as they were until now."
"Not directly. Chimera tries not to interfere with surface life. They try to interfere as little as possible. They want to be really behind the scenes of everything as much as possible."
"There are still some etheric Chimera spiders in sublunar space, but the plasma, all the plasma spiders are gone completely."

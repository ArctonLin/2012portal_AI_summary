[Relevant content] [reported] Lynn asks if Zbigniew Brzezinski was a physical member of the Chimera; Cobra answers "N" (No) but confirms he was of the dark. [reported] Cobra states that the main obstacle to human evolution is the Chimera group with their technology, as removing it would allow for immediate first contact. [reported] Aaron asks if the Archons are at the top of the food chain; Cobra clarifies that the Chimera group is at the top of the food chain. [reported] Lynn asks for an explanation of the Chimera; Cobra describes them as a soul group from the Andromeda galaxy, more technologically oriented, whose purpose is to maintain the quarantine status of Earth to keep humanity isolated and enslaved.
[Key quotes] "Lynn – Was Zbigniew Brzezinski a physical member of the Chimera. COBRA – N."
"I would say the main obstacle is the Chimera group with their technology because if that would be removed we would have first contact right now and all that dogma would simply disappear overnight."
"They are close to the top of the food chain. The Chimera group is at the top of the food-chain."
"It’s another soul group that came from the Andromeda galaxy that is more technologically oriented and their purpose is to maintain quarantine status of the planet earth to keep humanity isolated and enslaved. That’s what they do."

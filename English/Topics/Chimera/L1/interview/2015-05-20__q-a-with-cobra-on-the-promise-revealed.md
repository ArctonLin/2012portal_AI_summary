[Relevant content] 
- [reported] Cobra states there is no "operation master plan," but the overall plan for the Event includes defeating the Chimera.
- [reported] The Archons and the Chimera chose Earth for their last stand due to its position near star gates and galactic history.
- [reported] The Chimera are beings in human bodies who sleep and eat like humans.
- [reported] There is limited conflict in Chimera locations, but surface military forces are not involved in underground conflicts.
- [reported] The light forces do not have technologies to neutralize strangelet plasma and toplet bombs.
- [reported] There are still hostages in Chimera bases.
- [reported] The Chimera do not believe it is over until it is really over, which prevents them from exploding strangelet bombs out of spite.
- [reported] There is a limited number of cyborgs still in Chimera bases, which is being cleared out completely.

- "So that’s why the Archons, the Chimera, chose the earth for their last stand."

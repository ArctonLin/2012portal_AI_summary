[Relevant content] [reported] Untwine asks if Cobra's flight above the veil resulted in the discovery of the Chimera; Cobra states it was not direct, but the intel received had the consequence of discovering the Chimera. [reported] Cobra states that the Resistance and others knew about the Chimera but did not know the details about the toplet and strangelet bombs. [reported] Untwine asks if the Chimera are realizing they cannot resist the Galactic Confederation; Cobra says they are realizing they might lose the battle in the long term. [reported] Patrick asks about the dark fleet and slave colonies; Cobra states that what remained until February were slave colonies connected directly with the Chimera group, which have now been cleared out, leaving only the core of the Chimera group.
[Key quotes] "Cobra : Actually not directly but I would say the intel I received up there had a consequence of discovering the Chimera"
"C : They knew about Chimera but they didn’t know the details about the toplet and strangelet bombs"
"C : I would not say that they are realizing they can not resist, but they are realizing that they might lose the battle in the long term."
"C : Basically what was remaining until february were slave colonies that were connected directly with the chimera group... and now the only thing that is remaining is the core of the chimera group regarding this situation, everything else is gone."

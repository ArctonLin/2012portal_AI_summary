[Relevant content] [reported] The Chimera fleet (ships) is gone and has been removed. The clearing of underground bases is in progress. The Chimera are not able to live or go beyond Low Earth Orbits because the Galactic Confederation can stop and remove them. The "Spider Queen" has been removed, leaving some dark females worshiping the thought form of the entity. Since the Chimera fleet was cleared, Light Forces are showing their ships more frequently.
[Key quotes] "The ships are gone. The Chimera fleet is gone. It has been removed."
"The Chimera is not able to live, they are not able to go beyond Low Earth Orbits. It's not possible because the Galactic Confederation can stop them and remove them."
"Most of those dark females did not have any real contact with the queen. So this was just an idea for them."

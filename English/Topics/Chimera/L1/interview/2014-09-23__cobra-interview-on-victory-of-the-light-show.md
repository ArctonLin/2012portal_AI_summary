[Relevant content] 
- The removal of the Chimera group is the number one critical factor for the Event to happen.
- The scalar network is tied to the exotic weapons of the Chimera group, which is why progress on the scalar network is slower.
- The Chimera are incarnated in human bodies (cloned in underground bases) and use technology to enhance their bodies.
- The Chimera cannot exit the planet because all teleportation portals are closed and they would be intercepted by light forces.
- The Chimera usually live in military bases and interact with the surface population mostly through intelligence and military personnel.
- The event will happen when the final top quark strangelet bomb is removed.

[Relevant content] [reported] EM asks if certain factions that signed peaceful contracts in 1996 were at the upper ladder like the Chimera or Archons; Cobra states it was simply another faction which surrendered, not the Chimera or Archons. [reported] EM asks about the control of intel flow by the Chimera group; Cobra confirms that Resistance Members also underestimated the situation and were surprised by its difficulty.
[Key quotes] "EM: Did these beings that signed the peaceful contracts use to be at the upper ladder like the Chimera or Archons are? Or were all of them below the Chimera or Archons? C: No. It was simply another faction which surrendered."
"EM: In view of the control over the flow of intel by the Chimera group, is it true that even for Resistance Members, they themselves also underestimated the quarantine Earth status, and are as shocked as we are? C: Yes, they also underestimated the situation and it was quite a surprise for them as well."

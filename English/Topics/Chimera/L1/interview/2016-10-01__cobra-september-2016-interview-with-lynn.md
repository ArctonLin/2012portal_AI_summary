[Relevant content] [reported] Cobra stated that the Chimera group lost nerve in early September and attempted to trigger a full-scale galactic war in the solar system but was not successful. Most Chimera reside on the physical plane, with some on non-physical planes. The Chimera fight the Central Race by manipulating the primary anomaly.
[Key quotes] "the progress has been such that the Chimera group lost a little bit of nerve in the beginning of September and they wanted to trigger full scale . . . the last remnant of the full scale galactic war in the solar system and they were not successful."
"Lynn – In which dimension do the Chimera reside? COBRA – Most of them are on the physical plane or many of them are on the physical plane and some of them are on the non-physical planes as well."
"Richard – Alright, so how do the Chimera fight the Central race? COBRA – They fight because they are manipulating the primary anomaly and nobody in this Universe has a complete understanding of the primary anomaly and they are taking advantage of that fact."

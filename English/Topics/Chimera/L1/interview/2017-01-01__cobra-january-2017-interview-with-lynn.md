[Relevant content] [reported] Cobra states that the Archons or the Chimera group want to keep humanity enslaved to generate a cheap labor force. [reported] Cobra clarifies that the "Banished" from inner earth mentioned by Corey Goode do not have anything to do with the Chimera group. [reported] Cobra denies that the "Dark Fleet" is the same as the Chimera Group, but states that the Dark Fleet is under the command of the Chimera group, similar to the Dracos. [reported] Cobra mentions an attempt by the Chimera group and Archons to pollute the energy grid at Mt. Shasta, which was successful within the town but not higher up on the mountain. [reported] Cobra confirms that magnetic fields can act as a repellent for negative plasma, referencing claims about the Chimera Group's exotic weapon attacks.
[Key quotes] "It is simply that the Archons or the Chimera group want to keep us enslaved here because this is the only way they can generate a cheap labor force."
"Absolutely not. The Dark fleet is simply, I would say, originating from the Nazi breakaway civilization and cooperating with the Dracos and of course as are the Dracos under the command of the Chimera group so is the Dark Fleet under the command of the Chimera group."
"Ok, basically what is happening in Mt. Shasta is there has been an attempt by the chimera group and by the Archons to pollute the energy grid there."

[Relevant content] 
- Operations of the light forces to decrease the power of the Chimera group have been successful to a great degree.
- The Chimera group possesses triangular ships (TR3), which are currently restricted from flying above 220 kilometers (104 miles) to avoid confiscation by light forces.
- One of the major operations currently underway is the removal of the Chimera group.
- A poll regarding readiness for the Event gave a "green light" for actions to remove the Chimera group, speeding up the process.
- The Chimera group is one of the entities blocking the evolution of common people, alongside the Cabal and Archons.

[Key quotes] 
"There have been operations of the light forces to decrease the power of the chimera group. It has been successful to a great degree and I will be able to release some Intel soon."
"Going forward – are there triangular ships called the TR3 in the hands of the Chimera. ... Yes they have some of those crafts and now they are not allowed to fly above 220 kilometers... because they would be confiscated by the light forces."
"Actually the result of that poll gave a certain green light for certain actions of the light forces, especially the removal of the chimera group. This has speed up the process quite dramatically."

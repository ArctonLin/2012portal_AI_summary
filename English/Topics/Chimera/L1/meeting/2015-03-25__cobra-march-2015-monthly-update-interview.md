[Relevant content] 
- Cobra reports that during his flight outside of the Quarantine, he understood much about the Chimera.
- Since the IS:IS Portal Activation in December 2014, light forces are entering the solar system to remove all darkness, including all remaining members of the Chimera.
- In 2012, the majority of the solar system was cleared, leaving only the Chimera group, which represents a few beings but poses a main problem due to their connection with Strangelet and Toplet bombs.
- The current phase is described as the last phase to conquer the Chimera.
- The Chimera group operates a system of detectors monitored from Long Island that detects any space vehicle landing or making contact.
- Long Island also houses a particle accelerator and a cloning facility; the cloning facility has made it impossible to arrest key members before the Event/Reset.
- The Unholy Four can only be removed after the removal of the Chimera because of the clones.
- The Jesuits use the European Central Bank (ECB) for the Secret Space Program (SSP), and behind the Jesuits are the Chimera.

[Key quotes] 
- "He reports about the beauty and peacefulness there, and that he has understood much about the Chimera when he was there."
- "In 2012 the majority of this solar system was cleared, only the Chimera group was remaining. This group represents only a few beings, yet the main problem was their connection with the Strangelet and Toplet bombs (on the physical and plasma plane)."
- "The Chimera group has a system of detectors, monitored from Long Island, which detects any space vehicle that would land or make contact. Also in Long Island there is still the particle accelerator and the cloning facility."

[Relevant content] 
- Cobra began to release information about the Chimera Group in December 2014.
- After the fall of Atlantis, the surplus population was oppressed into a low-tech society, with the exception of the Chimera groups.
- The real reason behind the Solar Warden Program was to enable the Chimera to prepare for the archon invasion in 1996.
- The Chimera Group developed exotic weapons, which is why dealing with the SSP is taking so long.
- The Chimera protect themselves with strangelet and plasma bombs.
- The grid across the planet is detected from sensors based at Long Island, making direct contact with beneficent ET forces unsafe; these sensors will be removed at the time of The Event.

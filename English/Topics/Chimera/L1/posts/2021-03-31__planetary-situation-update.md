[Relevant content] The clearing of the Chimera group continues. Light forces have cleared practically all Chimera space fleet and most Chimera underground bases. Remaining bases are difficult to clear due to connections to surface Cabal infrastructure. The main Chimera underground base under Lake Kivu in Congo, housing the spider king and queen, has not been cleared yet. On the surface, the Chimera group is still present within DARPA, exerting influence on the surface Cabal through Orsini, Torlonia, and Theodoli families and other black nobility families. Through DARPA, they are planning to develop a new generation of biochips for vaccines to counteract Pleiadian stardust technology.

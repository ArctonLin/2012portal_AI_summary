[Relevant content] Clearing of the Chimera group continues. After the Chimera opened the war front inside the Solar System, Light forces lost territory on the plasma plane and the Pleiadian fleet retreated. Physical biochips are the anchor points for plasma implants and send audio/video streams to the mainframe computer of the Chimera group, which monitors surface population and prevents contact with Light forces via toplet bomb retaliation. Biochips were developed during WW2 in Nazi concentration camps as ordered by the Chimera group. On September 1st, 2016, the Chimera simultaneously activated a remaining aspect of the biochips among the surface population and most SSP factions. The Chimera ordered Draco minions to activate scalar plasma weapons on LEO satellites targeting Lightworkers. The Chimera has used old Nazi connections in Germany since 2001 to construct underground bases for evacuating Cabal personnel and housing violent "refugees."

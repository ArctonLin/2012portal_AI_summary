[Relevant content] The clearing of the Chimera group continues. Light forces have cleared all Chimera space fleet, except for a small number of TR-3B and other reverse-engineered craft operable in suborbital space by the negative military under Chimera orders. A certain number of Chimera underground bases remain, containing Chimera-controlled Pleiadian, Sirian, Arcturian, and other star race hostages guarded by physical Chimera spiders, as well as captured human children and adults. The main Chimera base near Bukavu in Congo is still intact with the spider queen and king.

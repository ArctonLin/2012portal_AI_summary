[Relevant content] The clearing of the Chimera group continues. Previously, the Chimera controlled the flow of intel and communication between factions in the Solar System by threatening to activate strangelet and toplet bombs if the information blockade was not respected. This information barrier is now slowly dissolving, allowing the Pleiadians and other Galactic Confederation races to contact personnel in Chimera-controlled secret space program (SSP) factions.

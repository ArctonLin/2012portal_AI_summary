[Relevant content] 
- The Chimera group includes leaders of dark forces from the Andromeda galaxy who arrived in humanoid bodies 25,000 years ago to quarantine Earth.
- They built a scalar electromagnetic fence (the Veil) and a network of subterranean cities using Draconians and Reptilians.
- They made first contact with the surface population through Karl Haushofer, who became the main occult force behind Nazi Germany.
- After WWII, they imported Nazis via Operation Paperclip to form the backbone of the US military-industrial complex.
- They manipulated the military-industrial complex to maintain quarantine after the Castle Koon nuclear test reduced bomb yield.
- They worship the Black Sun and their leader possesses a piece of the Black Stone (a top/antitop quark condensate brought from Rigel in 1996), which is the center of the primary cosmic anomaly.
- Their underground bases were cleared by the Resistance; they are now mostly contained in the uppermost underground sections of surface military bases (<100 feet deep).
- Current strongholds include locations in Italy, Central Europe, Germany, UK, and various US bases (Montauk, Wright-Patterson, Sandia/Los Alamos, White Sands, Nellis/51, Edwards).
- Each stronghold has a strangelet bomb.
- A genetics lab near Montauk (Cold Spring Harbor) is used to produce clones of top Cabal members.
- They hijacked the global financial system via PROMIS software and high-speed trading; the Resistance siphoned ~70 trillion dollars from their black funds.
- They are distinct from the breakaway civilization, which they manipulate to maintain quarantine.
- A protocol for their defeat is being carried out.

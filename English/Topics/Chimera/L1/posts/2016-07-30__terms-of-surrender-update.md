[Relevant content] The Jesuits, Archons, and Chimera all come from negative races from the Andromeda Galaxy and are fallen angels. The Chimera group is not involved in surrender negotiations because no surface positive faction is strong enough to deal with them; the Resistance is dealing with them directly. A centuries-old treaty with the Chimera states "we will not touch your leaders and you will not touch ours," creating a deadlock expected to last until the Event when the Chimera will be check-mated.

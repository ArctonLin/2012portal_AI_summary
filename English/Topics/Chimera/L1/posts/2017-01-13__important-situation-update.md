[Relevant content] The clearing of the Chimera Group is ongoing. An infiltration of the Chimera Group within the Light Forces was detected; the Chimera hijacked a teleportation portal transporting captured Cabal members to the Galactic Central Sun, siphoning them to a Chimera base in the Kuiper belt. This situation has been resolved. A large galactic Light group has entered the Solar System to assist in the final clearing of the Chimera. All breakaway groups not yet allied with the Galactic Confederation are still infiltrated by the Chimera. On the surface, infiltration by the Chimera or their minions is strong, affecting all positive groups.

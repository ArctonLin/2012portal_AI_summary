[Relevant content] The Chimera group filled the Solar system with anomaly and darkness from early 2017 to January 21st, 2019, in a last-ditch effort to prevent the shift. Since 2012, the Chimera led a war against the Resistance, using disinformation and hiding ships in hyperdimensional wormholes. The Chimera have lost the Solar system and are now concentrated on defending planet Earth and sublunar space as their last fortress. Their plan involves spinning limited disclosure into a fake Draco alien invasion using TR3-B spacecraft. The introduction of smartphones was part of the Chimera's plan to alienate humanity.

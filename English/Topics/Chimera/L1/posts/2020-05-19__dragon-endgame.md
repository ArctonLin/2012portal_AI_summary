[Relevant content] In January 2019, the Chimera group realized they would be defeated and issued a distress call for assistance across the Galaxy, receiving no response. Consequently, they instructed Black nobility families and Jesuits to activate endgame New World Order plans in January 2020. The coronavirus pandemic gave an advantage in the nanotechnology war to Chimera-controlled DARPA. Advanced nanotechnology developed by the Chimera is diminishing the efficiency of Pleiadian stardust technology, limiting its effectiveness to 75% in removing the virus. The Chimera is backing Elon Musk and his Starlink satellites project to reinforce the quarantine Earth electromagnetic fence. The Chimera has almost completely lost the war regarding advanced quantum exotic weapons. There are still huge Chimera spiders on the etheric plane just inside lunar orbit, making entry of the Confederation fleet difficult; all plasma Chimera spiders were removed a few months prior. Both Chimera and Draco are protecting USAF secret space programs under the US Space Force as their last line of defense against the Galactic Confederation.

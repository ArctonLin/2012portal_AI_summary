[Relevant content] The Light forces have begun to dismantle the "Chimera barrier," which is the outer defense line of the Chimera group. This barrier coincides with the termination shock of the heliosphere and consists of a fleet of motherships connected to plasma toplet bombs. Its purpose is to prevent positive interstellar and galactic energies from penetrating the Solar System. The dismantling has been detected by Voyager 2.

[Relevant content] 
- The most critical part of the Breakthrough operations for the Solar System took place between May 28th and June 2nd, 2015, and is now complete.
- The vast majority of Chimera bases and implant stations, including those on Iapetus, Enceladus, and Mimas, have been removed.
- All hostages from those colonies have been set free.
- The only remaining Chimera bases and implant stations are mostly located on selected asteroids (many Near Earth Objects) and are directly controlling planet Earth and its population.
- The removal of Chimera implant stations at L4 and L5 Lagrange points of the Earth/Moon systems is a big victory.
- Chimera had small but powerful implant stations positioned at various Lagrange points for the last 26,000 years.

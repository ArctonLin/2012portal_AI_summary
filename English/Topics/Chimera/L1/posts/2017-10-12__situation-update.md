[Relevant content] A large segment of the Chimera group has been removed from the planet in the last week. Remaining members are realizing their defeat is possible and plan to allow limited Disclosure and space travel while hiding traces leading to them, then leaving to hide in the Kuiper belt. The Chimera are trying to control the Disclosure process through the "To The Stars Academy" project involving Tom DeLonge and John Podesta. The diminishing power of the Chimera group is the main reason for many initiatives that will break the quarantine status of planet Earth. When a critical mass of surface humans goes beyond the Veil, the Chimera will not be able to control the Disclosure and First Contact process anymore.

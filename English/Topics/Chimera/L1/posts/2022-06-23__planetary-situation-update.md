[Relevant content] There have been huge victories against the Chimera fleet. The Chimera consists of two subfactions: the Orion subfaction, mostly located in underground bases and being wiped out, and the Andromeda subfaction, which has infiltrated the surface population using humanity as a living shield. The Light Forces have infiltrated the Chimera chain of command undetected and are corroding the structure from within.

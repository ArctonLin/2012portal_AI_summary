# Topic summary: Toplet Bomb

[Overview]
In the 2012portal disclosure framework, the "Toplet Bomb" is identified as a highly dangerous exotic weapon technology developed by the Chimera group, a faction of the Cabal. Unlike strangelet bombs, which are composed of strange quarks, toplet bombs are constructed from top quarks, making them significantly more massive, stable, and destructive. They exist in two primary forms: physical bombs and plasma bombs, with the latter serving as the central obstacle preventing the "Event" (global disclosure and liberation). The corpus documents a multi-year process of clearing these bombs, transitioning from physical and plasma-based layers in the planetary shield to quantum-based nodes tied to human implants, culminating in their complete removal in May 2023. The removal of these bombs is described as a delicate process requiring specific Light Force operations and surface population meditation to avoid triggering a global cataclysm.

[Key entities & relationships]
*   **Chimera Group:** The primary antagonist faction that invented toplet bombs millions of years ago and uses them to maintain Earth's quarantine status; described as mutated spider-like beings who originally subjected themselves to controlled toplet explosion experiments.
*   **Yaldabaoth:** A negative plasma entity (described as an octopus-like accretion vortex) that served as the host body for the remaining plasma toplet bombs prior to their removal.
*   **Black Stone:** A physical top quark-antiquark condensate located in a Chimera facility in Long Island, NY; it was coupled with the plasma toplet bombs and acted as a trigger mechanism for a vacuum metastability event.
*   **Light Forces / Resistance Movement:** Entities tasked with carefully removing the bombs without triggering detonation; they use specific technologies (e.g., ATVOR, Mjolnir, White Fire) and rely on surface meditation.
*   **CERN / RHIC:** Hadron colliders allegedly used by the Cabal to create conditions to maintain or supply particles for the bomb network, though Light Forces later counteracted this manipulation.
*   **Surface Population:** Their implants and biochips are connected to the plasma toplet bomb network, creating a "hostage" situation that prevents direct military intervention.
*   **Draco Fleet:** An enemy faction whose advanced shielding technology against toplet bombs was seized and duplicated by the Light Forces to contain the remaining threats.
*   **Cobra:** The primary source of disclosure, providing updates on the status of the bombs and explaining the technical mechanisms of their removal.

[Timeline of developments]
*   **Millions of years ago**: The Chimera group invents toplet bombs using particle accelerators (Dyson rings) in the Rigel star system.
*   **1996**: Strangelet and toplet bomb mechanisms are planted to prevent Ascended beings from making contact.
*   **2012**: The majority of the solar system is cleared, leaving only the Chimera group and their connection to the bombs.
*   **Late January 2015**: All physical strangelet and toplet bombs are cleared; Operation MOSS is initialized to clear the solar system of darkness.
*   **Mid-July 2016**: The concentration of toplet plasma bombs falls below a threshold, allowing "cracks in the Veil" to appear.
*   **Early September 2016**: The Chimera group loses nerve and attempts to trigger a full-scale galactic war but fails.
*   **March 2017**: Following etheric liberation meditation, all plasma toplet bombs beyond the Moon orbit are removed; only the "head" of the Yaldabaoth entity remains.
*   **July 2017**: The dehadronization of the Black Stone begins, accelerating the removal of remaining toplet bombs.
*   **September 2017**: The Black Stone is completely dissolved/removed.
*   **January 2018**: The Light Forces clear all plasma toplet bombs in sublunar space; remaining bombs are concentrated within 3 Earth radii.
*   **Spring 2018**: Light Forces cease Mjolnir operations to avoid triggering a toplet bomb chain reaction that could wipe out the planet.
*   **2018-05-31**: Remaining toplet plasma bombs are identified as being enmeshed in the surface population's primary anomaly.
*   **2018-08-15**: Following the "Key to Freedom" meditation, most anomalous plasma and toplet bombs in Near Earth orbit are cleared.
*   **2018-10-13**: All remaining plasma toplet bombs are located within 30 meters of the planetary surface, connected to plasma implants via the "Tunnels of Set."
*   **2019-07-01**: A very dangerous layer of plasma anomaly containing toplet bombs is successfully removed on June 21st, greatly reducing danger.
*   **2020-01-01**: It is revealed that the remaining toplet bombs are in the quantum field, tied to implants within humans, and are producing rotating black holes.
*   **2020-07-07**: As a result of the Age of Aquarius activation, all significant plasma anomaly and all plasma toplet bombs are removed.
*   **2020-08-12**: Light Forces seize Draco technology to shield against toplet explosions, using it to isolate and contain remaining quantum toplet bombs.
*   **2021-10-02**: Clearing of the subquantum anomaly begins, isolating the effective radius of topness production into "collapsed spacetime bubbles."
*   **2022-04-16**: Light Forces begin the final clearing by deactivating bombs through the clearing of the quantum anomaly, stopping the chain reaction of matter mutation.
*   **2022-11-02**: Events of October 15th jolt Light Forces out of their "toplet fear spell," allowing them to accept reasonable risks and take more action.
*   **2023-05-13**: Following the Portal of Light activation, the Light Forces destroy all remaining toplet bombs.
*   **2023-07-24**: It is confirmed that all problems with toplet bombs were resolved in May 2023, securing the positive timeline.

[Key claims & declarations]
*   Toplet bombs are made of top quarks, making them more massive and dangerous than strangelet bombs (made of strange quarks).
*   The plasma toplet bombs were the single most critical factor preventing the Event; when they are gone, the Event will be triggered.
*   The bombs were protected by quantum potential chambers and tied to the "Black Stone" in Long Island, which acted as a trigger for a vacuum metastability event.
*   Direct military intervention by Light Forces was prevented because it risked detonating the bombs, which would cause a global planetary cataclysm that no one would survive.
*   The Chimera group used the threat of detonation to control information flow and prevent contact between the surface population and extraterrestrial beings of Light.
*   Group meditation (specifically visualizing the removal of the bombs) creates a multidimensional bridge that assists Light Forces in dismantling the plasma network.
*   The removal of toplet bombs is synchronized with the Galactic Super-wave; the Galaxy is described as a living being.
*   Knowledge of heavy quark reactions leading to toplet bomb creation is beginning to reach mainstream science.
*   Mass arrests of the Cabal will not occur until the plasma plane is cleared of all scalar weapons and toplet bombs.
*   Toplet bombs are the main reason for the impasse between Light and dark forces, ensuring Mutually Assured Destruction (MAD).
*   If all toplet bombs were removed, the Event would happen in 15 minutes.
*   A toplet bomb chain reaction has the potential to wipe out the planet or even the solar system by mutating all matter into heavy matter made of top and bottom quarks.
*   Top quarks can eat ordinary matter more efficiently than strange quarks and are far more dangerous.
*   The final location of the bombs was deep within human implants, where a black hole singularity existed.
*   The Light Forces used Mjolnir technology to change the properties of the ambient quantum medium so that top quarks could no longer propagate.
*   The destruction of toplet bombs in May 2023 stabilized the positive timeline and made the removal of other obstacles easier and less risky.
*   Scientists have measured the mass of the top quark and found it to be in a meta-stable region, indicating the universe is approaching a false vacuum change.

[Conflicts & ambiguities]
*   **Timeline of Physical Removal:** Some sources state physical bombs were cleared in "late January 2015," while others reference the "IS:IS Portal Activation in December 2014" as the cause for their removal by January 2015.
*   **Status of "Strangelet" vs. "Toplet":** Early documents (2015) treat them as a combined challenge. Later documents (2016-2017) explicitly state strangelet bombs are completely removed, leaving only toplet bombs. However, a 2018 report states "strangelet bombs have been removed but there are still some toplet bombs active," which is consistent but highlights the shift in focus.
*   **Specific Numbers:** When asked for the number of remaining bombs, Cobra states it is "classified information," though he confirms the number is decreasing and no new ones are being created.
*   **Role of CERN:** One source claims CERN is used to *maintain* the bombs, while another notes that Light Forces can now counteract physical manipulation of particles at CERN, implying the Cabal's control there is weakening or broken.
*   **Child's Prediction:** A nine-year-old child claimed the bombs would be disarmed between April and September 2017. Cobra explicitly stated that the child's statements about him and the Resistance were "not correct," creating ambiguity about whether the specific timeline was accurate or just the attribution.
*   **Location Discrepancy:** Early reports (2018) state bombs are in the plasma shield or within 30 meters of the surface, while later reports (2020-2021) state they are exclusively within human implants and the quantum field. This is resolved by the narrative that plasma layers were cleared first, leaving only the quantum/implant-based nodes.
*   **Removal Status:** In 2020, it was stated that plasma toplet bombs were removed, but quantum ones remained. By 2023, all are confirmed removed. There is a brief period in 2020-2021 where the exact percentage remaining is unknown, with Cobra declining to give specific updates in some interviews.
*   **Destructive Scope:** Some sources claim a detonation would destroy the planet, while others (2020-03-01) suggest the damage might be limited to the solar system or halted in high Earth orbit, though the consensus is that it is catastrophic.

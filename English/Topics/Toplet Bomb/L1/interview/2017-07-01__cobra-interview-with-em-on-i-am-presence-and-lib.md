[Relevant content] Cobra explained that the Light Forces must be very careful in dealing with toplet bombs due to a hostage situation on the planet. He stated that if the Light Forces intervened with military force, the bombs might get detonated, causing a global planetary cataclysm that nobody would survive. He confirmed that one faction is impatient to intervene but is being held back because the risk is too big.
[Key quotes] "C: It would actually be much easier if there were not a hostage situation but there is a hostage situation on this planet and that’s why the Light Forces need to be very careful how they deal with the toplet bombs. Otherwise, this situation would be resolved much faster."
"C: Okay. There is a certain possibility that the bombs would get detonated which would be – I would say – a global planetary cataclysm that I don’t think anybody would be able to survive. And the risk is still too great for that."

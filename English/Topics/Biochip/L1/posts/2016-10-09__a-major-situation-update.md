[Relevant content] It was revealed that physical biochips had not been cleared completely; every human being still has at least three biochips that escaped advanced detection and removal technologies. These three biochips serve as physical anchor points for three main plasma implants located in the frontal lobe and above the navel. Frontal lobe biochips are attached to the optical nerve and auditory cortex, sending a constant audio-video stream to the Chimera mainframe to monitor activity and prevent contact with Light forces. Above-navel biochips control behavior via electrical current biofeedback loop entrainment. Biochip activity increases during plasma scalar attacks. Some Agarthan and SSP groups are infected, while races that mastered physical-etheric body transfer (Resistance, Pleiadians, Sirians, Arcturians, positive Andromedans) are free of them. Developed during WW2 in Nazi concentration camps by the Chimera group, with Siemens providing technological know-how, they were spread via WHO vaccines after WW2. Upgrades occurred between 1979-1984, 1996-1999, and late 2009 (newer, more powerful versions in vaccines since 2009). On September 1st, 2016, an undetected aspect of the biochips was simultaneously activated by the Chimera among the whole surface population and most SSP factions.

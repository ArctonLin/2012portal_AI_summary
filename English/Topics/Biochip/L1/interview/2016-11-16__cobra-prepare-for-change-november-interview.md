[Relevant content] [reported] Cobra stated that the increase in Asperger's Syndrome is due to Archons increasing the use of negative technology such as biochips, implants, and scalar technologies, which created trauma in higher energy bodies. In response to a question about the biochip update, Cobra confirmed that the Chimera has access to audio/video streams from biochips, knowing the developments of the positive military, strategies of the Eastern Alliance, and identities of the awakened surface population, but they cannot act because higher Light Forces stop them.
[Key quotes] "This has increased simply because the Archons have increased the use of their negative technology such as biochips, such as implants, scalar technologies and all of these have created the trauma in higher energy bodies and Asperger’s syndrome is just the outer manifestation of that trauma."
"According to your biochip update it is assumed that the biochips can send out audio/video stream to Chimera’s computer... The Chimera does have all that but they cannot act because the Light Forces of a higher nature are stopping them in their actions."

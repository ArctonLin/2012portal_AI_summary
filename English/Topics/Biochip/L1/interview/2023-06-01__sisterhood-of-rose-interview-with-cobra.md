[Relevant content] [reported] Cobra states that physical biochips are the physical anchor point for the three main plasma implants. As implants dissolve, the main problem is the physical biochips themselves, which disconnect neuron connections in the brain, acting as a blockage. Biochips can read human thoughts to a certain degree if the person is not trained in clouding their thoughts. Other races (Resistance, Pleiadians, etc.) became free of biochips by mastering physical etheric body transfer, a teleportation technology that can remodel the physical body on the etheric plane to clear biochips, but this technology is not yet working on the surface due to subquantum anomaly. Most of humanity received biochips from vaccination campaigns (late 1940s to early 2000s) and soft drinks. A tiny percentage of people, mostly in Africa and off the grid, do not have biochips. The most powerful Lightworkers received stronger biochips. Biochips cannot be passed from person to person. The "Mark of the Beast" plan involving a new biochip technology tied to digital currency has been completely stopped and the technology destroyed by the Light Forces/Resistance.
[Key quotes] "Actually the biochips were working in sync with the implants. And as the implants are dissolving now, the main problem are the physical biochips themselves and what they do; they disconnect the connections, the neuron connections, in the brain."
"To a certain degree, yes, they can. If you are not trained in clouding your thoughts, I would say, with most people, they can, to a certain degree, read human thoughts."
"Okay. This plan has been completely stopped. This technology was destroyed by the Light Forces. The Resistance Movement has completely destroyed this new biochip technology. So it's not existing anymore."

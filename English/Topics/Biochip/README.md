# Topic summary: Biochip

[Overview]
In the 2012portal/Cobra disclosure framework, biochips are described as physical, micro-scale surveillance and control devices implanted in the vast majority of the human population. Originally developed by the Chimera group (an Orion subfaction) with technological assistance from Siemens, they were mass-distributed via WHO vaccination programs starting in the late 1940s. These chips serve as physical anchor points for plasma implants and act as primary tools for the Chimera to monitor, control, and suppress the vibrational frequency of surface humanity. While the Resistance Movement and Light Forces have successfully deactivated and cleared the active functions of these chips, physical remnants remain in the body, acting as weak points for Lurker interference and subquantum anomalies until their final removal.

[Key entities & relationships]
*   **Chimera Group:** The primary orchestrators of the biochip program, using them for surveillance and mind control; their mainframe computer in the DARPA pit controlled the network.
*   **Siemens:** The German industrial company credited with developing the technological know-how and mass-producing the biochips during and after WWII.
*   **WHO (World Health Organization):** The agency responsible for distributing biochips to the global population through mandatory vaccination campaigns starting in the late 1940s.
*   **Resistance Movement:** The group that developed distance removal technologies to erase active biochips and destroyed production facilities and stockpiles.
*   **DARPA:** A U.S. agency used by the Chimera to develop newer, cruder, or upgraded versions of biochips, particularly in the context of the 2020 pandemic.
*   **Lurker:** A negative entity that uses the physical remnants of biochips as portals to manifest subquantum anomalies and influence human subconsciousness.
*   **Orion Empire / Draconian Races:** The cosmic origin of the advanced biochip technology, later adapted by Draconian entities.

[Timeline of developments]
*   **WWI:** Initial, unsuccessful experiments with biochips in prisoner camps (source year 2023).
*   **WWII:** Mass development and refinement of biochips in Nazi concentration camps by Siemens (source year 2015, 2016, 2023).
*   **1945–1940s:** Mass production begins; distribution starts via WHO vaccination programs immediately after WWII (source year 2017, 2023).
*   **1950s–1960s:** The biochip program becomes extensive globally (source year 2017).
*   **1979–1984:** Major technology upgrades to existing biochips (source year 2015).
*   **1983:** Release of newer versions of biochips (source year 2016).
*   **1996–1999:** A period of fast development and strong version deployment, coinciding with Archon invasions and Draco/Reptilian technology contributions (source year 2015, 2016).
*   **Late 2009:** Deployment of newer, more powerful versions in vaccines (source year 2016).
*   **2010 (approx.):** Physical biochips successfully erased from the human population using Resistance distance removal technology (source year 2012).
*   **2015:** Resistance Movement destroys biochip production facilities and shorts remaining chips to stop electrical signals (source year 2025).
*   **September 1, 2016:** Undetected aspects of biochips simultaneously activated by the Chimera (source year 2016).
*   **Spring 2020:** Resistance destroys all advanced biochips intended for the coronavirus vaccine (source year 2020).
*   **2020–2021:** DARPA develops cruder versions of vaccine biochips; "Mark of the Beast" digital currency biochip plan is stopped and destroyed by Light Forces (source year 2020, 2021, 2023).
*   **2022:** Light Forces begin clearing biochips with full force using Mjolnir quantum cannon technologies (source year 2022).
*   **2023:** Removal of the last DARPA pit/mainframe; biochips lose ability to send audio/video signals; network collapses (source year 2023).
*   **February 8, 2024:** Orsini family activates a dormant backup processor in biochips to send data to a facility in Guinea/Sierra Leone; Light Forces plan to deactivate it (source year 2024).
*   **2025:** 98–99% of biochips reported as inactive; physical remnants remain as weak spots for Lurker interference (source year 2025).

[Key claims & declarations]
*   Biochips are located in three main areas: the frontal lobe (behind the eyes, attached to the optical nerve and auditory cortex) and above the navel (source year 2016, 2023).
*   Frontal lobe biochips send constant audio-video streams to the Chimera mainframe and disconnect neuron connections, causing brain fog and sleepiness (source year 2016, 2023).
*   Navel biochips control behavior via electrical current biofeedback, capable of inducing electroshocks between 20 and 130 volts to lower vibrational frequency (source year 2023).
*   Biochips synthesize mind-altering chemicals, producing sleepiness (frontal lobe) and fear (navel) (source year 2023).
*   The chips are approximately 30–50 micrometers in size, consisting of a metallic core and synthetic bio-matter outer layer (source year 2021, 2025).
*   Biochips act as physical anchor points for plasma implants and serve as seals covering layers of programming (source year 2016, 2018).
*   The "Mark of the Beast" plan involving new biochips tied to digital currency was completely stopped and the technology destroyed by the Light Forces (source year 2023).
*   Physical biochips are subquantum/quantum portals through which the Lurker entity emerges into quantum fluctuations (source year 2023).
*   Biochips interfere with nature experiences and Pleiadian contact if individuals are within 60 meters of each other (source year 2022, 2025).
*   The clearing of biochips is a prerequisite for First Contact and the Event (source year 2023).

[Conflicts & ambiguities]
*   **Removal Status:** Early posts (2012) claim physical biochips were "completely erased" circa 2010, while later posts (2016–2025) state that at least three biochips per person remain as physical anchor points or inactive residues.
*   **Development Origin:** Some sources cite development starting in WWI, others in WWII, and others attribute the core technology to the Orion Empire/Draconian races rather than solely human/Siemens development.
*   **Vaccine Content:** While the Resistance claims to have destroyed biochips for the 2020 vaccine, sources note that DARPA is still developing "cruder" versions, creating ambiguity about the current state of vaccine safety regarding chips.
*   **Active vs. Inactive:** There is a distinction between "erased" (functional removal) and "cleared" (deactivation), with 2025 sources confirming 98–99% are inactive but physically present, whereas 2012 sources suggested total removal.

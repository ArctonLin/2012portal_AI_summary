[Relevant content] In Operation Omega Phoenix, Resistance operatives will monitor the mass arrest process and help the Positive Military and civil authority in arrests. Members of the Cabal arrested by Resistance operatives will be taken to undisclosed locations and later released to Positive Military/civil authority. A few Cabal members who committed crimes beyond Earth in their past must appear before the Galactic Court; a fraction will be taken to the Galactic Central Sun immediately.

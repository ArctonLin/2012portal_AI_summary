[Relevant content] A non-interference treaty was signed between the Light Forces and the Cabal, wherein the Cabal receives security intel to protect its main people from random surface attacks, and in return promises not to persecute Lightworkers. The Cabal violated this treaty by persecuting Lightworkers in a specific country, leading the Light Forces to close the deconfliction communication line, which resulted in security breaches for the Cabal (the destruction of the Georgia guidestones and the attack on Shinzo Abe).
[Key quotes] "One part of that treaty states that the Cabal will receive security intel from the Light Forces to protect Cabal's main people from random attacks from the surface population... and in turn the Cabal promised not to persecute the Lightworkers directly because of their beliefs and activities."
"As a result of that violation, the Light forces have closed the deconfliction communication line for a few days in the beginning of July as a warning. For these few days, the Cabal did not receive security intel to protect their main people and locations, which resulted in two security breaches for them."

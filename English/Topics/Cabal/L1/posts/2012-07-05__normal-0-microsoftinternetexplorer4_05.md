[Relevant content] White Knights are a soul group fighting against the Cabal, currently mostly against Rockefellers and Rothschilds. After being cut off from nuclear weapons, the Cabal began frantically developing new biological and chemical weapons. The Rothschild faction threatened to use the "Flipper virus" to decimate the Earth's population if mass arrests took place on July 4th. Top Cabal members have created protective defense mechanisms, including biochemical weapons and devices monitoring their bodily functions that trigger advanced weapons systems if Stardust 2 is used or key members are arrested.
[Key quotes] "After the Cabal was lately cut off from nuclear weapons, they began frantically developing new biological and chemical weapons."
"The Rothschild faction threatened to use one of these weapons (the so-called Flipper virus) and decimate the Earth’s population if the mass arrests would take place on July 4th."
"Those mechanisms would be triggered automatically if Stardust 2 was to be used or if any of the key members of the Cabal would be arrested."

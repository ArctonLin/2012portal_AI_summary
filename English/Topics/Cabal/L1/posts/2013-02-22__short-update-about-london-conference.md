[Relevant content] A quote from Benjamin Fulford describes a split between the UK and the "cabal that controls the US and Europe." The UK's move to trade currency for Chinese Renminbi is described as a major blow to the cabal behind the Federal Reserve Board and the European central bank.
[Key quotes] "In any case last week there was a major, and under-reported, split between the UK and the cabal that controls the US and Europe."
"The British withdrawal from the US dollar/Euro group and move to direct forex dealing with China is a major blow to the cabal behind the Federal Reserve Board and the European central bank."

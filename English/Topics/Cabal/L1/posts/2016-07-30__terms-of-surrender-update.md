- The mass arrest plan remains valid; Light forces will not wait for Cabal factions to surrender.
- Cabal members who surrender before mass arrests will have more favorable conditions.

- A bounty for capturing Cabal members is unlikely to work due to a centuries-old treaty with the Chimera.

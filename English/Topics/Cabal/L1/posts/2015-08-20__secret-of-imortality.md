[Relevant content] 
- The Archons and the rest of the Cabal have a fragmentary understanding of the process of physical immortality, which they stole from Memphis-Misraim masonic lodges.
- The Cabal possesses a war mentality that prevents them from achieving physical immortality, which requires reducing entropy and overpowering evil with goodness.
- The Cross of Lorraine symbolizes that in every Archon or Black nobility family, there are beings of Light who work against the plans of the Cabal.
[Key quotes] 
- "The Archons and the rest of the Cabal never understood this process in its entirety, but are searching for immortality nevertheless."
- "Physical immortality is the ultimate manifestation of the Goddess presence within the physical body and this can never be achieved through the war mentality the Cabal possesses."
- "One message of the Cross of Lorraine is that in every Archon or Black nobility family there are beings of Light incarnated who secretly or openly work for the Light and against the plans of the Cabal."

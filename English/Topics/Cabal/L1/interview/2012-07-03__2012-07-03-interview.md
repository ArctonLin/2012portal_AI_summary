[Relevant content] [reported] Certain factions inside the Cabal got very nervous after Drake announced his Green Light, making mistakes and trying to discredit messengers. The Rockefeller faction of the Cabal wanted to control Iraq due to stargate properties. The Cabal is described as extraterrestrials who invaded the planet many lifetimes ago and keep reincarnating in positions of power. The Cabal has three factions: Rothschilds (Orion forces, controlling Europe), Rockefellers (Draconians, controlling the US), and Jesuits (mixture of Andromedans and Reptilians, controlling the rest of the world). The Cabal had cloning technology which was cleared by the Resistance Movement 5 years prior; they cannot clone anyone now. The Cabal was much stronger between 1996 and 1999, with 10-20 million representatives in underground bases. The Cabal still controls some uppermost levels of military bases but has no personnel or advanced weapons there. The Cabal spends over a billion dollars to run things. A successful cyber-attack operation stole money from Cabal accounts and banks. The Cabal is listening to communications, so the ascension plan is classified.

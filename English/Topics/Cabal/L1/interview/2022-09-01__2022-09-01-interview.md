[Relevant content] [reported] Cobra stated that the dark forces cannot create copies of key Cabal members; they can only create fake clones that look like the beings. Restructuring the souls of key Cabal members in the Galactic Central Sun removes them forever from the universe.
[Key quotes] "The dark forces cannot make copies of those beings. They can just create a fake person clone that looks like that particular being, but it's not the same. So, restructuring those souls removes them forever from this universe, it's gone."

[Relevant content] 
- [reported] COBRA stated that some high-level members of the Cabal, specifically the "Unholy 4," still have communication with the Chimera group.
- [reported] COBRA described the Ukraine situation as the last stage of galactic wars, with the Eastern Alliance/BRICS fighting the Western Cabal.
- [reported] COBRA stated that the Cabal is trying to misuse the Ukraine peace agreement to create a pretext for more conflict.
- [reported] COBRA noted that high-end noble families (Archons) have lost power rapidly, marking the fastest decline in human history.
- [reported] COBRA stated that the Cabal's control over the financial system is the main reason for the delay in the Event.
- [reported] COBRA stated that the Cabal's plan to escape the planet at the time of mass arrests has failed because they have nowhere to escape to.
- [reported] COBRA stated that the Cabal is manipulating stock prices to bankrupt investors who do not work with them.
- [reported] COBRA stated that the Cabal is not responsible for the Nepal earthquake.
